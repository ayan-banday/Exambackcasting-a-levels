[9]Question 1 — Differentiation
A curve has equation \(y=\dfrac{x^2}{x-1}\), \(x\neq1\).
(a) Find \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}\) and the coordinates and nature of the stationary points. [6]
(b) Write down the equations of the two asymptotes. [3]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{2x(x-1)-x^2}{(x-1)^2}=\dfrac{x(x-2)}{(x-1)^2}\). \(=0\Rightarrow x=0,2\). \((0,0)\) maximum, \((2,4)\) minimum (second-derivative or sign test). M1A1A1A1A1A1.

(b) Vertical \(x=1\); oblique: \(y=x+1+\dfrac{1}{x-1}\to y=x+1\). A1A1A1.

[8]Question 2 — Integration
(a) Find \(\displaystyle\int \dfrac{x^2}{x-1}\,dx\) (write the integrand as \(x+1+\tfrac{1}{x-1}\) first). [3]
(b) Find \(\displaystyle\int x\ln x\,dx\) by parts. [3]
(c) Evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\sin^2 x\,dx\). [2]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\dfrac{x^2}{2}+x+\ln|x-1|+C\). A1A1A1.

(b) \(u=\ln x,\ dv=x\,dx\): \(\dfrac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x}{2}dx=\dfrac{x^2}{2}\ln x-\dfrac{x^2}{4}+C\). M1A1A1.

(c) \(\sin^2x=\tfrac12(1-\cos2x)\Rightarrow\big[\tfrac{x}{2}-\tfrac{\sin2x}{4}\big]_0^{\pi/2}=\dfrac{\pi}{4}\). M1A1.

[8]Question 3 — Differential equations
A tank drains so that the depth \(h\) of water satisfies \(\dfrac{dh}{dt}=-k\sqrt{h}\), where \(k>0\). Initially \(h=H\).
(a) Solve the differential equation, expressing \(h\) in terms of \(t\). [5]
(b) Show that the tank empties after a finite time and find it in terms of \(H\) and \(k\). [3]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\displaystyle\int h^{-1/2}dh=\int -k\,dt\Rightarrow 2\sqrt h=-kt+c\). \(t=0,h=H\Rightarrow c=2\sqrt H\). \(\sqrt h=\sqrt H-\tfrac{kt}{2}\Rightarrow h=\big(\sqrt H-\tfrac{kt}{2}\big)^2\). M1A1M1A1A1.

(b) Empty when \(h=0\Rightarrow \sqrt H=\tfrac{kt}{2}\Rightarrow t=\dfrac{2\sqrt H}{k}\) (finite). M1A1A1.

[6]Question 4 — Maclaurin series
(a) Find the Maclaurin expansion of \(\ln(1+2x)\) up to and including the term in \(x^3\). [3]
(b) Hence, or otherwise, find the expansion of \(\dfrac{\ln(1+2x)}{1-x}\) up to the term in \(x^2\). [3]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\ln(1+2x)=2x-\dfrac{(2x)^2}{2}+\dfrac{(2x)^3}{3}-\cdots=2x-2x^2+\dfrac{8}{3}x^3-\cdots\). M1A1A1.

(b) \((2x-2x^2+\cdots)(1+x+x^2+\cdots)=2x+(2-2)x^2+\cdots=2x+0\cdot x^2+\cdots=2x-0x^2\). Recompute: \(2x(1+x)=2x+2x^2\); \(-2x^2(1)=-2x^2\) → \(2x+0x^2\). So \(\approx 2x\) (the \(x^2\) term is 0). M1A1A1.

[9]Question 5 — Vectors (scalar product)
Relative to \(O\), \(\mathbf a=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\-1\end{pmatrix}\), \(\mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}0\\3\\1\end{pmatrix}\), \(\mathbf c=\begin{pmatrix}4\\-1\\k\end{pmatrix}\).
(a) Find the acute angle \(AOB\). [3]
(b) \(P\) divides \(AB\) in ratio \(1:2\). Find \(\vec{OP}\). [2]
(c) Find \(k\) so that \(A,B,C\) are collinear. [4]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=2\), \(|\mathbf a|=\sqrt6,|\mathbf b|=\sqrt{10}\), \(\cos\theta=\tfrac{2}{\sqrt{60}}\Rightarrow 75.0^\circ\). M1A1A1.

(b) \(\vec{OP}=\tfrac{2\mathbf a+\mathbf b}{3}=\tfrac13\begin{pmatrix}4\\5\\-1\end{pmatrix}\). M1A1.

(c) \(\vec{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}-2\\2\\2\end{pmatrix},\vec{AC}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\-2\\k+1\end{pmatrix}\). Parallel: \(\lambda=-1\) from x, y checks, z: \(k+1=-2\Rightarrow k=-3\). M1M1A1A1.

[9]Question 6 — Vectors (line + perpendicular)
Points \(A(1,0,2)\) and \(B(3,2,4)\). Point \(C\) has position vector \(\begin{pmatrix}2\\5\\1\end{pmatrix}\).
(a) Find a vector equation of the line \(l\) through \(A\) and \(B\). [2]
(b) Find the position vector of the foot of perpendicular \(F\) from \(C\) to \(l\), and hence the shortest distance from \(C\) to \(l\). [5]
(c) Find the area of triangle \(ABC\). [2]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\vec{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\2\end{pmatrix}\|\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\). \(l:\ \mathbf r=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\2\end{pmatrix}+\lambda\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\). M1A1.

(b) \(F=(1+\lambda,\lambda,2+\lambda)\), \(\vec{CF}=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda-1\\\lambda-5\\\lambda+1\end{pmatrix}\); \(\vec{CF}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}=0\Rightarrow3\lambda-5=0,\lambda=\tfrac53\). \(F=(\tfrac83,\tfrac53,\tfrac{11}{3})\). Distance \(=|\vec{CF}|\) with \(\lambda=\tfrac53\): \(\vec{CF}=\tfrac13\begin{pmatrix}2\\-10\\8\end{pmatrix}\), \(|\vec{CF}|=\tfrac13\sqrt{4+100+64}=\tfrac13\sqrt{168}=\tfrac{2\sqrt{42}}{3}\). M1M1A1A1A1.

(c) Area \(=\tfrac12|\vec{AB}\times\vec{AC}|\). \(\vec{AC}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\5\\-1\end{pmatrix}\), \(\vec{AB}\times\vec{AC}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\2\end{pmatrix}\times\begin{pmatrix}1\\5\\-1\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}-12\\4\\8\end{pmatrix}\), \(\tfrac12\sqrt{144+16+64}=\tfrac12\sqrt{224}=2\sqrt{14}\). M1A1.
