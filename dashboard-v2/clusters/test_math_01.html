[11]Question 1
The diagram shows the curve \(C\) with equation \(y = x\sqrt{4-x^2}\) for \(0 \le x \le 2\).
(a) Find the exact coordinates of the maximum point of \(C\). [4]
(b) Find the exact area of the region bounded by \(C\) and the \(x\)-axis. [3]
(c) This region is rotated through \(2\pi\) radians about the \(x\)-axis. Find the exact volume generated. [4]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\sqrt{4-x^2}+x\cdot\dfrac{-x}{\sqrt{4-x^2}}=\dfrac{4-2x^2}{\sqrt{4-x^2}}=0\Rightarrow x^2=2,\ x=\sqrt2\). \(y=\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2=2\). Max \(\left(\sqrt2,\,2\right)\). M1A1A1A1.

(b) \(A=\displaystyle\int_0^2 x\sqrt{4-x^2}\,dx=\Big[-\tfrac13(4-x^2)^{3/2}\Big]_0^2=0-(-\tfrac13\cdot8)=\dfrac{8}{3}\). M1M1A1.

(c) \(V=\pi\displaystyle\int_0^2 x^2(4-x^2)\,dx=\pi\Big[\tfrac{4x^3}{3}-\tfrac{x^5}{5}\Big]_0^2=\pi\big(\tfrac{32}{3}-\tfrac{32}{5}\big)=\dfrac{64\pi}{15}\). M1M1M1A1.

[8]Question 2
(a) Find \(\displaystyle\int \frac{(\ln x)^2}{x^2}\,dx\). [5]
(b) Hence evaluate \(\displaystyle\int_1^{e} \frac{(\ln x)^2}{x^2}\,dx\), giving your answer in terms of \(e\). [3]
Reveal solution

(a) By parts \((u=(\ln x)^2,\ dv=x^{-2}dx,\ v=-x^{-1})\): \(-\dfrac{(\ln x)^2}{x}+2\displaystyle\int\dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}dx\). Second integral by parts: \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{\ln x}{x^2}dx=-\dfrac{\ln x}{x}-\dfrac1x\). So \(=-\dfrac{(\ln x)^2}{x}-\dfrac{2\ln x}{x}-\dfrac{2}{x}+C\). M1M1A1M1A1.

(b) At \(e\): \(-\tfrac1e-\tfrac2e-\tfrac2e=-\tfrac5e\). At \(1\): \(0-0-2=-2\). Value \(=-\tfrac5e-(-2)=2-\dfrac{5}{e}\). M1M1A1.

[12]Question 3
In a model of a spreading rumour, the number \(x\) (in thousands) of people who have heard it after \(t\) days satisfies \(\dfrac{dx}{dt}=kx(10-x)\), where \(k>0\). Initially one thousand people have heard it, and after one day two thousand have.
(a) Show that \(t = \dfrac{1}{10k}\ln\!\left(\dfrac{9x}{10-x}\right)\). [6]
(b) Find the exact value of \(k\). [2]
(c) State the number of people who eventually hear the rumour, and find the time taken for nine thousand to have heard it. [4]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\displaystyle\int\dfrac{dx}{x(10-x)}=\int k\,dt\). Partial fractions \(\dfrac{1}{x(10-x)}=\dfrac{1}{10}\!\left(\dfrac1x+\dfrac1{10-x}\right)\Rightarrow \dfrac{1}{10}\ln\!\dfrac{x}{10-x}=kt+c\). At \(t=0,x=1:\ c=\dfrac1{10}\ln\dfrac19\). So \(kt=\dfrac1{10}\ln\dfrac{x}{10-x}-\dfrac1{10}\ln\dfrac19=\dfrac1{10}\ln\dfrac{9x}{10-x}\Rightarrow t=\dfrac{1}{10k}\ln\dfrac{9x}{10-x}\). M1M1A1M1M1A1.

(b) \(t=1,x=2:\ 1=\dfrac1{10k}\ln\dfrac{18}{8}=\dfrac1{10k}\ln\dfrac94\Rightarrow k=\dfrac{1}{10}\ln\dfrac94\). M1A1.

(c) As \(t\to\infty,\ x\to10\Rightarrow\) 10 000 people. For \(x=9:\ t=\dfrac{1}{10k}\ln\dfrac{81}{1}=\dfrac{\ln81}{10k}=\dfrac{\ln81}{\ln(9/4)}\approx5.42\) days. M1A1M1A1.

[9]Question 4
A curve has equation \(y=\ln(1+\sin x)\).
(a) Show that \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}=-\dfrac{1}{1+\sin x}\), and hence find the Maclaurin series for \(y\) up to and including the term in \(x^3\). [6]
(b) Hence find \(\displaystyle\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\ln(1+\sin x)-x}{x^2}\). [3]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\dfrac{dy}{dx}=\dfrac{\cos x}{1+\sin x}\). \(\dfrac{d^2y}{dx^2}=\dfrac{-\sin x(1+\sin x)-\cos^2x}{(1+\sin x)^2}=\dfrac{-\sin x-1}{(1+\sin x)^2}=-\dfrac{1}{1+\sin x}\). Values at 0: \(y=0,\ y'=1,\ y''=-1,\ y'''=\dfrac{\cos x}{(1+\sin x)^2}\big|_0=1\). Series: \(y=x-\dfrac{x^2}{2}+\dfrac{x^3}{6}-\cdots\). M1A1M1A1A1A1.

(b) \(\dfrac{(x-\tfrac{x^2}{2}+\cdots)-x}{x^2}=\dfrac{-\tfrac{x^2}{2}+\cdots}{x^2}\to-\dfrac12\). M1M1A1.

[10]Question 5
Points \(A\), \(B\), \(C\) have position vectors \(\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\\2\end{pmatrix}\), \(\begin{pmatrix}3\\2\\4\end{pmatrix}\), \(\begin{pmatrix}4\\3\\5\end{pmatrix}\). The vector \(\mathbf d=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix}\).
(a) Show that \(A\), \(B\) and \(C\) are collinear, and state the ratio \(AB:BC\). [3]
(b) Find a unit vector perpendicular to both \(\vec{AB}\) and \(\mathbf d\). [3]
(c) Find the value of \(t\) for which the vector \(\begin{pmatrix}t\\2\\-1\end{pmatrix}\) is perpendicular to \(\vec{AB}\). [2]
(d) \(M\) is the midpoint of \(AB\). Find \(|\vec{OM}|\) and determine whether \(\vec{OM}\) is perpendicular to \(\vec{AB}\). [2]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\vec{AB}=\begin{pmatrix}2\\2\\2\end{pmatrix}\), \(\vec{AC}=\begin{pmatrix}3\\3\\3\end{pmatrix}=\tfrac32\vec{AB}\) → parallel and share \(A\) → collinear. \(\vec{BC}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\Rightarrow\vec{AB}=2\vec{BC}\), so \(AB:BC=2:1\). M1A1A1.

(b) \(\vec{AB}\parallel\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\); \(\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}\times\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\\0\end{pmatrix}=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\-2\end{pmatrix}\), \(|\cdot|=\sqrt6\). Unit vector \(=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt6}\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\-2\end{pmatrix}\) (or its negative). M1A1A1.

(c) \(\begin{pmatrix}t\\2\\-1\end{pmatrix}\cdot\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}=t+2-1=0\Rightarrow t=-1\). M1A1.

(d) \(\vec{OM}=\tfrac12(\mathbf a+\mathbf b)=\begin{pmatrix}2\\1\\3\end{pmatrix}\), \(|\vec{OM}|=\sqrt{4+1+9}=\sqrt{14}\). \(\vec{OM}\cdot\vec{AB}=2+1+3=6\neq0\) → not perpendicular. M1A1.

[10]Question 6
Relative to the origin \(O\), points \(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \(\mathbf a=\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\\2\end{pmatrix}\) and \(\mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}4\\0\\3\end{pmatrix}\).
(a) Find the acute angle \(AOB\). [2]
(b) The point \(M\) lies on \(AB\) with \(AM:MB = 2:1\). Find the position vector of \(M\). [2]
(c) Find the exact length of the projection of \(\vec{OB}\) onto \(\vec{OA}\). [2]
(d) Find the exact area of triangle \(OAB\). [4]
Reveal solution

(a) \(\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b=4+0+6=10\), \(|\mathbf a|=3,|\mathbf b|=5\), \(\cos\theta=\tfrac{10}{15}=\tfrac23\Rightarrow\theta=48.2^\circ\). M1A1.

(b) Ratio theorem \(\vec{OM}=\dfrac{1\cdot\mathbf a+2\cdot\mathbf b}{3}=\dfrac{\mathbf a+2\mathbf b}{3}=\begin{pmatrix}3\\\tfrac23\\\tfrac83\end{pmatrix}\). M1A1.

(c) Scalar projection \(=\dfrac{\mathbf a\cdot\mathbf b}{|\mathbf a|}=\dfrac{10}{3}\). M1A1.

(d) \(\mathbf a\times\mathbf b=\begin{pmatrix}6\\5\\-8\end{pmatrix}\), \(|\mathbf a\times\mathbf b|=\sqrt{125}=5\sqrt5\); area \(=\tfrac12\cdot5\sqrt5=\dfrac{5\sqrt5}{2}\). M1M1A1A1.

[8]Question 7
Water is poured into an inverted right circular cone of semi-vertical angle \(30^\circ\) at a constant rate of \(8\ \text{cm}^3\,\text{s}^{-1}\).
At the instant when the depth of water is \(12\ \text{cm}\):
(a) find the rate at which the depth of water is increasing; [5]
(b) find the rate at which the area of the wetted curved (slanted) surface is increasing. [3]
Reveal solution

(a) \(r=h\tan30^\circ=\dfrac{h}{\sqrt3}\). \(V=\tfrac13\pi r^2h=\dfrac{\pi h^3}{9}\Rightarrow\dfrac{dV}{dh}=\dfrac{\pi h^2}{3}\). \(\dfrac{dV}{dt}=\dfrac{dV}{dh}\cdot\dfrac{dh}{dt}\Rightarrow 8=\dfrac{\pi(144)}{3}\dfrac{dh}{dt}=48\pi\dfrac{dh}{dt}\Rightarrow\dfrac{dh}{dt}=\dfrac{1}{6\pi}\ \text{cm s}^{-1}\ (\approx0.0531)\). M1M1M1M1A1.

(b) Slant \(l=\dfrac{2h}{\sqrt3}\); \(A=\pi r l=\dfrac{2\pi h^2}{3}\Rightarrow\dfrac{dA}{dh}=\dfrac{4\pi h}{3}=16\pi\) at \(h=12\). \(\dfrac{dA}{dt}=16\pi\cdot\dfrac{1}{6\pi}=\dfrac{8}{3}\ \text{cm}^2\text{s}^{-1}\). M1A1A1.
