Integration — Supplementary (Techniques) Q27 · HCI/2014/C2/BT2/8

(a) \(x = a + \tan\theta \Rightarrow \dfrac{\mathrm dx}{\mathrm d\theta} = \sec^2\theta\). (1)

\(\displaystyle\int \sqrt{1+(x-a)^2}\,\mathrm dx = \int \sqrt{1+\tan^2\theta}\,\sec^2\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = \int \sec\theta\cdot\sec^2\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = \int \sec^3\theta\,\mathrm d\theta\)  (shown). (1)  [uses \(1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta\) and \(\sqrt{\sec^2\theta}=\sec\theta\)]

(b) Change limits: \(x=a\Rightarrow\tan\theta=0\Rightarrow\theta=0\); \(\ x=2a\Rightarrow\tan\theta=a\Rightarrow\theta=\tan^{-1}a\). (1)

\(\displaystyle\int_{a}^{2a}\!\sqrt{1+(x-a)^2}\,\mathrm dx = \int_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\!\sec\theta\cdot\sec^2\theta\,\mathrm d\theta\). By parts, \(u=\sec\theta\Rightarrow u'=\sec\theta\tan\theta;\ \ v'=\sec^2\theta\Rightarrow v=\tan\theta\): (1)

\(=\big[\sec\theta\tan\theta\big]_0^{\tan^{-1}a} - \displaystyle\int_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\!\sec\theta\tan^2\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = \big[\sec\theta\tan\theta\big]_0^{\tan^{-1}a} - \int_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\!\sec\theta(\sec^2\theta-1)\,\mathrm d\theta\). (1)

\(\therefore 2\displaystyle\int_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\!\sec^3\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = \big[\sec\theta\tan\theta\big]_0^{\tan^{-1}a} + \int_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\!\sec\theta\,\mathrm d\theta\)  (rearranging). (1)

\(\displaystyle\int_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\!\sec^3\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = \tfrac12\big[\sec\theta\tan\theta\big]_0^{\tan^{-1}a} + \tfrac12\big[\ln|\sec\theta+\tan\theta|\big]_0^{\tan^{-1}a}\). (1)  [\(\int\sec\theta\,\mathrm d\theta = \ln|\sec\theta+\tan\theta|\), MF26]

At \(\theta=\tan^{-1}a\): \(\tan\theta=a\), and \(\sec\theta=\sqrt{1+a^2}\) (right triangle: opposite \(=a\), adjacent \(=1\), hypotenuse \(=\sqrt{1+a^2}\)). At \(\theta=0\): \(\sec\theta\tan\theta=0\), \(\ \ln|1+0|=0\).

\(=\tfrac12\!\left[a\sqrt{1+a^2}\right] + \tfrac12\ln\!\left(a+\sqrt{1+a^2}\right) = \dfrac12\!\left[\,a\sqrt{1+a^2} + \ln\!\left(a+\sqrt{1+a^2}\right)\right]\). (1)
