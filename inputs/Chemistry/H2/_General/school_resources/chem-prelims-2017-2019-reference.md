# H2 Chemistry 9729 Prelim Patterns — 2017 to 2019 (Early Syllabus Era)

**Source:** ~418 JC prelim papers across 19 JCs over 3 years.
**Method:** Deep-read 12 priority papers (RI, HCI, NJC, VJC each year) + supplementary ACJC sampling and breadth scan across remaining JCs.
**Era:** 2017 is the FIRST year of 9729 (replacing 9647). 2017-2019 = early syllabus calibration period.

---

## Era Context — What This Period Means

| Aspect | Implication |
|---|---|
| 2017 = first 9729 cohort | JCs were still calibrating "what counts as a 9729 question." Expect more conservative coverage close to syllabus core; less obscure topic stretching than 2022+. |
| Transition from 9647 | Schools recycled known 9647 patterns into new 9729 paper structure (P2 split from old P3, P3 free response added). Many questions feel "classic" — fundamental dot-and-cross, Born-Haber, ester hydrolysis, Group 2 trends. |
| 9729 paper format introduced | P1 = 30 MCQ (1 h, 15%), P2 = Structured (2 h, 30%, 75 marks), P3 = Free Response with Section A all-compulsory + Section B 1-of-2 (2 h, 35%, 80 marks), P4 = Practical (2.5 h, 20%, 55 marks). This structure was new and JCs were still finding their feet on Section B difficulty calibration. |
| No COVID / no online disruption | Papers run conventionally. Practical P4 fully assumed available in school. |

## Topic Distribution Per Paper (Mode)

### Paper 1 (MCQ × 30, ~1 mark each)

| Topic Cluster | Typical # Qs | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Atomic structure / IE trends | 2-3 | Successive IE graphs (RI 2018 Q1; HCI 2017 Q1; NJC 2017 Q4; NJC 2019 Q4) appear EVERY year. Identify group/period from IE jumps. |
| Bonding & shapes | 3-4 | Bond angle comparisons (HCI 2017 Q4; NJC 2019 Q6); dot-and-cross; hybridisation in unfamiliar molecules (2-furonitrile, propadiene). |
| Gases / ideal gas | 1-2 | Two-bulb mixing (RI 2017 Q5; HCI 2019 Q5; NJC 2019 Q7) — recurring favourite. |
| Chemical energetics | 2-3 | Enthalpy of combustion vs formation (HCI 2017 Q8); Hess cycles; entropy direction. |
| Reaction kinetics | 2-3 | Half-life + order from data (HCI 2017 Q10 — radioactive decay; NJC 2019 Q11). Rate law from mechanism (HCI 2017 Q9). |
| Equilibria + Kp/Kc | 1-2 | Kp partial pressure (NJC 2017 Q9); Le Chatelier predictions (HCI 2017 Q11). |
| Acids/bases/buffers | 2-3 | Indicator choice for weak acid/strong base titrations (HCI 2017 Q13 — methylamine + HCl). |
| Solubility/Ksp | 1 | Comparing Ksp to predict highest [Ag+] (HCI 2017 Q12). |
| Redox/electrochem | 2-3 | E° comparisons; anodising; halogen oxidising power (HCI 2017 Q26). |
| Period 3 chemistry | 1-2 | Identify element from oxide/chloride behaviour (HCI 2017 Q28; RI 2018 Q9). |
| Transition metals | 2-3 | Colour + shape of Cu complexes (HCI 2017 Q29); cation flow chart deduction (HCI 2017 Q30 — usually the closing big Q). |
| Organic — basics | 2-3 | Stereoisomer counting (HCI 2017 Q14 — triglyceride; 2017 Q18 — enantiomeric excess). Free radical Q• count. |
| Organic — reactions | 4-5 | Bromine with arenes (HCI 2017 Q17); Fehling's positive/negative (Q22); KMnO4 oxidation products (Q16); LiAlH4 vs H2 reduction outcomes. |
| Phenols / carboxylic acids | 1-2 | pKa ranking, especially with -NO2 vs -OCH3 substituents. |
| Organic synthesis trap | 1-2 | "Which transformation is NOT correct?" (HCI 2017 Q25); "Which yields deuterium product?" (Q21). |

**MCQ flavour:** mostly 4-distractor single-best-answer. Multi-correct ("1, 2 and 3 only") appears 4-6 times per paper. Numerical MCQs require calculator and ~2 min each — pace ~2 min/Q.

### Paper 2 (Structured, 75 marks)

Typical breakdown: 5-6 questions, 8-19 marks each.

| Question slot | Topic mix | Mark range |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Energetics (Hess, BHC, Kp) OR Period 3 IE/oxides | 8-14 |
| Q2 | Transition metals (complexes, colour, redox) OR halogen chemistry | 10-16 |
| Q3 | Organic synthesis with mechanism + reagent boxes (epoxides, alcohols, Friedel-Crafts) | 11-22 |
| Q4 | Kinetics with data graph + mechanism + Boltzmann | 11-16 |
| Q5 | Buffer/pH/titration calculation OR Ksp/equilibria | 10-18 |
| Q6 | Bonus mixed (data analysis, "magic show" demo Q on HCI 2017) | 5-15 |

**Pattern signatures 2017-2019 P2:**
- ALWAYS a Born-Haber or Hess energy cycle with at least one given hydration/atomisation value (RI 2018 Q4; NJC 2017 Q1; NJC 2018 Q2; VJC 2017 Q2; HCI 2018 Q1).
- ALWAYS at least one mechanism drawn with curly arrows (electrophilic substitution, nucleophilic addition, free radical, SN1/SN2). HCI 2017 had unimolecular nucleophilic substitution on styrene oxide with H2¹⁸O isotopic labelling — recycled by NJC 2018 verbatim.
- ALWAYS one buffer pH calculation including indicator choice OR Henderson-Hasselbalch ratio (VJC 2017 Q1; HCI 2019 Q2; ACJC 2019 Q2).
- ALWAYS one transition metal colour + d-orbital splitting explanation, often tied to ligand substitution change (HCI 2017 Q5 — Ni²⁺ tetrahedral; HCI 2019 Q3 — Co complexes).
- One organic synthesis with multi-step "state reagents and conditions for steps 1-3" + intermediate structures (3-7 marks).
- One "compare acidity / basicity / thermal stability" prose answer for 2-4 marks.

### Paper 3 (Free Response, 80 marks)

Structure: **Section A** = 3 compulsory Qs (60 marks) + **Section B** = 1 of 2 (20 marks).

**Section A typical content:**
- Q1: Energetics or Period 3 elements + 1 organic deduction sub-part (15-20 marks)
- Q2: Halogen / transition metal chemistry + redox calculation (16-23 marks)
- Q3: Organic deduction puzzle (compound X to Y to Z with FG tests) (17-22 marks)

**Section B typical content:**
- Q4: Mixed inorganic + medium organic synthesis (~20 marks)
- Q5: Amino acid / large organic molecule deduction (~20 marks)

**The signature "deduce structures from observations" question:** This is the recurring 8-13 mark monster.

Examples 2017-2019:
- HCI 2017 P3 Q5d: P, Q, R, S, T, U, V deduction with histidine + reactions
- HCI 2018 P3 Q4d: M, N, P, Q deduction with chiral analysis
- HCI 2018 P3 Q5e: U → V (HBr) → W (KMnO4) chain
- HCI 2019 P3 Q5d: B contains C, H, O; reacts 1:4 with KMnO4 producing 4 CO2
- NJC 2017 P3 Q3c: J (Mr 134) and K (Mr 90) deduction
- NJC 2018 P3 Q4 (Section B): full polypeptide chain + glutamic acid titration + reactions
- VJC 2017 P3 Q4c: K, L, M, N deduction (C4H9NO3)
- VJC 2017 P3 Q5c: P, Q, R, S deduction (C12H15O3Cl)
- VJC 2018 P3 Q3c: D, E, F, G, H deduction (β-diester chemistry)
- VJC 2018 P3 Q5b: W, X, Y, Z constitutional isomers from test table
- VJC 2019 P3 Q2b(iii): A, B, C, D, E deduction (Michael addition path)
- RI 2018 P3 Q5d: 12-mark monster — J, K, L, M, N, P, Q deduction (C10H10N2O2 starting material)
- ACJC 2017 P3 Q2c: D, E, F, G, H, J deduction (C16H22O3 ester)

**Examiner pattern:** The Section B Q5 is almost always the deduction puzzle. If you only practice ONE Section B per paper, do the deduction one.

### Paper 4 (Practical, 55 marks)

Typical structure across 2017-2019: 3-4 questions.

| Q type | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Volumetric titration (acid-base, redox, double-indicator) | EVERY paper | Iodometric (Cu²⁺/I⁻ + S₂O₃²⁻) is the most common — HCI 2017, RI 2018. Double-indicator NaOH/Na2CO3 — HCI 2017. NaClO determination — RI 2018. |
| Thermometric / calorimetric (enthalpy from ΔT) | EVERY paper | Plot ΔT vs volume of one reactant; extrapolate to find Vmax, ΔTmax; calculate Hneut or similar. HCI 2019 P4 Q1 = textbook example. |
| Planning question | 1-2 per paper | "Plan an experiment to determine X". May appear standalone or as final sub-part. |
| Qualitative analysis (QA) | EVERY paper | Identify unknown salt(s) by colour + reactions with NaOH(aq), NH3(aq), H2SO4, NaI(aq), etc. QA notes printed at back of paper. |
| Error analysis / improvement | EVERY paper | "Suggest one significant source of error and improvement." Standard 1-2 marks each. |

## MCQ Patterns Worth Banking

1. **Successive IE deduction** (Period 3/4 element identification from graph): RI 2018 Q1, HCI 2017 Q1, NJC 2017 Q4, NJC 2019 Q4. Setup: graph shows successive IEs (log scale common), candidate identifies group + element.

2. **Two-bulb gas mixing**: RI 2017 Q5, HCI 2019 Q5, NJC 2019 Q7. Two bulbs different P/T/V, open tap, find final P at given T. Test of n₁ + n₂ = n_total + Boyle's/Charles' applied per bulb.

3. **Stereoisomer count for complex molecule**: HCI 2017 Q14 (triglyceride), HCI 2017 Q18 (enantiomeric excess), VJC 2017 P2 (multi-chiral). Usually 2ⁿ where n = chiral centres + counting trick for symmetry.

4. **Mechanism → rate law consistency**: HCI 2017 Q9 (CHCl3 + Cl2), HCI 2019 Q10 (NO + H2). RDS determines rate equation; check stoichiometry of intermediates.

5. **Period 3 element identification from oxide/chloride behaviour**: HCI 2017 Q28, RI 2018 Q9, NJC 2018 P2 Q1b. "X chloride hydrolyses to acidic solution but oxide gives basic — what is X?"

6. **Transition metal cation flow chart**: HCI 2017 Q30 (last MCQ tradition). Solution J + various reagents producing colour cascade — identify cation.

## Structured Question (P2) Recurring Setups

### Energetics
- **Hess cycle to find unknown** with one or two given ΔH values + Data Booklet bond energies (RI 2018 Q2 anthracene resonance energy).
- **Born-Haber cycle for ionic compound** with given hydration enthalpies (NJC 2017 Q1; HCI 2018 Q1 — HF dissociation cycle including electron affinity; NJC 2018 Q2 — LiH then LiCl comparison).
- **Lattice energy comparison** for CaCl2 vs Al2O3 vs MgSO4 (NJC 2017 Q5 — CaCl2 → predict Al2O3).
- **Enthalpy of solution from ΔT data** (RI 2018 Q4 — Epsom salt MgSO4·7H2O determined experimentally then Hess back to hydration ΔH).

### Bonding / Period 3
- **Dot-and-cross of unusual species**: N2O, NO2 (NJC 2017 Q1b); IF4⁻ (RI 2018 Q2c); HN3 (HCI 2018 Q1d); ClSO3H (NJC 2019 Q2); S4N4 with double/single bonds (VJC 2018 Q1f).
- **Bond angle / shape of weird cations/anions**: BrF2+ vs BrF4⁻ (VJC 2018 Q1e); WO4²⁻ (VJC 2019 Q4d).
- **Compare melting points of Period 3 chlorides/oxides** with structure-bonding explanation (NJC 2017 Q5b; VJC 2018 Q5a).

### Transition Metal / Coordination
- **d-orbital splitting diagram** with high spin vs low spin comparison (NJC 2018 Q3b; VJC 2019 Q2b; HCI 2019 Q3c).
- **Ligand substitution + colour change explanation** invoking ΔE → wavelength absorbed → complementary colour seen.
- **Identify complex from elemental analysis** (Ru complex — NJC 2017 Q2c; Co complex — RI 2018 Q3; CrCl3·6H2O isomers — RI 2018 Q3d).
- **EDTA / chelate effect** (NJC 2019 Q2e).

### Kinetics
- **Rate equation determination from concentration-time graph** (NJC 2017 Q4a — halogenoalkane substitution; VJC 2017 Q4a — ester hydrolysis; RI 2018 Q3a — aldol reaction).
- **Boltzmann distribution to explain rate change** (NJC 2017 Q4; RI 2018 Q3b; HCI 2018 Q1d; HCI 2019 Q1).
- **Catalyst mode of action** — homo vs hetero, ALWAYS includes "lowers Ea via different mechanism." (NJC 2019 Q3 — vanadium catalysis; HCI 2019 Q3f — cobalt catalysis).

### Acids / Bases
- **Buffer pH calculation + indicator selection** + equation showing how buffer resists added acid/base (VJC 2017 Q1; HCI 2019 Q2 — MES buffer at pH 5.5).
- **Polyprotic / amino acid titration**: histidine 3 pKa (HCI 2017 Q5); glutamic acid 3 pKa with isoelectric point (HCI 2018 Q4); thioglycolic acid 2 pKa (VJC 2018 Q3a); malonic acid 2 pKa with titration sketch (VJC 2019 Q2a).
- **Buffer ratio calculation given pH = pKa ± shift** (HCI 2019 Q2).

### Organic
- **Mechanism with full curly arrows** (electrophilic substitution Friedel-Crafts; SN1 vs SN2 styrene oxide; nucleophilic addition aldol; electrophilic addition with HBr including Markovnikov; free radical chlorination/bromination).
- **Predict products from given reagents on multifunctional molecule** (HCI 2017 Q4 — elenolic acid with LiAlH4, hot iodine/NaOH, hot KMnO4/H+).
- **Compare acidity of two carboxylic acids** with one substituted (VJC 2017 P3 Q5 — 4-methoxybenzoic acid vs benzoic acid).
- **3-step synthesis from benzene** to target compound (recurring — 2017 ACJC, NJC, VJC; 2018 RI, HCI).

## Paper 3 Section A — Recurring Frameworks

### Q1 — usually energetics + acid/base + Group 2/13 chemistry
- Thermal decomp of Group 2 carbonates with explanation of trend (cation polarising power)
- Born-Haber cycle of an ionic compound
- pH calculation for polybasic acid/base titration with sketch graph
- Comparison of solubility of two Group 2 salts via Ksp

### Q2 — usually transition metal + ligand + redox + organic synthesis
- E°cell calculation for non-standard conditions (Nernst-like reasoning)
- Predict reaction direction from E° values + observed colour change
- Latimer diagram interpretation (HCI 2018 Q5d; VJC 2019 Q3a) — appears in late 2017-2019 papers as a "novel info" extension

### Q3 — organic deduction
- The classic A → B → C deduction with 6-12 functional group test observations
- Mark distribution heavily weighted toward STRUCTURES (1 mark each) with bonus marks for explaining reaction types

## Practical (P4) Patterns

### Standard kit students must perform
1. Pipette + burette titration (acid-base + redox)
2. Calorimetry: read thermometer to 0.1 °C, plot ΔT vs volume, extrapolate
3. QA notes interpretation: cation tests with NaOH/NH3, anion tests with HNO3/AgNO3, organic tests (NaHCO3, FeCl3, 2,4-DNPH, Tollens', Fehling's, hot KMnO4, alkaline I2/NaOH)

### 2017-2019 P4 fingerprints
- HCI 2017 P4 Q1: NaOH + Na2CO3 mixture by double-indicator titration (thymolphthalein then methyl orange)
- RI 2018 P4 Q1: CuSO4 concentration via iodometry (Cu²⁺ + I⁻ → CuI + I2, then I2 + S2O3²⁻)
- RI 2018 P4 Q2: NaClO + S2O3²⁻ thermochemistry, plot ΔT vs V to find stoichiometry + ΔHrxn
- HCI 2019 P4 Q1: H2SO4 + NaOH plot ΔT vs V to find concentration + Hneut, then predict effect of replacing with malonic acid

### Recurring P4 trap zones
- **Iodometry stoichiometry**: 2 Cu²⁺ + 4 I⁻ → 2 CuI + I2; I2 + 2 S2O3²⁻ → 2 I⁻ + S4O6²⁻. Net 2 mol Cu²⁺ ≡ 2 mol S2O3²⁻ (1:1 directly). High-frequency calculation mistake.
- **Dilution factor reasoning** in pilot experiment (HCI 2017 P4; RI 2018 P4).
- **Error analysis** distinguishing systematic (calibration, heat loss) from random (parallax, end-point). Improvement must address THE error stated.
- **QA note matching** for unknown salt (yellow ppt + dissolves in excess NH3 = AgI or AgBr; etc.)

## 9647 → 9729 Transition Markers

### New topics emphasized in 9729 (vs old 9647) — appearing in 2017-2019 papers
- **Latimer diagrams** (HCI 2018, VJC 2019) — entered syllabus
- **Henderson-Hasselbalch via given equation** instead of pure first-principles (most JCs adopted by 2019)
- **Boltzmann distribution explicit sketch + label** required more often (was sometimes left implicit in 9647)
- **Nernst equation as given formula** to apply (NJC 2019 Q1) — assessment style introduced
- **Polypeptide / amino acid structural drawing in extended writing** (NJC 2018 Q4)

### Style changes carried OVER from 9647 (still present in 2017-2019)
- Multi-step organic synthesis with reagent boxes (unchanged from 9647)
- Period 3 oxide/chloride trends with equations (unchanged)
- Dot-and-cross diagrams (unchanged)
- Mechanism with curly arrows (unchanged, but more rigorous expectation on showing lone pairs and dipoles in 9729)
- Ksp solubility comparison (unchanged)

### Differences with later years (2022+)
- Sustainability / industrial process / green chemistry context less prominent in 2017-2019. By 2022, ALL papers have a "real-world chemistry" framing block. 2017-2019 still allow abstract chemistry questions.
- 2017-2019 prefer multi-functional natural products (elenolic acid, isoflavenoids, alanine derivatives) over modern pharmaceuticals.
- Practical (P4) is more straightforward in 2017-2019 — fewer "design your own experiment" planning sub-parts; more direct cookbook + analysis.

## Top 10 Question Setups to Bank (for Ethan)

These are the highest-frequency, highest-yield question structures across 2017-2019. Master these = ~60% of all marks attemptable.

1. **Born-Haber cycle for ionic compound** with EA and hydration ΔH given — used in 8+ papers across the 3 years. Draw cycle, identify the unknown step, sum to ΔHform.

2. **Compare two transition metal complexes** (different ligands or different geometries) explaining colour difference via d-orbital splitting + ligand field strength. (HCI 2017 Q2 phen vs ferrozine; HCI 2019 Q3 Co/Cl vs Co/SCN; NJC 2018 Q3 high vs low spin).

3. **Multi-step organic deduction** with 6+ functional group test observations giving 4-6 unknown structures + explain ~3 reaction types. (See Section A Q3 list above — appears EVERY paper, Section B Q5 also.)

4. **Friedel-Crafts mechanism (alkylation OR acylation)** with all electrons shown, then sketch product after KMnO4 oxidation of the side chain. (NJC 2017 Q5; ACJC 2019; VJC 2018 Q1.)

5. **Rate equation determination from data table or graph** — order with respect to each species + write rate equation + calculate k with units + Boltzmann sketch. (NJC 2017 Q4; RI 2018 Q3; HCI 2019 Q1; HCI 2019 Q2 enzyme.)

6. **Buffer pH problem chain**: pKa of weak acid given, given ratio → pH OR vice versa; add small amount of strong acid/base; calculate new pH; choose indicator from table. (VJC 2017 Q1; HCI 2019 Q2; HCI 2018 Q4.)

7. **Polyprotic acid titration sketch** with 2-3 pKa values + draw structures at each equivalence point + identify isoelectric point. (HCI 2017 Q5 histidine; HCI 2018 Q4 glutamic acid; VJC 2019 Q2 malonic acid.)

8. **Period 3 element identification** from oxide pH + chloride hydrolysis observations + write equation for hydrolysis + suggest pH. (VJC 2018 Q5; NJC 2018 Q1; HCI 2018 Q3.)

9. **Iodometric titration P4 problem**: Cu²⁺/I⁻ → CuI + I2 → titrate with S2O3²⁻ → calc [Cu²⁺]. Plus error analysis. (HCI 2017; RI 2018; appears in MANY non-priority JC P4s too.)

10. **3-step synthesis from benzene** to substituted aromatic (often methylbenzoic acid, phenol derivative, or amino-substituted compound). State reagents + conditions + intermediate structures. (Recurring in EVERY year's P2 or P3 of every JC.)

## Calibration Rules — How To Pitch Practice Questions From This Era

- **Use 2017-2019 as the "core competency" baseline.** If Ethan can clear these papers cleanly, he has the fundamentals. Later years (2022+) layer on application/context but the chemistry is the same.
- **2017 is the easiest of the three** in terms of question novelty — it was the first 9729 cohort, JCs were conservative.
- **2018 and 2019 slowly creep up** in Section B Q5 difficulty and in P3 deduction puzzle length.
- **For SIR on a specific topic**, use the relevant Q from the 12 priority papers as the test stem — they're all marked with detailed solutions in the source folder.
- **For mock paper practice**, RI > HCI > NJC > VJC in difficulty calibration (RI is hardest, VJC is most "textbook-fair"). Ethan should mock papers in this order to step up gradually.
- **P4 practical**: do at least one HCI or RI P4 from this era end-to-end (with timer) before October prep starts. The structure is unchanged.
- **Avoid 2017 RI P3 Section B Q5 (DMAN) for first attempt** — uses concepts that were unusual that year and most students dropped marks; do it as the LAST priority question after the rest of the cluster is solid.
