## H2 Chem 2013-2016 Examiner Patterns (9647 syllabus era — NOT current)

### Paper format (9647 prelims)

| Paper | Format | Duration | Marks | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper 1 | 40 MCQ (single-answer A/B/C/D) | 1 hour | 40 | No multiple-completion (single-best) — pure MCQ. No negative marking. |
| Paper 2 | Structured questions, ALL compulsory | 2 hours | 72 | **Q1 is ALWAYS the Planning Question (12 marks)**. Then 4–5 more structured Qs. |
| Paper 3 | Free Response, **answer ANY 4 of 5 questions** | 2 hours | 80 | Each Q = 20 marks. Mixed topic per Q (typically organic + inorganic blended). |
| Paper 4 | **DOES NOT EXIST in 9647 prelims (2013-2016).** Practical was a separate national paper. | — | — | First P4 prelim in archive = 2017 (9729 era). |

**Cover sheet grade breakdown observed (RI 2016 P2):**
> P1 / 40   P2 / 60   P3 / 80   Planning / 12   Penalty   Total / 100

So at A-level proper, scoring weighting was: P1 (40), P2 (60 from structured), P3 (80), Planning (12 from P2 Q1), totalling /192 → scaled to /100. Different from 9729.

### Topic distribution per paper (deep-read JCs)

#### Paper 2 Q1 (Planning, always 12 marks)
Topics rotate through experimental procedures:
- RI 2013 P2 Q1: Enthalpy of isomerisation (cis→trans hexene) via flame calorimetry
- NJC 2013 P2 Q1: Solubility of Ca(IO3)2 via iodometric titration
- HCI 2014 P2 Q1: Determining Avogadro's constant via electrolysis of KI
- RI 2014 P2 Q1: Activation energy from iodine-propanone kinetics
- RI 2015 P2 Q1: Preparation of [Co(NH3)5(H2O)](NO3)3 cobalt(III) complex
- NJC 2015 P2 Q1: Qualitative organic identification (5 unknowns)
- HCI 2016 P2 Q1: Entropy change via electrochemical cell ΔG = -nFE
- RI 2016 P2 Q1: Determining Faraday constant via copper plating

**Planning Q archetypes (very stable across all 4 years):**
1. Thermochemistry (calorimetry, heat capacity, enthalpy values)
2. Electrolysis (Faraday, Avogadro, mole-ratio determinations)
3. Titrations (redox, iodometric, back-titration)
4. Kinetics (initial rates, activation energy)
5. Preparation + recrystallisation (especially transition metal complexes)
6. Qualitative organic ID (functional group tests)

#### Paper 2 Q2–Q6 (structured, compulsory)
Mark distribution variable (e.g. RI 2013 = 12/13/11/10/18/8; RI 2015 = 12/7/23/17/13; HCI 2016 = 12/17/14/13/16).

Topics seen:
- Periodicity / Period 3 trends
- Transition metals (Fe, Cu, Cr, Ni, Co complexes; ligand exchange; colour; oxidation state)
- Energetics + Born-Haber cycles
- Chemical equilibrium (Kc, Kp, Le Chatelier)
- Ionic equilibria (Ka, Kb, Kw, buffer pH, hydrolysis, Ksp, partition coefficient)
- Reaction kinetics (orders, rate equations, half-life, mechanism deduction)
- Electrochemistry (E°, electrolysis, fuel cells)
- Organic synthesis (multi-step routes, reagent + condition recall, mechanism)
- Spectroscopy/structure deduction (Mr, %composition, isomers, NMR-style logic)

#### Paper 3 (pick 4 of 5, each 20 marks)
Each question is a **multi-topic blend** — typically pairs one inorganic/physical with one organic theme. Stem usually starts with a real-world context (margarine hydrogenation, perfume hydrolysis, nickel catalyst, anti-malarial drug, anti-cancer cisplatin, prion proteins, hair perm chemistry).

Common topic pairings:
- Halogens + alkenes/arenes electrophilic chemistry
- Transition metals + ligand chemistry + redox
- Period 3 oxides/chlorides + acid-base + bonding
- Amino acids/proteins + carbonyls + acid-base
- Organic mechanism (SN1/SN2, EAS, ESR, nucleophilic addition) + reaction scheme synthesis
- Kinetics + organic reaction + mechanism step
- Thermochemistry/energetics + electrochemistry

### 9647 → 9729 differences (what to know)

**Structural changes (biggest):**

| Aspect | 9647 (2013-2016) | 9729 (2017+) |
|---|---|---|
| Paper count | 3 written (P1+P2+P3) + practical (separate) | 4 papers (P1 MCQ + P2 Structured + P3 Free Response + P4 Practical) |
| P2 Planning Q | Q1 fixed at 12 marks, weighted separately on grade sheet | Planning integrated into structured questions, no fixed slot |
| P3 question choice | **Answer any 4 of 5 × 20 marks** | **ALL compulsory** structured/free-response; no choice |
| MCQ structure | 40 single-best Qs (Section A only) | Has Section A (single-best) + Section B (multiple-completion, "1, 2, 3 correct") |
| Practical at prelim | **No prelim P4** in 2013-2016 archive | P4 prelims appear from 2017 onwards |
| Total mark weighting | /192 scaled to /100 | Different paper weighting under 9729 |

**Content/style differences:**
- 9647 P3 question-choice allowed students to skip 1 weak topic; 9729 forces full-syllabus coverage. **9729 students cannot dodge any topic.**
- 9647 Planning was a distinct skill block (12 marks); 9729 distributes planning skills across multiple Qs.
- Organic synthesis multi-step routes (Strecker, Claisen, Williamson) appear in both eras but 9729 expects tighter mechanism with curly arrows and more atom-economy reasoning.
- 9647 leaned heavier on "describe what you would observe" style qualitative analysis; 9729 favours quantitative + data interpretation more.

**Likely syllabus content shifts (general knowledge — verify with 9729 syllabus PDF):**
- Spectroscopy (mass spec, IR) added/expanded in 9729.
- Green chemistry / atom economy emphasis in 9729.
- Some legacy nomenclature (e.g. "vapourisation," older systematic names) appears in 9647 papers — 9729 uses updated IUPAC.

### Evergreen topics (carry directly to 9729)

These are FULLY transferable — 9647 questions on these are fair practice for 9729:

1. **Atomic structure, electronic configuration, ionisation energies** (RI 2014 P3 Q1d, NJC 2015 P2 Q2)
2. **Chemical bonding** — ionic/covalent/dative, VSEPR shapes, hybridisation, polarity (NYJC 2016 P3 Q2)
3. **Periodicity** — Period 3 trends in mp/bp, oxides reacting with water, chloride hydrolysis (RI 2013 P3 Q3, HCI 2015 P3 Q3)
4. **Chemical energetics** — enthalpy changes, Hess cycles, Born-Haber, bond energy (RI 2013 P3 Q2, RI 2016 P3 Q2)
5. **Reaction kinetics** — orders, rate equations, half-life, mechanism, autocatalysis (RI 2013 P3 Q1, HCI 2016 P3 Q1)
6. **Chemical equilibria** — Kc, Kp, Le Chatelier (NYJC 2016 P3 Q2b, HCI 2015 P3 Q4)
7. **Ionic equilibria** — Ka, Kb, Kw, pH, buffer, Ksp, hydrolysis of salts (HCI 2014 P3 Q3, HCI 2016 P3 Q2, NJC 2015 P3 Q2d)
8. **Electrochemistry** — E°, EMF, fuel cells, electrolysis (RI 2013 P3 Q2b, RI 2016 P3 Q2)
9. **Halogens + halogen chemistry** — Group 17 trends, displacement, ICl, interhalogens (HCI 2013 P3 Q1, NJC 2014 P3 Q1, HCI 2015 P3 Q2a-b)
10. **Transition metals** — d-block defs, complex ions, colour, ligand exchange, isomerism (RI 2013 P3 Q2, HCI 2014 P3 Q5, RI 2016 P3 Q3d-e)
11. **Organic foundations** — alkanes/alkenes/arenes/halogenoalkanes mechanisms (HCI 2014 P3 Q1, HCI 2016 P3 Q1c-d)
12. **Carbonyl chemistry** — aldehydes/ketones, nucleophilic addition, 2,4-DNPH, Fehling's/Tollens' (HCI 2013 P3 Q2, NJC 2014 P3 Q2, RI 2015 P3 Q1d)
13. **Carboxylic acids + derivatives** — esters, acyl chlorides, amides (RI 2013 P3 Q1, HCI 2014 P3 Q3)
14. **Nitrogen compounds** — amines, amides, basicity, amino acids/proteins (HCI 2014 P3 Q3b, RI 2015 P3 Q2, RI 2016 P3 Q1g)
15. **Organic synthesis route deduction** + reagent recall (universal across all papers)

### Outdated questions / styles to AVOID for 9729 prep

1. **P3 question-choice mechanics** — 9647 lets you skip 1 question. Do NOT replicate this when timing yourself for 9729 — do ALL 4 P3 Qs to mimic the new format.
2. **Planning Q as a standalone 12-mark block** — practise it, but expect 9729 to embed planning into normal structured Qs (smaller, integrated chunks).
3. **Nomenclature drift** — older 9647 papers use "vapourisation" instead of "vaporisation," "ethanenitrile" vs current "ethanenitrile" — minor, but be aware your answer should match current IUPAC.
4. **No spectroscopy questions** — 9647 papers in 2013-2016 contain almost no IR/mass-spec interpretation. 9729 expects this. Supplement with 2017+ papers for spectroscopy practice.
5. **No green chemistry / atom economy framing** — absent from 9647 stems. 9729 has these explicitly.
6. **No P4 practical-style data analysis at prelim level** — 9647 had a national practical only. Do NOT rely on 2013-2016 prelims for practical prep; use 2017+ P4 papers.
7. **Older systematic names** (e.g. "iron(II)" written as "ferrous" in occasional 9647 marking schemes) — read for content but write modern names in your answers.
8. **Acid-base/pH styling** — some 9647 questions use approximations (e.g. assuming [H⁺] = √(Ka·c)) that 9729 expects you to justify explicitly. Always state assumptions.
