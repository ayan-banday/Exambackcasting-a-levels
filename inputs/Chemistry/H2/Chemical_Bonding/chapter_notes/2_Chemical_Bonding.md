    1. What are the similarities and differences between an ionic lattice and metallic lattice? 
        1. Similarities: Hard (Alloy); conduct elec (ionic — soluble + molten/aqueous; high mp, bp) 
        2. Differences: Soluble; Malleable vs. brittle; elec (ionic solid)
    2. Explain what determines the strength of a covalent bond 
        1. Bond order (electrons) - Orbital Overlap (sigma/pi) - polarity 
    3. Explain how hybridisation leads to the difference between graphite and diamond despite being giant molecular structures 
        1. sp2 vs sp3 — unhybridised p orbital — 1)Deocaised electrons 2)weak dispersion forces — 1) conduct elec 2)be malleable
    4. How does intermolecular forces of attraction affect solubility of a substance? 
        1. Solute-Solvent > solvent-solvent + solute-solute
        2. solute-solvent: Extensive H bond (No intramolecular)
        3. Solvent-solvent: PD-PD
        4. Solute-solute: Dispersion forces (no of e- + surface area)
