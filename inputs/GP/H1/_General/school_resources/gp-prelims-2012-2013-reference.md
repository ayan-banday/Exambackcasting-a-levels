# GP Prelims 2012-2013 — Examiner Patterns Reference

Last updated: 2026-05-30
Scope: 132 files across 18 JCs / institutions, 2012-2013 prelim cycle.

Paper code transition: **8806 (2012) → 8807 (2013)**. P1 = 1h30m, 1 essay from 12, 500-800 words, marked 30 Content / 20 Language = 50 total. P2 = 1h30m, comprehension on 1 or 2 passages, marked 35 Content / 15 Language = 50 total (AQ 8-10 marks of the Content total).

## 2012 · ACJC · P2

Passage 1: Anna Rodrigue + Barry Schwartz, value of efficiency (~6 paras). Passage 2: Adam Frank, tyranny of efficiency / time-logic (~5 paras). AQ Q11: "Anna Rodrigue and Barry Schwartz think that the pursuit of efficiency is beneficial, whereas Adam Frank thinks that it is detrimental to both the individual and society. Which view do you and your society subscribe to and why? Illustrate your views with reference to the ways in which you and your society regard the pursuit of efficiency." [8]
Q1-9 comprehension structure: 9 sub-Qs (1-2m each), summary at Q9 (120 words, "Efficiency is beneficial because…", 8m). Vocab Q10 5 marks across both passages. Answer scheme uses two-column format (text reference | inferred/paraphrased).

### JC house style observations

- **ACJC** keeps a heavy two-passage tradition (both years). Mark scheme uses two-column "Text reference | Inferred" format. AQ phrasing "Which view do you subscribe to" (2012) → "consider the use of X in your society" (2013).
- **DHS** consistently single-passage, "How relevant are these perspectives to you and your society?". Includes "R / Ex / Ev" annotation guidance for AQ.
- **HCI** consistently two-passage, polar-opposite views (optimistic vs pessimistic, traditional vs subversive). Always asks "more persuasive."
- **IJC** rotates structure (2012 = 2-passage with opposing views on social media; 2013 = 1-passage). AQ blends "do you agree" with practical implication ("should X be encouraged/discouraged among young people").
- **NJC** prefers Pico Iyer–type cultural-observation passages. Mark scheme rigorous: notes which inferred answers are rejected (e.g., "sarcasm" wrong inference, mere severity inadequate).
- **NYJC** consistently single-passage with social-trend writers (Gibbs, Miller).
- **PJC** runs a 1-passage format with compound AQ phrasing ("develop some of the points… and some account of the experiences which have helped you to form them" — clear house style across both years).
- **RI** moved from 2-passage (2012 happiness debate) to 1-passage (2013 Giddens on tradition). House style: 5 paras each in 2-passage, ~10 paras single-passage. AQ "How applicable" standard.
- **TJC** consistently 2-passage with polar opposing views (nostalgia 2012, then 1-passage 2013 Andrew Keen). AQ "How relevant are their observations to your own society."
- **VJC** has an unusual house style: 13 sub-Qs in P2 (one extra), AQ marked at 10m (higher than typical 8m), labels its paper "H2 GP" in 2013 (anomaly — official code was 8807 H1). Mark scheme requires explicit Singapore evidence with "MARUAH", "Hong Lim Park", etc. listed in suggested answers.
- **MI** (Pre-University 3, not a JC) consistently 1-passage, AQ "How applicable", structurally identical to JCs.
- **CJC** 2013 uses 10m AQ and the R/Ex/Ev annotation system — only college besides VJC and YJC to deviate from 8m.
- **YJC, TPJC, SRJC** all maintain consistent 1-passage 12-13 sub-Q format with standard AQ phrasing.

### Comparison to 2008-2010 era (what shifted)

Based on this 2012-2013 dataset and the 2008-2010 reference cluster:

1. **Paper code transition**: 2012 still uses 8806 (older code) for both papers; 2013 switches to 8807 (new SEAB code) reflecting the transition from old H1 GP syllabus. Question style stays consistent across the change.

2. **Sharp uptick in social media / new media references**: 2008-2010 era largely focused on "new media" as an abstract; 2012-2013 cycle explicitly names "social media," "Facebook," "Twitter" in essay prompts and passages (Deresiewicz, Levy, Keen). Privacy concerns become more explicit (NJC 12 Q8, AJC 13 Q3, PJC 13 P2).

3. **Singapore-specific framing more frequent**: "In your society" / "in your country" / "Is this true of your society" appears in roughly 30-40% of P1 questions in 2012-2013 versus what appeared to be ~20-25% in 2008-2010. The 2013 cycle is especially Singapore-oriented (NJC 13 Q4 racial harmony, VJC 13 Q3 social tolerance, MJC 13 Q11 entrepreneurship in Singapore, RVHS 13 Q4 family matters in your country).

4. **Apocalyptic / pessimistic framing emerging**: 2012-2013 includes more "future of mankind" questions (HCI 12 P2 polar passages, ACJC 12 Q11 society never satisfied, RVHS 12 Q11 obsessed with fear), reflecting post-2008-financial-crisis and Eurozone anxieties.

5. **Identity questions more nuanced**: 2008-2010 focused on race/ethnicity broadly; 2012-2013 shifts to identity through choice ("identity divides more than unites" AJC 13 Q11, citizenship as more than paper CJC 12 Q1, Friedman 2013 TPJC P2 on multiple citizenship).

6. **Ageing demographics becoming explicit**: ACJC 12 Q3 ("grey is the new gold"), DHS 12 P2 (Demographic Singularity), AJC 13 Q5 (elderly burden), DHS 13 Q10 (old age responsibility), YJC 13 Q3 (golden years), RVHS 13 Q6 (immigration for ageing society). Reflects Singapore demographic crisis salience post-2011 White Paper context.

7. **AQ marks creep up**: Standard was 8m across 2008-2010; CJC, VJC, YJC begin marking AQ at 10m in 2013. By 2014+ this becomes more common (later A-level moves toward 10m AQ standard).

8. **"How applicable" formula dominates AQ phrasing**: This is now the canonical 1-passage AQ — same across most JCs. By contrast 2008-2010 reportedly had more variety. House style consolidation is visible.
