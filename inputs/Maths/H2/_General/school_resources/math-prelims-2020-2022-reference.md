# H2 Math (9758) Prelim Reference: 2020–2022

JC Preliminary Examination question banks from 2020, 2021, 2022 syllabus 9758. Built from deep reads of 6 priority JCs per year (RI, HCI, NJC, NYJC, VJC, ACJC where available) plus skim-extracts of remaining JCs (CJC, DHS, MI, YIJC, TJC, ASRJC, SAJC, EJC, RVHS, JPJC, TMJC).

Format: 3-hour P1 = 100 marks pure math; 3-hour P2 = 40 marks Section A pure + 60 marks Section B probability/statistics. 12 questions in P1 typically, 11 questions in P2 (5 pure + 6 stats).

### Mark allocation patterns

- **P1 typical structure (~100 marks across 11–12 Qs):** Mostly 4–8 mark Qs early, climbing to 10–14 mark Qs at the end. Last 2 Qs are always applied (DE modelling or optimization).
- **P2 Section A (40 marks):** 4–5 Qs averaging 8–10 marks each.
- **P2 Section B (60 marks):** 6 Qs ranging 5–13 marks; the 11–13 mark Qs are almost always the normal distribution Q or DRV Q.
- **No-calculator question:** Every paper has at least one complex numbers Q labelled "Do not use a calculator." Polynomial root-finding is standard.

### COVID-era observations (2020–2022)

The H2 Math 9758 syllabus was unaffected by COVID. No format changes, no topic removals, no mark adjustments observed.

**Thematic shift in real-world contexts:** From 2020 onwards, applied questions reference COVID-adjacent contexts more openly:
- 2020 HCI P1 Q9: oil spill on fish (not COVID)
- 2020 NYJC P1 Q12: reproduction number R₀ of infectious disease (clearly COVID-inspired)
- 2020 NJC P2 Q12: food delivery during circuit breaker, free delivery service
- 2020 RI P1 Q12: Atlantic croaker (von Bertalanffy)
- 2021 HCI P1 Q10: flu virus infection dynamics
- 2021 VJC P2 Q10: bakery waiting times during pandemic
- 2021 NJC P2 Q6: Bus 156 waiting time during COVID-19 (2022 NJC P2)
- 2022 HCI P2 Q9: handmade chocolate bars (cottage industry context post-supply-chain)

The mathematics is unchanged. The framing reflects timely real-world contexts but tests the same techniques: DE solving, hypothesis testing, normal/binomial modelling.

### Top 10 questions worth banking (highest signal for exam encoding)

1. **2020 RI P1 Q12 — von Bertalanffy growth model.** Sets up DE from proportional statement, solves to general solution, fits parameters. Template for any "growth rate proportional to (limit minus current value)" Q.
2. **2020 HCI P1 Q12 — Avatar reflection across surface.** Vectors 3D with reflection. Combines line, plane, reflection, distance, intersection. Tests every vector skill in one Q.
3. **2020 NJC P1 Q12 — Rotterdam sculpture.** Three perpendicular slabs as planes, build Cartesian equation, find foot of perpendicular, pigeon paths. Premier vectors 3D applied Q.
4. **2020 ACJC P1 Q10 — Light ray reflected at mirror plane.** Skew lines + reflection across plane + ray cutting both lines. Connects vectors, complex geometry.
5. **2021 RI P1 Q10 — Loan repayment DE.** Models exponential growth + constant repayment. Derives finite-time condition. Sets template for population/finance DE.
6. **2021 HCI P1 Q10 — Logistic virus growth.** dI/dt ∝ I(N-I). Logistic equation as a standard model. Long-run analysis.
7. **2021 RI P2 Q9 — Non-binomial probability tree.** Bag with replacement rule + extra balls. Forces students to recognise NOT binomial. Strong test of independence/identical distribution conditions.
8. **2022 RI P1 Q11 — Drink can optimization (two-stage).** Standard cylinder optimization then redesigned can with rate of change. Tests both optimization and related rates.
9. **2022 HCI P1 Q12 — Cargo drone motion.** Couples second-order DE with first-order DE via dv/dt notation. Sketches v vs t, analyses dx/dt and d²x/dt² long-run. Premier physics-modelling Q.
10. **2022 NJC P1 Q12 — Torricelli funnel drainage.** v² = 2gh and dV/dt = -Av (volume flow rate). Standard hydraulic DE. Solves to explicit h(t), finds emptying time. Template for any drainage/leak Q.
