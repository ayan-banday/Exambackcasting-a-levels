## Format Spec Summary (Confirmed from papers)

### H2 Math 9758 Promo (JC1)
- **1 paper, 3 hours, 100 marks**
- **Pure Math content only** — Section A/B split is NOT used; one undifferentiated Paper 1 covering J1 topics
- 11-13 questions, ascending difficulty
- "Give non-exact numerical answers correct to 3 sf, or 1 dp for angles in degrees" (standard rubric)
- Graphing calculator (TI-84 / Casio) expected; "unsupported answers from a graphing calculator are allowed unless a question specifically states otherwise"
- Some JCs deduct up to 2 marks for "improper presentation" (e.g., NJC 2024 explicitly states this)
- MF27 formula list provided

### H1 Math 8865 (current code) / 8864 (legacy) Prelim
- **1 paper, 3 hours, 100 marks**
- **Section A: Pure Math (40 marks)** + **Section B: Statistics (60 marks)** — standard
- Pure topics: systems of equations (matrix word problem), inequalities, exponential/logarithmic, calculus (max-min, integration, definite integrals, area), curve sketching, modelling
- Stats topics: permutations/combinations, probability (incl. conditional, tree diagrams), discrete distributions (binomial), normal distribution + linear combinations, sampling, hypothesis testing (1-tail and 2-tail, set-of-values), correlation/regression (scatter, PMCC, line of best fit y=mx+c, prediction)
- **Stats overlap with H2:** Sections 6, 7 (Probability), 9–11 (Normal, Hypothesis Testing, Correlation/Regression) — directly reusable. No vectors, complex numbers, differential equations, Maclaurin series, sigma notation, recurrence in H1.

## Synthesis: H2 Promos + H1 Math Patterns

### H2 Promo calibration (J1 difficulty)

**Topic coverage (always present in modern H2 Promo):**
1. Inequalities (rational, non-calc) — Q1 or Q2 territory, 3-5 marks
2. Maclaurin series (from given DE, expand to x² or x³) — 5 marks
3. Sigma / method of differences + recurrence relations — combined 5-7 marks
4. Graph sketching + transformations (1/f, f', |f|, f(ax+b)) — 6-9 marks
5. Vectors (2D and 3D), including planes, lines, perpendicularity, line-plane intersection — 10-15 marks
6. Parametric curves + tangents + related rates — 8-12 marks
7. Functions: composite, inverse, range, domain restriction — 8-12 marks
8. Differentiation applications (max-min, related rates, implicit) — 10-15 marks
9. Integration (substitution, by parts, partial fractions) — 5-12 marks
10. AP/GP application (compound interest, loans, training schedules) — 10-15 marks
11. Linear systems (matrix word problem, 3x3) — sometimes Q1, 3-4 marks

**Topics OFTEN absent or rare in Promos:**
- Complex numbers — only some JCs (RI 2024 Q7, VJC 2024 Q7, RJC 2022, HCI 2021); usually appears in **Prelim** for full H2
- Differential equations (separable, linear) — rare in Promos, appears in Prelim
- Discrete distributions, normal, hypothesis testing, correlation/regression — **NOT in Promos** (Statistics is in Prelim P2 only)
- 3D vector volumes/perpendicular distance — sometimes Promo, more often Prelim

**Mark allocation pattern in H2 Promo:**
- 11-13 questions
- First 3-4 questions: 3-5 marks each, single topic
- Mid 4-5 questions: 5-10 marks, 2-3 parts
- Final 3-4 questions: 10-15 marks each, multi-part applications (vectors + planes, AP/GP financial, parametric + integration)

**Difficulty vs Prelim:**
- Promo Pure ≈ Prelim P1 Pure in style, slightly easier on integration/DE since J1 has not yet covered all techniques (partial fractions sometimes left to J2)
- Promo emphasises foundation: differentiation, graphs, vectors, functions
- Prelim layers in: integration techniques, DE, complex numbers (P1), full Stats (P2)
- **Use Promo for J1 topic isolation; do NOT use as Prelim mock**

### H1 Math examiner patterns

**Topic coverage (H1 8865 syllabus, fully sampled):**

*Pure (40 marks, ~5-6 questions):*
1. Systems of linear equations (matrix word problem, 3x3) — 3-5 marks
2. Inequalities (rational, non-calc) — 3-4 marks
3. Exponential and logarithm: differentiation, equation solving, modelling — 4-6 marks
4. Differentiation applications (max-min real-world: fencing, container, profit) — 6-8 marks
5. Integration (definite, area between curves, partial fractions if simple) — 4-8 marks
6. Modelling (revenue/cost, growth, cooling) — 6-10 marks
**Note: NO vectors, complex numbers, DE, Maclaurin, sigma, recurrence in H1.**

*Statistics (60 marks, ~6-7 questions):*
1. Permutations and combinations (word arrangements with restrictions) — 4-6 marks
2. Probability (conditional, tree diagrams, Venn) — 4-8 marks
3. Discrete distributions (binomial: P(X=x), P(X>x), assumptions, mean/variance) — 6-10 marks
4. Normal distribution (single, linear combinations, σ from probability) — 6-12 marks
5. Sampling methods (systematic, stratified) — sometimes 2-3 marks
6. Hypothesis testing (1-tail, 2-tail, set-of-values of t or α, validity assumptions) — 8-12 marks
7. Correlation and regression (scatter, PMCC interpret, regression line y on x, prediction validity in/out of data range, reversed regression) — 8-12 marks

**Stats/probability questions reusable for H2 P2 stats practice:**

H1 Stats syllabus is a **strict subset of H2 P2 Stats syllabus**. H2 Stats adds:
- Poisson distribution (H1 has only binomial; H2 has both + Poisson approx)
- Larger linear combinations of distributions

But H1 covers everything in H2 except Poisson. **Direct overlap:** binomial, normal, hypothesis testing, correlation/regression, sampling, probability, permutations/combinations. **Mark H1 questions on these topics as legitimate H2 P2 Stats practice** — same skill, same mark scheme style, same difficulty.
