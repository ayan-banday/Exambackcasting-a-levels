### 9740 vs 9758 differences observed
- **Poisson distribution** is heavily tested in 9740 (every P2 has at least one full Poisson question, often 8–15m, sometimes paired with Poisson→Normal approximation). **Not in 9758 syllabus.** Skip all Poisson questions for direct prep, but they remain useful for **conditional probability** and **distribution-of-sums** practice.
- **Differential equations** appear in every P1 (4–10m) and often again in P2 Section A (5–10m), with substitution methods (z = ye^(2x), w = y/t², u = x − y, etc.) as core technique. **9758 dropped DE entirely from 2017 onwards.** Skip unless using as integration-by-substitution practice.
- **Sampling method explanation** in 9740 weighs heavier (4-mark "describe how to take a quota/stratified/systematic sample" questions are common). 9758 still asks but less verbose.
- **Question framing in P1** is identical between 9740 and 9758 syllabuses (same Cambridge spec), so all Functions, Complex, Vectors, Sequences, Maclaurin, Integration, Parametric, Curve sketching, Inequalities questions are 1:1 valid prep.
- **Stats Q in 9740** tend to combine 2-3 sub-topics (e.g. Poisson + Normal approximation + hypothesis test in one long question) — 9758 keeps the same combo style.
- **Notation:** "Find P(A | B)" and conditional probability phrasing identical. Argand diagram conventions identical. Vector r = a + λb in both.
