## Headline calibration rules (top 5)

1. **P1 architecture locked:** inequality/functions opener → mid-paper calculus/sequences/complex → vectors closer (12-14m) or applied closer (12-15m). P2A always includes Maclaurin + vectors. P2B always includes normal + hypothesis test as longest questions (10-14m).

2. **Mark allocation locked:** "Show that" = 2-4m. Sketches = 2-3m (shape + asymptotes + intercepts). "Hence" follow-ups = 1-3m. Multi-part vectors/complex = 8-14m. Hypothesis test core = 4-6m + 2m assumptions.

3. **Verbatim Cambridge stems are mandatory:** "stating equations of asymptotes, coordinates of stationary points, intersections with axes" / "In this question you should state the parameters of any distributions you use" / "(You need not show that your answer gives a maximum.)" / "Without using a calculator" reserved for complex numbers + inequalities only.

4. **Diagram standards:** 3D vectors must include O + i,j,k labels. Argand diagrams shown as grid with O marked, student plots. Scatter diagrams grid only, student plots. Distribution sketches use bell curves with means labelled.

5. **Strict out-of-syllabus avoid list** (removed in 2017 9758 transition): Poisson distribution, geometric distribution, t-test, sign test, confidence intervals, second-order differential equations, polar coordinates.

## 9740 → 9758 transition (2017) — what changed

- **Removed:** Poisson distribution, geometric distribution, t-test, sign test, confidence intervals, second-order DEs, polar coordinates.
- **Emphasised:** sampling distribution + CLT, DRV with given probability function, regression with non-linear transformations (1/x, √x, x², ln x), longer real-world applied questions.
- **Style locked from 2018 onwards.** MF15 → MF26 → MF27 (2024+).
- **Practical impact:** ~15-20% of pre-2017 prelim questions are now off-syllabus. Flag and skip when drilling.

## Confidence by topic family for paper generation

**HIGH:** Functions, graphs, transformations, vectors 3D, complex numbers, sequences/series, integration, parametric, normal distribution, hypothesis testing, P&C, probability, correlation/regression, first-order DEs.

**MEDIUM:** Maclaurin (standard MF27 series — further-diff approach varies by JC), Binomial distribution with multiple assumptions, Inequalities "Hence" variants (substitution patterns vary).

**LOWER:** Method of differences with deduction extensions (Cambridge vs prelim style differs), DRV game expectation with sample mean (deeper Section B closers vary by year).
