## How to use this corpus (priority order for Ethan)

1. **Mark-scheme-first.** Before encoding a topic, pull that chapter's GST/TPE *solutions* + the matching Cambridge TYS solution. Read what earns the method (M) and answer (A) marks, then encode backwards.
2. **School material > generic.** For any drill or paper on a topic, use the HCI chapter notes + tutorials here before the other-school `knowledge-base/`. Use `knowledge-base/` for *additional* authentic-style questions and paper-generation calibration.
3. **JC2 priority (now):** Ch10 Complex Numbers + Statistics S1–S6 are current. These are the live topics for UWA2 → prelims → A-levels. Pure Ch1–Ch9 are revision/maintenance via SIR.
4. **TPE Rev Packages = exam-grade.** The `S1–S6 2026 TPE` and `09/10 TPE` packages are the closest to A-level standard in this set — use them for timed Mode-3 practice.
5. **Practical (P4) not in this corpus** — H2 Math has no practical; ignore the PRACTICAL RULE for Math.
