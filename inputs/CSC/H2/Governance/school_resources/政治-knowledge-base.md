### 梁 第2课题第1讲：中国的党国体制
- **Source:** 梁
- **Lecture position:** 第1讲
- **Sub-topic:** 党国体制 (Party-State System) — foundational lecture for entire 课题二
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **党国体制 (Party-state system):** Party power "国家化" (statified). Party absorbs state organs into its own organizational system. The Party is the most powerful and active force; party penetrates state and society through dense organizational network. CCP holds absolute power, achieves total control over state and society. Originally referred to communist party-states (also Leninist party-state).
  - **使命型政党 vs 选举型政党 (Mission-type party vs Election-type party):** 郑永年 framework. CCP is "使命型政党" — legitimacy from carrying historical mission and realizing blueprint, not from elections. Mission internal to organization, members deeply identify with it. Different in kind from Western competitive election parties.
  - **民主集中制 (Democratic Centralism):** Root organizational principle of CCP. 民主 = full expression of party member views. 集中 = unified will/intelligence/action. "四个服从" (four obediences): 党员服从组织 / 少数服从多数 / 下级服从上级 / 全党服从党中央.
  - **一元化领导 (One-track unified leadership):** Mao-era model. Party makes both decisions AND executes them. 大政方针在政治局, 具体部署在书记处. Overlaps with 全能主义 (totalism) — political power dominates everything.
  - **党政分开 vs 党政机构并列 (Party-state separation vs Parallel party-state):** Deng Xiaoping's 1980 reform attempt (separate Party from government, each does its own job). Post-1989 江泽民 reverses to "党政机构并列" — Party and state organs run in parallel, but legislative/executive/supervisory power all concentrated in CCP. Under Xi: "党是领导一切的" (Party leads everything).
- **Core argument / mechanism:** China's party-state system is a unique political configuration responding to "late developer" challenges — needing strong unified leadership for nation-building and modernization given vast scale, multi-ethnicity, scarce resources. CCP transitioned from revolutionary party (社会动员) to ruling party (执政党) — but the only path is 法治化与制度化. Under 习, 党的权力国家化 deepens; he replaced Hu Jintao's "九龙治水" (fragmented authority — nine dragons) with personal/institutional centralization, but creates 单点故障 (single-point-of-failure) risks.
- **Specific data / cases / years:**
  - 1921 founding: 50 党员 → today 9804.1 万 党员, ~20% growth p.a.
  - 1949 PRC founded; revolution 1921-1949
  - "三大法宝" (Mao): 统一战线 / 武装斗争 / 党的建设
  - 1980年8月 Deng's 《党和国家领导制度的改革》 critiquing 文革
  - 1987 中共十三大 — only "real" political reform attempt
  - 1989 6.4风波 / 1991 苏东巨变 — triggers reversal
  - 15大 (1997): "依法治国" enters CCP doctrine
  - 16大: improving 执政能力建设
  - 19大 习: 党要领导一切
  - 2018 十三届人大一次会议 — 《监察法》 establishes 国家监察委员会, 与中纪委合署办公; integrated three-power division: 决策 (党/人大), 执行 (国务院), 监察 (国监委)
  - 中央政治局 ~20 委员; 政治局常委 5-7 人
  - 中纪委 — 双重领导体制
  - 八个民主党派 — 参政党 status (政治协商), called "民主花瓶"
- **Cross-课题 connections:** Foundation for all 课题二 questions (Q12-Q20). Connects to 经济 (集中力量办大事 enables five-year plans, 一带一路), 社会 (掌控社会, 户籍制), 外交 (对外代表中国共产党).
- **Maps to 集锦 question(s):** Q12 (核心政治制度), Q13-Q20 (各 sub-topic 都建立在党国体制基础上)
- **High-leverage vocabulary:** 党国体制, 使命型政党, 民主集中制, 一元化领导, 党政分开, 党政机构并列, 党是领导一切的, 集中力量办大事, 一张蓝图绘到底, 单点故障, 九龙治水, 全能主义, 政治吸纳, 一龙治水

### 梁 第2课题第2讲：中国的法治建设与司法改革
- **Source:** 梁
- **Lecture position:** 第2讲
- **Sub-topic:** 法治建设 / 司法改革 (Rule of Law / Judicial Reform)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **法治 (rule of law) vs 法制 (rule by law):** 法治 = law as supreme standard, constraining public power, contractual spirit, procedural justice, value system that includes "恶法非法". 法制 = ruling tool/instrument used by ruling class, can become weapon to attack political enemies. 中国宪法 1999 enshrined "依法治国" but in practice closer to rule by law than rule of law.
  - **党主法治 / 党导法治 (Party-led rule of law):** Western label = 党主法治 (Party rules over rule of law). Chinese label = 党导法治 (Party guides). Core thesis from 18届4中全会: "党的领导是中国特色社会主义最本质的特征，是社会主义法治最根本的保证". Tension: if Party leads, who supervises Party? Answer = 纪委 + 党的领袖. But if those exceed law? No external check.
  - **司法独立 (judicial independence):** Court/judges immune from interference by administrative, legislative, social organizations or individuals. Core: 机构独立, 法官独立, 案件独立审理, 经费/任免独立性. 法官独立是司法独立的最高形态. Dworkin: "法官是法律帝国的王侯". China selectively imitates technique but rejects principle.
  - **全面依法治国 (Comprehensive rule of law):** Adopted 2014 中共十八届四中全会 《关于全面推进依法治国若干重大问题的决定》. 16-character mantra: 科学立法、严格执法、公正司法、全民守法. One of "四个全面": 全面建成小康社会 + 全面深化改革 + 全面依法治国 + 全面从严治党.
  - **国家治理体系与治理能力现代化:** Proposed 2013 中共十八届三中全会 as goal of 全面深化改革. Reaffirmed 2019 十九届四中全会. Measurement standards: 民主化, 科学化, 法治化, 文明化.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** Chinese legal system evolution traces 人治 → 法制 → 依法治国 → 全面依法治国. Major milestones: 1954 first Constitution, 1979 《刑法》《刑事诉讼法》 systemic legal recovery from Cultural Revolution, 1982 现行宪法, 1997 15大 first inscribes 依法治国, 2014 全面依法治国. Reforms: 去地方化 (provincial unification of court personnel), 去行政化 (separating judicial hierarchy), 去官僚化 (员额制, 主审法官负责制, 司法责任制). But limited: 党权 still trumps 法权; reform stays "技术性治理" because political reform is forbidden territory.
- **Specific data / cases / years:**
  - 1954 first Constitution
  - 1978 十一届三中全会 — 改革开放, 16-字 法治方针 ("有法可依、有法必依、执法必严、违法必究")
  - 1979 《刑法》《刑事诉讼法》
  - 1982 现行宪法
  - 1997 15大 — "依法治国"
  - 1999 宪法修正案 — "建设社会主义法治国家"
  - 2001 加入 WTO
  - 2014 18届4中全会 — first 全会 themed on 法治
  - 2018 国家监察委员会 (与中纪委合署)
  - 中央层面: 跨行政区划法院与检察院 (知识产权法院, 金融法院)
  - 平反冤假错案: 呼格吉勒图案, 聂树斌案
  - 2011 维稳经费首次超出国防开支
- **Cross-课题 connections:** Underpins 经济 (法治市场), 社会 (社会公正), 反腐 (法治反腐 vs 政治反腐). Connects to 课题三 信任 (塔西佗陷阱).
- **Maps to 集锦 question(s):** Q13-Q14 (法治与司法改革)
- **High-leverage vocabulary:** 党主法治, 党导法治, 法治与法制, 司法独立, 全面依法治国, 四个全面, 国家治理现代化, 良法善治, 党的领导是社会主义法治的最根本保证, 科学立法严格执法公正司法全民守法

### 梁 第2课题第3讲：中国的公共服务
- **Source:** 梁
- **Lecture position:** 第3讲
- **Sub-topic:** 公共服务 (Public Service) — but heavily explains 服务型政府 evolution which is central to political legitimacy
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **政治稳定 (Political stability):** State of relative equilibrium in political structure + continuity/orderliness of political system. Core interest of China. Three paths to political stability: (1) 暴力镇压 = 刚性政治稳定; (2) 意识形态 promotion; (3) 满足社会需求 — democratization for political participation, effective public service supply for material needs.
  - **公共服务 (Public service) / 基本公共服务:** Government uses statutory authority + public resources to provide goods/services with shared characteristics. Two forms: 基础设施 (transport, water, power, education, hospitals) + 无形 (security, welfare, training). 4E gold rule for evaluating: Economy / Efficiency / Effectiveness / Equity.
  - **均等化 / 均衡性 / 可及性 (Equalization / Balance / Accessibility):** Three nested goals. 均衡性 = base; 可及性 = guarantee; 均等化 = goal. 均等化 emphasizes equal opportunity (geographical, group). 可及性 includes physical reach + institutional reach (no barriers, low cost).
  - **全能主义 → 发展型政府 → 服务型政府 (Three governmental models):** (1) 全能主义/totalism (1949-1978): party-state penetrates everything, calculated economy, 政企不分; (2) 发展型政府 (1978-2002): post 三中全会, "以经济建设为中心", GDP 锦标赛 driving local cadres, 强政府弱市场; (3) 服务型政府 (2003-2012+): SARS-triggered, "以人为本", 温家宝 first proposed 2004.
  - **政绩合法性 (Performance legitimacy):** CCP grounds legitimacy in delivering economic growth + improving lives. Now expanding to 公共服务 as legitimacy source. Failure of public service = legitimacy crisis.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** China shifted government models in response to political/economic crises. The "GDP-first" 发展型政府 created environmental/social/inequality problems → SARS 2003 catalyzed 服务型政府 turn. Building 公共服务 system has multiple effects: 缓解社会矛盾, 促进社会公平, 增强国家治理能力, 提升执政合法性. Current challenges: 越位/缺位/错位 of government roles, 城乡区域 imbalance, 数字鸿沟, 制度执行不力.
- **Specific data / cases / years:**
  - 1978 十一届三中全会 — 改革开放
  - 改革开放40年 GDP 年均 9.3%
  - 2003 SARS — catalyst for 服务型政府
  - 2003 海南改革发展研究院 (迟福林) 14条建议
  - 2004年2月 温家宝 中央党校讲话首提服务型政府
  - 2002 16大 — 政府职能 "经济调节、市场监管、社会管理和公共服务"
  - 2006 国家"十一五"规划 — 首提 基本公共服务均等化
  - 2014 城乡居民养老保险整合
  - 2016 城乡居民基本医疗保险整合
  - 2021 《基本公共服务标准》
  - 2023 数据: 全国社保卡 13.68亿人 (96.8%普及率), 各级各类学校 49.83万所, 在校生 2.91亿人; 学前教育毛入园率 91.1%, 初中毛入学率 100%, 高等教育毛入学率 60.2%; 卫生机构 107万个+, 床位 1017万张+, 卫生人员 1523万人+; 人均预期寿命 78.6岁; 孕产妇死亡率 15.1/10万; 婴儿死亡率 4.5‰
  - 全国社保支出占GDP ~13%
  - 2011 维稳经费首次超国防开支
  - 1999 城市最低生活保障 / 2007 农村最低生活保障
  - 三金工程 (金保/金税/金卫)
  - "西部大开发", 一带一路, 青藏铁路, 川藏公路, 村村通工程
  - 棚户区改造 2008+
  - "互联网+政务服务" / 一网通办
- **Cross-课题 connections:** 经济 (政府市场关系, 五年规划), 社会 (民生, 阶层固化, 风险社会), 课题一 (改革开放转型). Foundation for 回应民众诉求 (next lecture).
- **Maps to 集锦 question(s):** Q15 (公共服务能力)
- **High-leverage vocabulary:** 服务型政府, 发展型政府, 全能主义, 政治稳定, 政绩合法性, 公共服务均等化, 4E (经济性效率性效果性公平性), 以人为本, 互联网+政务服务, 一网通办, 风险社会

### 梁 第2课题第4讲：回应民众诉求能力
- **Source:** 梁
- **Lecture position:** 第4讲
- **Sub-topic:** 回应民众诉求 / 政府回应能力 (Government Responsiveness)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **回应型政府 (Responsive government):** Government with citizen-need orientation, taking active flexible measures to respond to interest demands, broad interaction, timely effective response to public demands. The opposite of authoritarian/absolute rule. Key measure: 主动性, 速度, 可信度, 清晰度, 持续性.
  - **塔西佗陷阱 (Tacitus Trap):** When a government loses basic public trust, no matter what it does or says, public will not interpret it as well-intentioned. Critical warning concept — once trust collapses, legitimacy crisis irreversible.
  - **风险社会 (Risk Society):** Beck 1986 《风险社会》. In late-modern industrial society, core problem shifts from "how to distribute wealth" to "how to manage human-made unforeseeable systemic risks". Increases citizen demands and unrest unless governance is responsive.
  - **群体性事件 (Mass incidents):** Collective actions involving 数十-上千 participants triggered by specific grievances (拆迁, 环境, 工资). Key features: 突发性, 集中诉求, 矛盾激烈, 多种组织形式. Driver = 未被有效回应的民众诉求. Government responsiveness ↑ = mass incidents ↓.
  - **亨廷顿: 制度化回应 (Huntington's institutionalized response thesis):** Core problem is not freedom but order. Demands must be channeled through institutional means (parties, parliaments, courts, bureaucracy). Without institutional capacity, populist response from street pressure destroys political stability. "动员而无制度" leads to 过载/崩溃.
  - **政治信任 / 政府公信力 (Political trust / Government credibility):** Foundation of legitimacy. Most direct source = active timely accurate response to public demands.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** As Chinese society enters 风险社会 with multiplying complex demands (利益, 权利, 情感, 公共政策), traditional unilateral management fails. Response capacity is now the decisive variable for political stability + governance modernization. China deploys multi-channel response system: 人大/政协/信访/基层自治 + 互联网+政务服务 + 网格化管理 + 12345热线 + 接诉即办 + 干部下访 + 政府购买服务. Yet major gaps: 权责不清, 形式主义, 数字鸿沟, 城乡资源不均, 反馈闭环缺失.
- **Specific data / cases / years:**
  - 1986 Beck 《风险社会》
  - 2014 国家信访局 网上信访 platform
  - 2022 《信访工作条例》正式施行
  - 2024 全国政协 8000+提案
  - 2023 两会建议提案 办复率 99.8%
  - 北京 接诉即办 mechanism
  - 12345 政务服务热线
  - "随申办" "粤省事" "浙里办" platforms
  - 国务院 "互联网+督查" 平台 / "我向总理说句话"
  - 上海一网通办 — 90%+ 高频事项, 2022 年访问量 10亿+次, 95%+事项 "最多跑一次" or "零跑动"
  - 江苏无锡智慧网格 / 贵州干部驻村工程
  - 北京接诉即办 (12345热线 闭环反馈)
  - 浙江最多跑一次
- **Cross-课题 connections:** 经济 (政府市场边界), 社会 (风险社会, 群体性事件), 反腐 (问责制), 课题三 信任 (塔西佗陷阱).
- **Maps to 集锦 question(s):** Q16 (回应民众诉求)
- **High-leverage vocabulary:** 回应型政府, 塔西佗陷阱, 风险社会, 群体性事件, 政府公信力, 接诉即办, 一网通办, 互联网+政务服务, 12345热线, 网格化管理, 干部下访, 民有所呼政有所应, 数字鸿沟, 制度化回应, 亨廷顿, 主权在民

### 梁 第2课题第5讲：党和国家机构改革与执政能力
- **Source:** 梁
- **Lecture position:** 第5讲
- **Sub-topic:** 党和国家机构改革 / 执政能力 (Party-State Institutional Reform / Governance Capacity)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **执政能力 (Governance/Ruling capacity):** Comprehensive capacity of ruling party to lead nation, manage society, drive development, maintain stability, respond to social demands. Comprises: 公共服务能力 / 危机应对能力 / 制度创新与治理现代化能力 / 国际战略与全球治理能力 / 科学决策能力 / 组织执行能力 / 社会动员能力. Core test of long-term ruling.
  - **以党代政 vs 以党领政 vs 党政分工 (Party substituting government vs Party leading government vs Party-government division of labor):** 以党代政 = Party directly does government's job; 以党领政 = Party guides via political leadership only; 党政分工 = Xi's evolved formula (NOT 党政分开 anymore — division of labor only, not separation of power). "只有党政分工，没有党政分开".
  - **法治政府 / 阳光政府 / 有限政府 / 责任政府 / 有为政府 (Five government types):** 法治政府 = constitutional rule, "权力依法设定、依法行使、依法受限". 阳光政府 = political transparency. 有限政府 = limited by law (Western-style). 责任政府 = accountable. 有为政府 = active where market fails, but limited by law (Chinese formulation distinguishing from 全能政府).
  - **权力清单 (Power list):** 法无授权不可为. Each government office must publicly list authorized powers. "把权力关进笼子" mechanism.
  - **三权分立: 决策权 + 执行权 + 监察权 (Three-power separation under Party leadership):** From 18大-19大 most historic institutional restructure. 决策权 = Party committees + 议事决策机构. 执行权 = government departments. 监察权 = National Supervision Commission (国家监察委员会) + 纪委合署办公. NOT Western three-branch separation — all under unified Party leadership.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** China's institutional reform has gone through 4 phases. (1) Deng/赵紫阳 (1979-1992): 党政分开 attempt; (2) 江朱 (1993-2002): 市场化转型, 1998 朱镕基 reform — most drastic since 1949 (国务院组成部门 40→29); (3) 胡温 (2003-2012): 大部制改革, 2008 reform integrating ministries; (4) 习 (2013+): consolidation under Party center, 2018 国家监察委员会 created. Major shift since 18大: leadership migration FROM 国务院 TO 中共中央. Bureaucracy size trajectory shows reduction then re-expansion. Risks: 以党领政 → 以党代政; 监察权扩张 → 三权失衡; 干部 risk-aversion ("宁可不作为").
- **Specific data / cases / years:**
  - 国务院组成部门 reduction: 52→42 (1982), 45→41 (1988), 42→41 (1993), 40→29 (1998), 29→28 (2003), 28→27 (2008), 27→25 (2013); 2018 -8 正部级 / -7 副部级
  - 1998 朱镕基 reform — 国务院机关 3.3万→1.6万 ("拆庙赶和尚")
  - 1980 邓 《党和国家领导制度的改革》
  - 1986 邓 "首先是党政要分开"
  - 1989 6.4 — reverses 党政分开
  - 1992 改革 turn to 国务院 leadership of reform
  - 1994 分税制改革 (朱镕基)
  - 1998 国务院机构改革 — most drastic
  - 1999 "依法治国" written into 宪法
  - 2003 SARS — 服务型政府 push
  - 2008 大部制改革 — 工业和信息化部, 人力资源和社会保障部, 交通运输部, 住房和城乡建设部
  - 2008 《政府信息公开条例》
  - 2013 改革主导权 from 国务院 to 中共中央
  - 2017 19大 《关于深化党和国家机构改革的决定》— first integrated 党+国家 reform
  - 2018 国家监察委员会, 国家市场监督管理总局, 应急管理部, 自然资源部, 生态环境部, 中央依法治国委员会, 中央财经委员会, 中央外事工作委员会
  - "九龙治水" problem

### 梁 第2课题第6讲：中国的中央与地方关系
- **Source:** 梁
- **Lecture position:** 第6讲
- **Sub-topic:** 中央与地方关系 (Central-Local Relations)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **大国善治 (Good governance of a vast nation):** China's vast territory + huge population + regional differences make direct central management impossible. Local governments are extension of central authority + flexibility for 因地制宜 + means to maintain stability. Without local governments, central cannot achieve 大一统 governance.
  - **条条 vs 块块 (Vertical lines vs Horizontal blocks):** Two competing organizational principles. 条条 = vertical functional control (ministry → provincial bureau → city bureau, 上下对口, 一竿子插到底, 垂直管理). 块块 = horizontal territorial control (province governs all sectors in its territory). Reform oscillates between these.
  - **分税制 (Tax-sharing system):** 1994 朱镕基 reform. Splits taxes into 国税 / 地税 / 共享税. 增值税 75%中央/25%地方; 资源税 (海洋石油 to center, others to local); 证券交易税 50/50. RESULT: central power 财权 ↑ but 事权 ↓; local 事权 ↑ but 财权 ↓. Created "央: 权大责小, 地: 权小责大" mismatch + "上级请客、下级埋单".
  - **晋升锦标赛 (Promotion tournament):** Brand by 周黎安. Local cadres compete on measurable indicator (GDP) for promotion under unified central HR control. Drives local development competition + investment-driven industrialization. Created 政绩 = 升迁 incentive structure.
  - **碎片化威权主义 (Fragmented Authoritarianism):** Lieberthal-Oksenberg framework. China is 威权主义 (centralized authority) but 实际运作 fragmented. 条条 (ministry) + 块块 (territory) crosscut, creating diffuse power. Central-local relationship features 讨价还价 (bargaining), 政策传导层层博弈, 信息不对称 + 执行偏差.
  - **(bonus) 上有政策, 下有对策 (Policies from above, countermeasures from below):** Standard mode of central-local 博弈. Local government 选择性执行 / 变相执行 / 附加性执行 — e.g., 唐山 2017 ordered to close 19 高炉 but built bigger steel plant.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** China's central-local relationship is the "总开关" of governance. Stuck in "放-乱-收-死" loop because 宪法 lacks clear division. Phases: (1) Mao (1949-1978) extreme centralization 条条专政; (2) Deng (1978-1990s early) 放权让利 — 1980 财税权下放, 1982 立法权下放, 1984 投资权下放 → 中央财政占GDP从1980年29%降到1992年12% → 诸侯经济; (3) 1994+ 选择性集权 via 分税制 + 垂直管理 — central regains political and fiscal control. Outcome: central holds 财权 + 人事权 (干部任免), local holds 事权 — produces 央地博弈 but central retains overwhelming advantage. 地方 has dual role: 国家公共利益维护者 + 自身利益追逐者.

### 沈 T3W3：以意识形态及政绩为基础的合法性
- **Source:** 沈
- **Lecture position:** T3W3 (Topic 3 Week 3)
- **Sub-topic:** 政权合法性 / 意识形态合法性 / 政绩合法性 (Legitimacy: ideological + performance)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **政权合法性 (Political legitimacy):** People's belief in government's right to rule. Subject's recognition of ruler's legitimacy = prerequisite for effective governance. Three classical types (Weber): 传统型 (Traditional), 魅力型 (Charismatic), 法理型 (Legal-rational). Modern Chinese formulation: 意识形态合法性 + 政绩合法性 + 制度合法性. Two theorists: Max Weber (people's recognition of authority), Habermas (process by which authority gains legitimacy must be scientific/democratic).
  - **天命观 (Mandate of Heaven):** Originated in early 周朝. Heaven授命 — small 周 conquered larger 商 because 周 received divine mandate. Key insight: ruler's actions/virtue can influence Heaven's will. Foundation of 中国古代 political legitimacy theory.
  - **政绩合法性 (Performance legitimacy):** Legitimacy derived from regime's economic performance + moral achievements + capacity to defend territory. China since 改革开放 has heavily relied on this. Subdivides into: 经济绩效 + 社会绩效 + 政治绩效.
  - **政绩困局/亨廷顿陷阱 (Performance dilemma / Huntington trap):** Authoritarian regimes (一党体制, 军政, 个人独裁) have ONLY 政绩合法性, lack 程序合法性. Result: bad performance → lose legitimacy; good performance → ALSO lose legitimacy (development promotes democracy, weakens monolithic ideology). 亨廷顿 framework — used to predict CCP eventual legitimacy crisis.
  - **意识形态合法性 (Ideological legitimacy):** Regime legitimacy derived from value system (tradition, religion, political ideals). 中共 ideological evolution: 马列主义/毛泽东思想 (Mao era) → 改革开放 as new ideology (Deng) → 三个代表 (江, 2000) → 科学发展观 (胡, 2003) + 和谐社会 (2004) → 中国梦 (习, 2012) + 社会主义核心价值观 + 四个全面.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** CCP rules without electoral or rule-of-law legitimacy → must rely on twin pillars 意识形态 + 政绩. Both are now under pressure: ideology weakened by 全球化 + 自媒体 + 社会思潮多元化 + corruption; performance weakened by 经济新常态 (2019 GDP 6.1% — lowest in 25 years), inequality (基尼系数 0.474 in 2018 — exceeds warning line), corruption, 地方债务. CCP responses: 强力反腐, 完善社保, 区域发展, 供给侧改革, 经济结构转型, 中国梦 narrative. The lecture explicitly poses this as the central legitimacy crisis question, invoking the "塔西佗陷阱" and 亨廷顿 困局.

  - 中国人均GDP 1978: 381元 → 2019: ~7万元 (183倍 in 40 years)

  - 70% 民众认为权力腐败是社会不公主因 (社科院调查)
  - 中国梦提出 2012
  - 三个代表 2000 (江)
  - 科学发展观 2003 / 和谐社会 2004 (胡)
  - 四个全面: 全面建成小康社会 + 全面深化改革 + 全面依法治国 + 全面从严治党

### 沈 T3W5：贪腐与反腐
- **Source:** 沈
- **Lecture position:** T3W5
- **Sub-topic:** 贪污腐败 / 反腐 (Corruption & Anti-Corruption)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **贪污 vs 腐败 (Tanwu vs Fubai):** 贪污 = improper means to gain personal profit causing harm to others (HK 廉政公署 《反贪法例》). 腐败 = abuse of public office for private gain (World Bank) / abuse of entrusted power (Transparency International). 政治腐败 (officials abusing power) vs 经济腐败 (managers abusing position).
  - **阿克顿定律 (Acton's law):** "Power corrupts, absolute power corrupts absolutely." Foundational diagnosis of why China's centralized system breeds corruption. Lord Acton 1834-1902.
  - **塔西佗陷阱 (Tacitus Trap) - corruption application:** When citizens believe corruption is endemic, government statements become disbelieved regardless of content. Corruption directly drives this trust collapse.
  - **权力反腐 vs 制度反腐 (Power-based vs Institution-based anti-corruption):** Distinction between top-down anti-graft campaigns (权力反腐 — short-term, unstable, 治标不治本) and structural reforms putting "权力关进制度的笼子里" (制度反腐). 习 transition from 权力反腐 → 制度反腐 still incomplete; "一把手" still has overwhelming power.
  - **国家监察委员会 (National Supervision Commission):** 2018 整合 党纪检 + 国家监察 — 与中纪委合署办公. Created via 监察法 + 宪法修正案. Forms part of 决策/执行/监察 三权分工.
- **Core argument / mechanism:** Corruption is systemic in China due to 制度建设缺陷 (no 三权分立, weak 司法独立, 党政不分), 经济高速发展 (rents to extract), 社会文化因素 (情礼法治国, 关系文化, 权力寻租). 习 anti-corruption: "无禁区、全覆盖、零容忍", "重遏制、强高压、长震慑", 受贿行贿一起查, "打虎拍蝇猎狐打伞破网". Successes: ~500万 punished, governance capacity strengthened, legitimacy boosted, citizens approve. Critiques: still 运动式反腐, used to remove rivals (扫除政敌, 终身连任 motive), causes 不敢作为, 民众参与不健全, 跨境 cooperation limited. Without 法治 + 独立监察 + 媒体监督, corruption cannot be structurally solved.
- **Specific data / cases / years:**
  - 2008 《政府信息公开条例》/ 《政务公开工作要点》
  - 习反腐 2013 起 — 从严治党, 老虎苍蝇一起打
  - 八项规定 + 反四风
  - 不敢腐、不能腐、不想腐
  - 落马大老虎: 周永康 (习时期), 薄熙来 (习时期), 徐才厚 (习时期), 陈良宇 (胡时期), 陈希同/王宝森 (江时期)
  - ~500万人 受不同程度处罚 (BBC估计)
  - 2018 监察法 + 国家监察委员会 + 宪法修正案
  - "天网2018" 国际追逃
  - 2019上半年: 11 中管干部 + 225 厅局级 落马
  - 70% 民众认为腐败=社会不公主因 (社科院)
  - 全球清廉指数 (CPI) — 习反腐期间反而倒退 (柳金财 2021)
  - 中国共产党党内监督条例
  - 关键 reform laws: 《中华人民共和国法官法》, 《检察官法》, 《人民警察法》
  - 中央纪委国家监委举报网站

### 沈 T3W7：少数民族的问题与管理
- **Source:** 沈

- **Sub-topic:** 少数民族 (Ethnic Minorities)
- **Key concepts (5):**
  - **民族区域自治制度 (Regional ethnic autonomy):** Under unified national leadership, areas where ethnic minorities concentrate establish self-governing organs. 自治区 (= 省 level), 自治州 (= 地级市 level), 自治县 (= 县 level). Established 1984 《民族区域自治法》. 44 of 55 minority groups have autonomous areas. >70% of minority population lives in autonomous areas.

  - **三股势力 (Three forces of evil):** 民族分裂势力 + 宗教极端势力 + 暴力恐怖势力. Frame for understanding 新疆 / 西藏 challenges. Targets: 东突 (East Turkestan), Tibet independence movements.

Critique: separates without integrating, autonomy is in name only ("汉族党委书记的领导监督下的自治"), 维稳成本 too high (2019 维稳费 1.4万亿 > 国防费).

## 3. 5 Critical Political Concepts — Deep Dive (3-line essay-ready definitions)

### A. 党国体制 (Party-state system)
党国体制是为解决中国权力分散化问题, 以中共为核心进行国家治理的一种组织化手段, 党通过其庞大严密的组织网络将国家、社会纳入自己的组织系统, 形成政党与国家的融合、政治和经济同构的一体化格局。其最典型表现是党把国家机关的权力统一于自身, 构成相当于又超越了国家组织的社会公共权力, 实现党对国家和社会的全面控制 ("党是领导一切的")。其核心优势是 "集中力量办大事" 与 "一张蓝图绘到底" 的政策连续性, 但也带来 "九龙治水" / "单点故障" / 制度僵化 / 法治弱化的根本性弊端。

### B. 党主法治 (Party-led rule of law)
党主法治是西方学者对中国 "全面依法治国" 的批判性概括 — 中国 "党的领导是社会主义法治最根本的保证" (18届4中全会), 党凌驾于法律之上, 通过纪委和党的领袖治党, 而非依靠司法独立和民主宪政。中国学者更倾向 "党导法治" 表述, 强调党通过党规与国法双重体系治国, 但仍 "将党关进党规的钢笼子里"。批评指出: 党权凌驾法权使法律沦为政治遮羞布, 党不能干预司法审判否则依法治国就沦为空谈, 而中国现实是 "东西南北中, 党领导一切", 司法改革只能是 "技术性治理" 的小步慢跑, 司法独立在党国体制下是禁区。

### C. 政绩合法性 (Performance legitimacy)
政绩合法性指统治者通过行使公共权力、履行自身职能、促进经济发展、社会进步、实现公民福祉的能力水平和效果获得人民认可与赞同, 进而获得的合法性资源 (改革开放以来中共的核心合法性来源)。但亨廷顿警告"政绩困局": 威权政府只有政绩合法性缺乏程序合法性, 不能有好政绩→失去合法性, 政绩太好→也失去合法性 (经济发展会促进民主). 当前中国面临经济新常态 (2019 GDP 6.1% 25年最低)、基尼系数 0.474、地方债务、腐败、公民民主意识觉醒等多重挑战, 单靠政绩已无法可持续维持合法性, 必须 "意识形态合法性 + 政绩合法性 + 制度合法性" 三足鼎立。

### D. 央地博弈 (Central-local bargaining)
央地博弈是中国国家治理体系最复杂的纵向权力配置问题 — 1994 朱镕基 分税制改革后, 地方政府兼具 "国家公共利益维护者" + "自身利益追逐者" 双重角色, 与中央在经济权限/政策执行上形成 "上有政策, 下有对策" 的博弈结构。地方拥有 "事权" 但缺 "财权" (央地支出比 中央~15% 地方~85%), 依赖土地财政与转移支付, 形成 GDP 锦标赛驱动下的工业化/城镇化, 但也造成地方债务、地方保护主义、政策变形执行 (如2017唐山关19高炉建大钢厂)。中央通过垂直管理 + 党管干部 + 选择性集权保持压倒性优势, 但 "碎片化威权主义" (Lieberthal) 仍是体制常态; 真正的优化路径是制定 央地关系基本法, 实现 权力法治化划分 + 激励相容机制。

### E. 塔西佗陷阱 (Tacitus Trap)
塔西佗陷阱指一个政府或政权由于丧失了人民对它的基本信任, 无论它做什么或说什么, 民众都不会认为是善意的 — 这是政治信任崩溃的临界点, 跨过即陷入合法性危机不可逆。在中国, 此概念被广泛用于诊断 (1) 腐败问题: 官员公权私用与利益链条加剧官民对立, 70%民众视腐败为社会不公主因; (2) 公共服务短板: 民生需求得不到回应造成 "维稳越维越不稳" 的怪圈; (3) 信息不对称: 政务公开不足、司法不公正使民众默认政府说谎。打破塔西佗陷阱的根本路径: 推动政府信息公开 (阳光政府)、回应民众诉求 (回应型政府)、实现司法独立与法治化、让权力在阳光下运行 — 否则即使强力反腐, 民众仍可解读为"运动式反腐+扫除政敌"。
