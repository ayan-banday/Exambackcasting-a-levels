## 2. The grading reality you're encoding for

Before you read any slide, encode these three facts. They determine what counts as "high-logic" content.

### 2.1 The automatic cap rule (this is the #1 logic point in all of CSC)

From 沈老师 marking rubric (source: `评分标准与答题策略-reference.md` §1.7):

| Missing element | Max grade |
|---|---|
| No cross-课题 (跨范畴) | 18/30 (Tier 3) |
| No balanced argument (平衡论述) | 18/30 (Tier 3) |
| Both missing | 15/30 (Tier 3 low) |

What this means for your study filter: a concept is "high-logic" in CSC if and only if it does at least one of the following:
- Crosses cleanly into another 课题
- Generates a real 反方 (counter-argument), not a strawman
- Provides the comparison axis for 比较分析 (the synthesis step)

If a concept does none of these, it's low-logic for CSC, regardless of how interesting or important the real-world topic is. Skip it.

### 2.2 The Bloom level you're being tested at

P2 问答题 tests AO1 (knowledge application) + AO2 (analysis + evaluation). It does NOT test pure recall.

Bloom verb evidence from the rubric:
- Tier 5 (25–30): "分析" dominant, "评价" critical, "综合不同观点"
- Tier 4 (19–24): "分析 = 描述" (balanced)
- Tier 3 (13–18): "述多于论" (description outweighs analysis)
- Tier 2 (7–12): off-target, fragmented

Translation: CSC P2 lives at Bloom 4 (Analyze) and Bloom 5 (Evaluate). Tier 5 also wants Bloom 6 (Create — generate clear judgement and synthesis).

Justin Sung's rule says: if a topic is 90% low-order and 10% high-order, study only low-order. CSC is the inverse — 90% high-order, 10% scaffolding facts. Apply the inverse rule: skip pure facts unless they unlock an analysis frame.

### 2.3 The 5-part essay structure every Q33–Q42 follows

From `评分标准与答题策略-reference.md` §1.11:

1. **界定关键词、表明立场** (define key terms, declare position)
2. **立场分析** (3 angles supporting your stand)
3. **平衡分析** (2 counter-points)
4. **比较分析 / 程度判断** (refute counter-points, reaffirm using 影响范围 / 程度 / 问题性质 / 解决难度)
5. **批判性总结**

Each paragraph internally = PEEL (论点 → 论据 → 论证 → 链接).

Every 外交 essay in Q33–Q42 follows this exact skeleton. Once you encode the skeleton + the 7 anchors, you can write any 外交 essay. The skeleton is the scaffolding. The anchors are the swappable contents.

## 4. The 7 highest-logic concept anchors for 外交

These are the concepts that explain the most other concepts in the topic. Each one links to at least 2 other 课题. Each one shows up in ≥3 of the Q33–Q42 essays. These are your ONLY targets for Day 1.

### Anchor 1 — 国家核心利益 (the master anchor)

The 2011 国新办《和平发展白皮书》list: 国家主权 / 国家安全 / 领土完整 / 国家统一 / 政治制度与社会大局稳定 / 经济社会可持续发展的基本保障.

Why it's #1 logic: every single Q33–Q42 essay uses this list as its 评价 axis. Without this anchor, no judgement can be made about "在多大程度上维护了核心利益". With it, every essay collapses into "rank these interests, show how X impacts them in different directions".

Cross-课题 links:
- 政治: 政治制度稳定 = 课题二 全部
- 经济: 经济可持续发展 = 课题一 全部
- 社会: 社会大局稳定 = 课题三 全部

This anchor IS the cross-课题 bridge for 外交. Encode it first.

### Anchor 2 — 总体国家安全观 (2014)

习近平 framework: 政治 / 国土 / 军事 / 经济 / 文化 / 社会 / 科技 / 网络 / 生态 / 资源 / 核 — 11 domains of security.

Why high-logic: turns every 双边关系 question into a multi-domain evaluation. "中美关系" is no longer just "trade" — it becomes "trade vs tech vs cyber vs geopolitical security". Forces breadth into balanced argument.

Cross-课题 links:
- 政治: 国家治理 → 政治安全
- 经济: 科技安全 → 卡脖子 → 课题一 服务业开放
- 社会: 社会安全 → 课题三 群体性事件

### Anchor 3 — 韬光养晦 → 奋发有为 (policy evolution timeline)

Mao 生存外交 (1949–78) → Deng 韬光养晦 + 和平与发展 (1979–2012) → Xi 奋发有为 + 新型大国关系 + 一带一路 + 人类命运共同体 (2013–).

Why high-logic: lets you contextualize ANY current policy as "shift from defensive to assertive". Generates 平衡 by default — every shift creates winners (assertive achievements) and losers (loss of low-profile flexibility).

Cross-课题 links:
- 政治: 习时代 党管外交 → 课题二 党国体制
- 经济: 走出去 + 一带一路 → 课题一 经济驱动力

### Anchor 4 — 修昔底德陷阱 (Thucydides Trap)

The structural rising-power vs status-quo-power tension. Frames 中美关系 entirely.

Why high-logic: provides the BOTH-sides analytical frame for 中美 questions. You can argue "China's rise inevitably triggers US containment" AND "skillful diplomacy avoids the trap" — instant balanced argument.

Cross-课题: technically single-domain, but it's the connective tissue for ALL 中美 essays.

### Anchor 5 — 政经分离 (political-economic decoupling)

The principle: economic cooperation and political/security conflict run in parallel without one resolving the other. Best illustrated by 中-亚细安 ("经济靠中国，安全靠美国") and 中日 (huge trade, deep security suspicion).

Why high-logic: explains the entire 平衡 structure of Q35, Q37, Q38, Q41. Lets you say "yes, X benefits China economically BUT does not resolve the underlying security tension, therefore..."

Cross-课题 links:
- 经济: 贸易 vs 投资 vs 制裁
- 政治: 主权 vs 经济利益 trade-off (e.g. 立陶宛 case)

### Anchor 6 — 三股势力 (terrorism / separatism / religious extremism)

The official framing of non-traditional security threats, especially 新疆 / 西藏 / Central Asia.

Why high-logic: turns 周边外交 + 上合组织 + 反恐合作 questions into structured arguments. Provides the 跨课题 bridge from 外交 → 课题二 (少数民族问题) → 课题三 (社会稳定).

Cross-课题 links:
- 政治: 少数民族管理 (课题二 第7讲 沈)
- 社会: 宗教复兴 + 价值观多元化 (课题三)

### Anchor 7 — 人类命运共同体 / 一带一路 / 新型大国关系 (the trio)

The current diplomatic doctrine, deployed by Xi from 2013 onwards as the "post-韬光养晦" Chinese contribution to global governance.

Why high-logic: every 全球治理 question (Q34, Q36, Q39, Q40, Q42) uses one of these three. They generate both 立场 ("China provides global public goods") AND 平衡 ("debt trap, neo-colonialism critique").

Cross-课题 links:
- 经济: 一带一路 = 经济 走出去 / 服务业开放
- 政治: 党管外交 + 顶层设计
- 环境: 绿色一带一路 + 南南合作

## 8. The filter question — apply on every paragraph

Justin's exact filter question, used on every slide or every KB paragraph:

> "How valuable is this piece of information at helping me explain other things in the topic? How helpful is it at making sense of multiple other things in the topic?"

If high → encode it. Write it on an anchor card.
If low → skip. Don't read in detail. Don't take notes.

You're making a guess on logic points. You won't be perfectly accurate. That's fine — you don't have time to be perfectly accurate, and your gut estimate will be ~80% right.

### CSC-specific filter sub-questions

These translate Justin's general filter into CSC's grading reality:

- Does this concept give me a new 评价 axis I haven't seen?
- Does this concept bridge 外交 to 经济/政治/社会? (Cross-课题 is the highest-leverage logic point in CSC because of the CAPS rule.)
- Does this concept generate a 反方 argument I couldn't otherwise make?
- Does this concept change the 立场 I'd take on a Q33–Q42 question?

If the answer to all four is no, the slide is decoration. Skip.

### Permission to go back

If you're 30 minutes into a later section and realize something you skipped earlier is actually relevant to what you're now encoding — go back and read it. Justin's explicit: "that's fine, you can always just go back and learn it now."

This permission is psychologically important. It lets you skip aggressively without anxiety about missing something. The cost of going back once is small. The cost of reading everything cautiously is huge.
