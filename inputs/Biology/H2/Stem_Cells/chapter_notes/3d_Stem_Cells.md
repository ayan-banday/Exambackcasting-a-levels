- HO/LV
    1. Compare totipotent, pluripotent, and multipotent stem cells in terms of their developmental potential. Which type is most critical for the earliest stages of organismal development, and why would the absence of this potency be detrimental?
    2. Embryonic stem cells and blood stem cells both have self-renewal capacity, but their functions differ. Which plays a more vital role in long-term organismal survival, and how would the loss of each specifically impact growth versus tissue maintenance?
    3. Consider the roles of lymphoid and myeloid stem cells in the immune system. If one lineage were completely lost, which would have a more severe effect on the body’s defense mechanisms, and why?
    4. External signals (e.g., microenvironment cues) and internal signals (e.g., gene expression) both guide stem cell differentiation. Which system of regulation is more indispensable for proper specialization, and how would disrupting one alter stem cell behavior?
    5. Given that stem cells can be directed for therapeutic use (e.g., bone marrow transplants, skin grafts), which property—self-renewal or potency—is more important for their success in medical treatments, and how would losing the less critical property affect clinical outcomes?
