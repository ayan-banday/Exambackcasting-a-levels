## Headline calibration rules (top 5)

1. **Paper format locked at 9744:** P1 30m/30Q/1h MCQ; P2 75-100m/~10Q/2h structured; P3 75m (Sec A 50m structured + Sec B 25m essay choice); P4 55m/3Q/2.5h practical (Ethan's October prep).

2. **P3 Q1 MUST integrate ≥3 syllabus topics.** Every deep-read JC follows this. Single-topic anchors have disappeared from the 9744 era. Essay (a)+(b) both must demand BREADTH not depth on single molecule — examiners explicitly punish over-depth.

3. **P1 topic balance per 30 MCQs:** 6 Cell+Biomol / 5 DNA+CC / 5 Virus+Bacteria / 4 Mendelian / 4 Energy / 3 Diversity+Evol / 3 Disease+Immunity. Stem variety quota: ≥8 combination + 6 row-matching + 4 data-interp + 2 reverse + 2 calc. ~60% figure-stimulus, ~70% application/data-interp level.

4. **P2 mandatory per paper:** chi-squared dihybrid genetics + figure interpretation + genetic diagram + "Suggest" AO3 + calculation. Command word distribution per 10-mark Q: 2 state / 3 describe / 3 explain / 2 suggest.

5. **Use real biological data and verbatim Cambridge stems:** FoxO1, BRCA1/2, EGFR, GBM, Hurler syndrome, HIV, HPV+cervical cancer, lac/gal operons, glycoprotein 9-point synthesis scheme. Flag and skip 9648-only topics (transgenic plants, plant tissue culture, banana vaccines, STAP cells fraud, pBR322 ampR/tetR).
