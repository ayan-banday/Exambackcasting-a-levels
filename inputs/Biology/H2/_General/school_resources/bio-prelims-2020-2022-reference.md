### 2022 · HCI · P3 Mark Scheme conventions (sampled):
- Format: bulleted points separated by `;` (semicolon ends each marking point)
- `ORA` = Or Reverse Argument (mark accepted if reverse logic given)
- `AW` = Alternative Wording acceptable
- `ref. to` = brief acknowledgment phrase acceptable
- `Max` cap (e.g., "4 max") - even if candidate gives 6 points, only 4 marks max
- "Reject" / "R" specifies wrong answers explicitly forbidden
- "Accept" / "A" gives alternative valid wordings
- "Ignore" = neutral, doesn't lose marks but doesn't gain
- Section B essays use **Levels of Response** marking: L1 (1-7), L2 (8-11), L3 (12-15) for 15-mark questions

### MCQ patterns (P1)

- **30 questions, 1 hour, 30 marks** (every JC follows this exactly).
- Mix: ~60-70% single-best-answer A/B/C/D, ~30-40% multi-statement "1, 2, 3, 4 - which combination correct"
- Diagram-based stems extremely common: EM micrographs, pedigrees, flowcharts of metabolism, graphs
- Calculation-required: scale magnification, allele frequency, energy yield, biomass percent change
- **Authentic 9744 trap features**: distractor options that are "factually true but irrelevant", "true for prokaryote, false for eukaryote (or vice versa)", "true for one virus but generalised wrongly"
- Pedigree analysis (X-linked, autosomal recessive/dominant, novel mutation) appears in EVERY paper
- Drosophila genetics + epistasis appears 70% of papers

### Structured Q patterns (P2)

- **75 marks, 2 hours, 8-11 questions** (most settle at 9-10 questions averaging 8-10 marks each)
- HCI follows 10×10 = some 10-mark questions; others split 9, 13, 11
- **Mark distribution per question**: 1m (state/name), 2-3m (describe one structure/process), 3-5m (explain mechanism), 5-8m (discuss/integrate multiple parts)
- **Data analysis frequency**: 80-90% of Section A questions are figure/table-based - graph interpretation, table comparison, calculation from raw data
- **Chi-squared question almost guaranteed** in every paper - includes:
  - State null hypothesis [1]
  - Complete observed/expected table [varies]
  - Calculate χ² value [3-4]
  - Compare with critical value at p=0.05 [1]
  - State conclusion linked to null hypothesis [1-2]
- **Genetic diagram**: full P→F1→F2 with gametes appears in every paper
- Common command word frequencies: "Explain" (most common), "Describe", "Suggest" (heavy on application), "With reference to Fig X..." (frame >50% Qs), "Compare", "Account for"
- "Suggest" appears in EVERY paper - signals AO3 application beyond syllabus

### Essay patterns (P3)

- **Section A: 50 marks** of 2-3 long structured (usually one 25-35m mega-question + smaller).
- **Section B: 25 marks**, choose 1 of 2 essays, each split (a) 10-15m + (b) 10-15m.
- **Common essay topic pairs (a) + (b) — 2021-2022 trend**:
  - Mitosis vs meiosis comparison + evolution significance
  - Aerobic vs anaerobic respiration + something
  - Eukaryotic gene expression regulation + significance
  - Viruses (structure, variation, evolution) + something
  - Membrane structure/function + something
  - DNA replication / end-replication problem + telomere
  - Immune response (B/T cells, antibodies, vaccines) + something
  - Photosynthesis / photophosphorylation + significance
  - ATP role in living organisms + complementarity/membranes
  - Protein structure-function in [cell signaling / replication / immune] + linked question
- **Essay verbs distribution**: "Describe" (factual narrative, syllabus content), "Explain" (mechanism), "Discuss" (balanced with evaluation), "Outline" (concise structured), "Compare" (similarities AND differences both required)
- Climate change + biodiversity (b) question appears as essay choice frequently
- Cycles in biology + named examples = recurring essay frame

### Practical skills (P4)

- **55 marks, 2.5 hours, ~2 long questions**
- **Q1 always enzyme/biochemistry quantitative**: surface area effects, concentration effects, temperature effects, water potential (mannitol/sucrose gradient)
- **Q2 always observation/measurement**: microscope plan diagrams, biological drawing with magnification, tally charts, biological keys
- **Standard apparatus**: syringes for gas collection, colorimeter, balance, gridded slides
- **Skills tested**: dilution series preparation, plotting graphs (line/bar/scatter), error analysis, identifying anomalies, suggesting improvements, calculating gradient/rate, percentage change, biological drawing standards (no shading, clear label lines, scale bar)
- **Statistical tests within practical**: standard deviation, t-test calculations occasionally
- Apparatus list provided ~7 days before exam (Candidates Information / CI document)
