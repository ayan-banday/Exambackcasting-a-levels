- HO/LV
    
    How would the reliability and specificity of cell signalling change if cells relied solely on second messengers like cAMP and Ca²⁺, without phosphorylation cascades? Which system is ultimately more critical for ensuring accurate cellular responses?
    
    In the regulation of blood glucose, insulin signalling relies on receptor tyrosine kinase (RTK) phosphorylation, while glucagon signalling relies on second messengers such as cAMP. Which pathway would have a more devastating effect on homeostasis if disrupted, and why?
    
    Between the diffusion of second messengers and the sequential steps of phosphorylation cascades, which contributes more significantly to signal amplification, and how would removing one mechanism alter the efficiency of responses like glycogen metabolism?
    
    Second messengers spread signals rapidly but non-specifically, while phosphorylation cascades ensure specificity through ordered kinase activation. If an organism had to sacrifice one property—speed or specificity—which trade-off would have a greater impact on survival?
    
    Diabetes often involves defects in insulin signalling. Considering the roles of both phosphorylation cascades (RTK signalling) and second messengers (glucagon/cAMP pathway), which type of disruption poses the greater long-term threat to glucose homeostasis, and why?
