# CSE — China Studies in Chinese 中国通识 · 9629 · H2 `[OFFICIAL]`

**Priority 1.** Standing: knows close to nothing, isolated facts only. Chinese-language barrier (translate first). Logic-chain marking is opaque. Computer-based (机考).

### How it's tested

| Paper | Type | Marks | Weight | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 案例分析** Case Study | A组 (5 sources, 4 sub-q) 35 + B组 (4 sources, 1 q) 25 | **60** | **40%** | 2h 45m |
| **2 问答题** Essay | A组 1 compulsory (30) + B组 2 of 3 (30 each = 60) | **90** | **60%** | 2h 30m |

P1 A组 sub-marks: Q1(a) 6 · Q1(b) 9 · Q1(c) 8 · Q1(d) 12. Essays on 5-level bands (30/essay). Each case = one core theme (any of the 4).

### What I'm tested on — the 4 themes `[OFFICIAL]`

**Theme 1 — 中国的经济及其可持续发展 (Economy & Sustainable Development)**
- Overall: planned → socialist market transition; growth drivers (reform; trade & investment)
- Challenges: structural (export-reliance, SOE inefficiency, banking debt); regional imbalance; energy & environment (fossil-fuel demand, degradation)
- Strategies: restructuring (domestic demand, SOE reform, open tech/services, banking reform); lagging regions (infrastructure, urbanisation); green development (renewables, protection)
- *Concepts: 经济发展, 经济增长, 经济可持续发展, 经济自由化*

**Theme 2 — 中国的治理及其挑战 (Governance & Challenges)** ← build first
- Overall: party-state system; legitimacy (ideology + performance); central–local relations
- Challenges: state governance (corruption, rule of law, responsiveness, ethnic minorities); governing capacity (govt power, central–local); participation (dissent, online)
- Strategies: anti-corruption, judicial reform, public services, ethnic-minority management; Party/state institutional reform; managing participation (official mechanisms, supervision)
- *Concepts: 政治稳定, 国家治理, 政权合法性, 意识形态*

**Theme 3 — 中国社会及其演变 (Society & Evolution)**
- Transformation: demographics (falling birth rate, rising life expectancy); urbanisation & mobility; structure change (stratification, family)
- Challenges: population (policy, ageing); mobility (income/regional disparity, inequality); civil society (NGOs, movements); pluralised values (religious revival, media vs tradition)
- Strategies: social-governance reform (population policy, welfare, hukou, NGO management); order (ideology, religious & media regulation)
- *Concepts: 社会转型, 社会稳定, 社会治理, 公民社会*

**Theme 4 — 中国的外交及其影响 (Diplomacy & International Relations)**
- Factors: core interests (sovereignty/territory, security, economic, ideology)
- Bilateral: China–US (trade/investment, geopolitics); China–Japan (trade/investment, security); China–ASEAN (regional economic cooperation, security)
- Global governance: world economy (intl economic orgs, multilateral trade); intl security (regional cooperation, UN); environment & climate (intl environmental + climate cooperation)
- *Concepts: 主权, 安全, 冲突, 合作*

### Highest-yield for the 20 days (from the examiner's guide)

1. **Governance** — very high frequency; top nodes 反腐 (case 1x + essay 10x+), 合法性 (2024 specimen). Pre-built logic-chains → best cold-start marks/hour. **Build first.**
2. **Society** — very high frequency; owns the 老龄化 "外来因素" essay trick; leans P2-essay.
3. **Economy** — often the P2 A组 compulsory; rests on the hard 结构性-vs-技术性 axis.
4. **Diplomacy** — reliable but thin model-essay corpus; most memorization, least scaffolding.

# GP — General Paper · 8881 · H1 `[OFFICIAL]`

**Priority 2.** Standing: D/C. Essay gap = structure, not ideas. Compre gap = time management.

| Paper | Marks (Content / Language) | Weight | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 Essay** | 50 (30 / 20) | 50% | 1h 30m |
| **2 Comprehension** | 50 (35 / 15) | 50% | 1h 30m |

- **P1:** 8 questions set, answer **1**, 500–800 words. Cross-disciplinary, conceptual.
- **P2:** 3 passages (~1200 words total), answer all (8–9 questions): short-answer comprehension on Passage 1 (9–11m) · summary on Passage 2 (8m) · cross-passage connections (4–6m) · application question (12m). Language marks (15) on summary + application only.
- **Domains:** Society & Culture · Economics · Politics · Arts & Humanities · Science & Technology · Environment.
- **AOs:** AO1 Critical & Inventive Thinking · AO2 Communication. Bands 1–5 (Content + Language scored separately on P1).

# Bio — Biology · 9477 · H2 only `[OFFICIAL]`

**Triage (strongest, ~60% cold).** Gap-hunt only.

| Paper | Type | Marks | Weight | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | MCQ (30, compulsory) | 30 | 15% | 1h |
| 2 | Structured (all compulsory) | 90 | 30% | 2h |
| 3 | Long structured + free-response | 75 | 35% | 2h |
| 4 | Practical | 50 | 20% | 2h 30m |

`[TPE]` P3 = **2 Jul** (Section A 55m structured + stimulus; Section B 20m, 2 essays choose 1). P4 practical = **30 Jun** (skills MMO/PDO/ACE; enzyme action, food tests, microscopy; practicals 1-4,7,8,11-14 + revision 1,2,3,5).

### What I'm tested on — 4 Core Ideas + 2 Extension `[OFFICIAL]`

*(Official structure uses Core Ideas, not "topics 1-14" — that's the school's chapter split nested inside these.)*
- **Core 1 — The Cell and Biomolecules of Life:** cell theory; membranes & organelles; bacterial cell; biomolecule structures; fluid-mosaic model; enzymes; stem cells
- **Core 2 — Genetics and Inheritance:** DNA structure/function; central dogma; chromatin; genetics of viruses & bacteria; genome organisation; gene-expression control; DNA analysis & genomics; mutations; cell division; chromosome variation; molecular biology of cancer; inheritance, dihybrid crosses, linkage, gene interaction, genotype × environment
- **Core 3 — Energy and Equilibrium:** energy need; photosynthesis; respiration (aerobic/anaerobic); cell signalling & communication
- **Core 4 — Biological Evolution:** species concept; neo-Darwinism; variation/selection/evolution; evidence; classification
- **Extension A — Infectious Diseases:** innate & adaptive immunity; genetic recombination; viral/bacterial infection modes; vaccination & antibiotics
- **Extension B — Impact of Climate Change on Animals and Plants:** human drivers; effects on environment/plants/animals; biodiversity/biomedicine/food supply; dengue spread

`[TPE]` **Not assessed in the mock:** DNA Structure & Replication, Molecular Techniques, Genetics of Viruses, Infectious Diseases. ⚠️ Infectious Diseases *is* Extension A in the full syllabus — confirm it's genuinely excluded for the TPE only.
