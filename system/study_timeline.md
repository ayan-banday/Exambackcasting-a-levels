## 1. The methodology (two regimes)

**Regime 1 — Recognition pass (now → 1 Aug).** First-pass *every* topic to "I've seen this, I know
its shape." NOT mastery. Weekly per-subject **cumulative** tests expose gaps. CORE topics go early so
cumulative testing re-hits them most.

**Regime 2 — Test-driven deep loop (Aug → exams).** The real learning:
1. Sit a **full-length test** for a subject.
2. Score low (~30%) — that's expected and fine.
3. Generate a **deep cluster** on exactly what failed (`spec.md` gap-closure by type).
4. Study that cluster properly → sit the next full test → repeat.

**Learning comes from failing tests, not pre-studying.** Prelims (20 Aug) and returned papers are the
richest gap sources.

**Capacity assumption:** ~3–4 hrs/day. A week of disruption cascades — protect the buffer weeks.
