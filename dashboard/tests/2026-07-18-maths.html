Question 11 [7]
Points \(A\) and \(B\) have position vectors \( \mathbf{a} \) and \( \mathbf{b} \) relative to origin \(O\).
(i) The point \(P\) divides \(AB\) in the ratio \( 2:3 \). Find the position vector of \(P\) in terms of \( \mathbf{a} \) and \( \mathbf{b} \). [2]
(ii) The point \(Q\) is such that \(B\) is the midpoint of \(PQ\). Find the position vector of \(Q\). [2]
(iii) Given now \( \mathbf{a} = 2\mathbf{i} + \mathbf{j} - \mathbf{k} \) and \( \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{i} - \mathbf{j} + 2\mathbf{k} \), find the exact area of triangle \(OAB\). [3]

1.

(i) \(2xy + x^2\frac{dy}{dx} + 3y^2\frac{dy}{dx}=0 \Rightarrow \frac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{-2xy}{x^{2}+3y^{2}}\). (ii) At \((1,2)\): grad \(= \frac{-4}{13}\); tangent \( y - 2 = -\frac{4}{13}(x-1) \), i.e. \( y = -\frac{4}{13}x + \frac{30}{13} \).

2.

\( \ln(1+3x) = 3x - \frac{(3x)^2}{2} + \frac{(3x)^3}{3} - \dots = 3x - \frac{9}{2}x^{2} + 9x^{3} \).

3.

By parts (\(u=\ln x\)): \( \frac{x^{3}}{3}\ln x - \int \frac{x^{3}}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{x}\,dx = \frac{x^{3}}{3}\ln x - \frac{x^{3}}{9} + C \).

4.

Partial fractions: \(\frac{5x-4}{(x-2)(x+1)} = \frac{2}{x-2} + \frac{3}{x+1}\). Integral \(= 2\ln|x-2| + 3\ln|x+1| + C\).

5.

(i) \(\int_0^2 (4-x^2)\,dx = [4x - \frac{x^3}{3}]_0^2 = 8 - \frac{8}{3} = \frac{16}{3}\). (ii) \(V = \pi\int_0^2 (4-x^2)^2 dx = \pi\int_0^2(16-8x^2+x^4)dx = \pi[16x-\frac{8x^3}{3}+\frac{x^5}{5}]_0^2 = \frac{256\pi}{15}\).

6.

Separable: \(\int\frac{1}{y}dy = \int x\,dx \Rightarrow \ln y = \frac{x^2}{2}+C\). \(y=2,x=0\Rightarrow C=\ln 2\). \( y = 2e^{x^{2}/2} \).

7.

(i) \(\int\frac{d\theta}{\theta-20} = -k\int dt \Rightarrow \ln(\theta-20) = -kt + C\); \(\theta=80,t=0\Rightarrow C=\ln 60\), so \(\theta = 20 + 60e^{-kt}\). At \(t=5,\theta=60\): \(40 = 60e^{-5k}\Rightarrow e^{-5k}=\frac{2}{3}\Rightarrow k = \frac{1}{5}\ln\frac{3}{2}\). (ii) \(\theta \to 20\,^\circ\text{C}\) as \(t\to\infty\) (\(e^{-kt}\to 0\)) — the room/surrounding temperature.

8.

(i) \(y = x+1 + \frac{4}{x-1}\); asymptotes \(x=1\) and \(y=x+1\). (ii) \(\frac{dy}{dx}=1-\frac{4}{(x-1)^2}=0\Rightarrow (x-1)^2=4\Rightarrow x=3\) or \(x=-1\); points \((3,6)\) and \((-1,-2)\). (iii) two branches, min \((3,6)\) top-right, max \((-1,-2)\) bottom-left, approaching \(y=x+1\) and \(x=1\).

9.

\(\cos^2 x = \frac{1+\cos 2x}{2}\); \(\int = \frac{x}{2} + \frac{\sin 2x}{4} + C\).

10.

(i) \(\mathbf{a\cdot b}=2-3-4=-5\), \(|\mathbf a|=3\), \(|\mathbf b|=\sqrt{14}\); \(\cos\theta = \frac{-5}{3\sqrt{14}} \Rightarrow \theta = 116.5^\circ\) (1 d.p.). (ii) projection length \(= \frac{|\mathbf{a\cdot b}|}{|\mathbf b|} = \frac{5}{\sqrt{14}} = \frac{5\sqrt{14}}{14}\).

11.

(i) Ratio theorem, \(AP:PB=2:3\): \(\mathbf{p} = \frac{3\mathbf a + 2\mathbf b}{5}\). (ii) \(B\) midpoint of \(PQ \Rightarrow \mathbf q = 2\mathbf b - \mathbf p = \frac{8\mathbf b - 3\mathbf a}{5}\). (iii) \(\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b} = \mathbf i - 5\mathbf j - 3\mathbf k\), \(|\mathbf{a}\times\mathbf{b}| = \sqrt{35}\); area \(=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{35}\).
