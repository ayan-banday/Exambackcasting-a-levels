## The one-line version

The CCP rules without elections or independent courts, so its right to rule depends on **two things working together: 意识形态 (中国特色社会主义) and 政绩 (经济增长 + 公共服务)**. **政绩 is the deeper base** — ideology fills the gap when performance flags. When neither delivers, the system hits **塔西佗陷阱** (trust collapse). Every governance challenge — corruption, central-local friction, weak rule of law, ethnic policy, public participation — is ultimately a stress test of this two-pillar legitimacy.

## How to think about Governance — three layers

### Layer 1 — How power is structured (基础与权力结构)

This is the foundation. Get this layer wrong and every essay below misses the analytical spine.

- **党国体制** — CCP holds exclusive power. Runs the state via 民主集中制 + 四个服从. CCP is a 使命型政党 (mission-type, 郑永年), not 选举型 — legitimacy comes from carrying a historical mission, not from being elected. Evolution: Mao's 一元化 → Deng's failed 党政分开 (1980, reversed after 1989) → 江 党政机构并列 → **Xi: 党政分工 (division of labour, NOT separation)**. The structure enables 集中力量办大事 but creates 单点故障 (single-point-of-failure) risks.

- **政绩合法性 — the spine of all 政治 essays.** Modern Chinese legitimacy has three sources: 意识形态 + 政绩 + 制度. CCP rules without 法理型 legitimacy (Weber) → must over-deliver on 政绩 + 意识形态. **政绩困局 (亨廷顿 trap):** authoritarian regimes only have 政绩合法性. Bad performance → lose legitimacy. Good performance → ALSO eventually lose legitimacy (development drives democratisation). When trust collapses → **塔西佗陷阱** → no statement believed regardless of content.

- **央地关系 — structural friction by design.** **1994 分税制 (朱镕基):** 增值税 75/25, 资源/证券 50/50. Result: 中央 财权大事权小, 地方 权小责大. "上级请客、下级埋单" → locals scrounge for revenue → 土地财政 → LGFV debt. **晋升锦标赛 (周黎安):** local cadres compete on GDP for promotion → 政绩 = 升迁 incentive. **碎片化威权主义 (Lieberthal–Oksenberg):** 威权 in name, fragmented in operation; 条条 (vertical) vs 块块 (horizontal) crosscut → 上有政策, 下有对策.

- **执政能力 + 机构改革 — how the party tries to keep itself effective.** Five government types (法治/阳光/有限/责任/有为) compete in theory; 有为政府 wins in practice (Chinese adaptation distinguishing from Western 有限). **三权分工 (NOT Western 三权分立):** 决策 (Party + 人大) / 执行 (国务院) / 监察 (国监委 + 纪委合署). 2018 institutional reform = the milestone — but reform stays "技术性治理" (technical) because political reform is forbidden territory.

### Layer 2 — Constraints on power (约束权力)

The party knows unchecked power is its biggest internal risk. Two parallel attempts to constrain it — both stuck at the same ceiling: party supremacy can't be challenged.

- **反腐 — Acton's law in action.** 阿克顿定律: "power corrupts, absolute power corrupts absolutely." Xi-era: 不敢腐 (deter) → 不能腐 (prevent) → 不想腐 (transform). **权力反腐 (campaign-style, 治标) vs 制度反腐 (structural, 治本):** the structural shift is real (2018 国监委 + 监察法) but **incomplete** — "一把手" still has overwhelming power, 灯下黑 persists (enforcers themselves corrupt), 牛栏关猫 (loose institutions), 既得利益 resist. Without independent judiciary + media + 民众监督, corruption cannot be structurally solved. **Default linkage:** 腐败 → 营商环境 → 经济可持续 (跨课题: 经济).

- **法治 — the deeper ceiling.** **法治 vs 法制:** 法治 = law constrains power ("恶法非法"); 法制 = law is a tool of power. China since 1999 enshrines 依法治国 but practice is closer to 法制. **党主法治 vs 党导法治:** Western critical label vs Chinese self-label. 18届4中: "党的领导是社会主义法治最根本的保证" — but if Party leads, who watches the Party? 司法改革 has done the **三去** (去地方化 / 去行政化 / 去官僚化 — provincial unification of court personnel, separation from administrative hierarchy, 员额制) — technical advances, but **cannot challenge party supremacy**. So rule of law in China is bounded by 党的领导 — and every reform hits that ceiling.

### Layer 3 — Managing society (社会、声音与身份)

The party manages, rather than represents, the population. Three pressure points where governance meets the people.

- **公共服务 + 回应人民诉求 — earning legitimacy through delivery.** Government model progression: **全能主义 (1949–78) → 发展型政府 (1978–2002, GDP-first 锦标赛) → 服务型政府 (2003+, SARS catalyst, 温家宝 2004)**. 4E criteria: 经济性 / 效率性 / 效果性 / 公平性. **均等化-均衡性-可及性:** 均衡 = base, 可及 = guarantee, 均等 = goal. HCI-named mechanisms: **接诉即办 (北京 12345 closed-loop) / 一网通办 (上海) / 最多跑一次 (浙江) / 网格化管理 (无锡) / 干部下访 (贵州)**. Driving theory: **风险社会 (Beck 1986)** = core problem shifts from distributing wealth to managing systemic risks → demands explode → responsiveness becomes the decisive variable. 亨廷顿: demands MUST be channelled institutionally or system overloads.

- **少数民族 — highest-stakes 跨课题 to 外交.** Official line: **三股势力 (分裂主义 / 极端主义 / 恐怖主义)** threaten unity. Policy: **民族区域自治 + 对口支援 + 兴边富民**. International criticism: assimilation + language erosion (Dec 2025 国家通用语言文字法 修订 removed minority-language teaching protection; July 2026 民族团结进步促进法 mandates 普通话 instruction). **The framing trap:** treating either official line OR Western critique as the full picture loses marks. The high-mark move is: hold both, analyse the tension, link to 外交 (国际人权批评 → 软实力损耗).

- **政治参与 + 网络治理 — channelling voice without sharing power.** Three-phase evolution: **萌芽 (1994–2002) → 形成 (2003–13, 微博 era) → 完善 (2014+, 网信办 + 防火长城 + 社会信用)**. Official channels: 协商民主 + 信访 + 12345 + 人大政协. Non-institutional: 网络问政, 群体性事件 (2010 peak 9万件), social movements. **赋权 vs 管控 双刃剑** — network governance enables 政府回应 AND surveillance; both true. Real costs of suppression: 白纸运动, 四通桥, 张展. National Cyber ID (July 2025) is the newest concrete mechanism — note as "近期发展" not core canon.

## The single most important concept

**Legitimacy = 政绩 > 意识形态, and every governance challenge is a stress test of this hierarchy.**

The CCP's right to rule rests primarily on what it delivers (GDP growth, poverty reduction, public services, national strength). Ideology (中国特色社会主义 + 习思想 + 两个确立) is the supplementary base — deployed harder when performance flags (post-2015 slowdown forced the ideological rebranding). Anti-corruption, rule of law, central-local friction, ethnic policy, public participation, network governance — **every node is ultimately about whether the party-state can sustain 政绩合法性 despite the structural risks its own design creates**.

When growth slowed (2019: GDP 6.1%, the 25-year low; 基尼 0.474; 维稳费 1.4万亿超军费), the regime doubled down on ideology (中国梦 2012, 两个确立 2021) AND on 反腐 AND on tightening 网络治理 — proving the hierarchy: when 政绩 wavers, **everything else must compensate**.

## How HCI tests this — three angles

Almost every 政治 essay tests one of these three:

1. **"Is X the BIGGEST / MOST IMPORTANT governance problem?"** → 最字题. Win by ranking on **解决难度 (结构性 > 技术性)** and **跨课题 impact**.
2. **"Can the party-state effectively do Y?"** → 是非题 / 程度题. Win by analyzing both the system's strengths (集中, 速度, 大国善治) and limits (no checks, no feedback loops, 单点故障).
3. **"Is X the FOUNDATION / KEY of Y?"** → 因素是非题 / 条件题. Win by **DISAGREE template**: the stated factor is necessary but not foundational; the deeper structural base is **政绩 + institutional design**.

## The 18 → 19 wall (the single most important rule)

To break from Tier 三 to Tier 四 (i.e. **19+ on a 30-mark essay**), you must do BOTH:

1. **跨课题** — link to 经济, 社会, OR 外交 as the natural endpoint of the causal chain (not a bolt-on).
2. **平衡论述** — genuine counter, not a token sentence.

| Miss | Cap |
|---|---|
| 没跨课题 | **18** |
| 没平衡论述 | **18** |
| Both | **15** |

**Default 跨课题 for governance:** 政绩合法性 → 经济增长 (use this unless the question forces 社会 / 外交). For 民族: → 外交. For 央地: → 经济.
