### The two axes / 两个轴线

| Axis / 轴线 | English | 中文 | What it describes / 描述什么 |

## Section 2 — The party-state evolution clarified (4 phases, NO confusion) / 党政关系演化澄清 (4 阶段, 不再混淆)

**English:** Ethan keeps blurring these. Here's the clean separation with what each phase ACTUALLY meant.

**中文:** Ethan 一直把这些混在一起。以下是干净分离 + 每阶段**实际**含义。

| Phase / 阶段 | Term / 术语 | Plain English / 直白英文 | 直白中文 | Defining feature |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **一元化** (Mao 1949–78) | "Party IS State" | 党即国家 | No institutional distinction. State is an empty shell of the Party. |
| 2 | **党政分开** (Deng 1980–89, FAILED) | "Party and State are SEPARATE" | 党国**分离** | Two autonomous entities. Party only does politics; State runs administration on its own. **Failed.** |
| 3 | **党政机构并列** (江胡 1989–2012) | "Parallel institutions, ambiguous leadership" | 党国**并行**, 领导模糊 | Both exist as institutions; same people often hold both; Party leads in practice but not always explicit. |
| 4 | **党政分工** (Xi 2013+) | "One integrated system, division of labour" | 一体系**分工** | Same institutions exist but Party leads EXPLICITLY ("党要领导一切"). State is functionally Party arm. |

### Critical distinctions / 关键区分

**English:** 

**党政分开 ≠ 党政机构并列** — Deng's failed reform had ZERO personnel overlap (different people running Party vs State); 江胡 era had EXTENSIVE overlap (same people).

**党政机构并列 ≠ 党政分工** — 江胡's "parallel" was AMBIGUOUS leadership; Xi's "分工" is EXPLICIT Party leadership. Xi's slogan: "**只有党政分工, 没有党政分开**" makes the distinction crystal clear.

**一元化 ≠ 党政分工** — Mao's 一元化 had NO real State institutions; Xi's 党政分工 has REAL State institutions doing real work, just under explicit Party direction.

**中文:**

**党政分开 ≠ 党政机构并列** — 邓 失败改革零人员重叠 (不同人管党 vs 国家); 江胡 时代大量重叠 (同一人)。

**党政机构并列 ≠ 党政分工** — 江胡 "并列" 是**模糊领导**; 习 "分工" 是**明确党的领导**。习的口号 "**只有党政分工, 没有党政分开**" 让区分清晰。

**一元化 ≠ 党政分工** — 毛 一元化 **没有**真实国家机构; 习 党政分工 **有**真实国家机构做实事, 只是在明确党的方向下。

### The killer example / 关键例子

**English:** Compare PREMIERS across eras (this is THE comparison for 党政 questions):

- **党政机构并列 (江胡):** 朱镕基 (1998–2003) — Premier with REAL policy autonomy. Drove WTO entry, SOE reform, banking reform, 1998 大部制 ("拆庙赶和尚" 3.3万 → 1.6万).

**朱镕基 = Premier with autonomy. 李克强 = Premier as executor.** This single comparison wins marks every time.

**中文 (关键例子):** 对比不同时代的**总理** — 这是 党政 题的核心比较:

## Section 3 — The trap concept family (don't blur them!) / 陷阱概念族 (别模糊!)

**English:** Three "trap" concepts are all real but DIFFERENT. Ethan keeps blurring them. Clean separation:

**中文:** 三个"陷阱"概念都真实但**不同**。Ethan 一直模糊它们。干净分离:

### Side-by-side / 并排

| Concept / 概念 | What it describes / 描述什么 | Source / 来源 | When to deploy / 何时使用 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **政绩困局** = **亨廷顿陷阱** | Authoritarian regimes have ONLY 政绩 legitimacy; both bad AND good performance erodes legitimacy long-term. ### How they relate / 三者关系

**English:**
- **阿克顿定律** = explains why centralisation creates corruption (input)
- **政绩困局** = explains how good performance still erodes legitimacy (process)
- **塔西佗陷阱** = describes the failure endpoint when trust collapses (output)

They're a CAUSAL CHAIN, not synonyms:
```
阿克顿定律 (unchecked power)
       ↓
Corruption + governance failures + 政绩 wobbles
       ↓
政绩困局 (good 政绩 → 中产 → demands → can't satisfy)
       ↓
Suppression + concealment + 报喜不报忧
       ↓
塔西佗陷阱 (trust collapse, irreversible)
```

**中文:**
- **阿克顿定律** = 解释中央化为何产生腐败 (输入)
- **政绩困局** = 解释好政绩为何仍侵蚀合法性 (过程)
- **塔西佗陷阱** = 描述信任崩塌时的失败终点 (输出)

它们是**因果链**, 不是同义词 (见上图)。

## Section 4 — Public services + responsiveness mechanisms clarified / 公共服务 + 回应机制 澄清

### Public services correct definition (Ethan got this wrong in Q3) / 公共服务 正确定义 (Q3 错)

**English:** 公共服务 = government-provided goods and services using statutory authority + public resources, with shared/non-exclusive characteristics.

**Critical:** Public services are FULLY part of 党国体制 — delivered BY the party-state, controlled BY the party-state, used BY the party-state as a legitimacy tool. They are NOT separate from 党国体制.

Also NOT necessarily voluntary or free:
- 9 years compulsory education (mandatory)
- Mandatory healthcare insurance contributions
- Many services subsidised but not free (hospitals charge, schools charge fees)

**中文:** 公共服务 = 政府以法定权威 + 公共资源 提供的具有共享性/非排他性 的物品和服务。

**关键:** 公共服务**完全是 党国体制 的一部分** — 由党国家提供、控制、用作合法性工具。**不是与之分开的。**

也不一定自愿或免费:
- 9 年义务教育 (强制)
- 强制医保缴费
- 许多服务有补贴但不免费 (医院、学校收费)

### Two government-type frameworks Ethan needs / 两个 Ethan 需要的政府类型框架

**English:**

**Three-government progression (the 三段论):**
- **全能主义** (1949–78): party-state penetrates everything, 计划经济, 政企不分
- **发展型政府** (1978–2002): GDP-first, 锦标赛, 强政府弱市场
- **服务型政府** (2003+): SARS catalyst, 温家宝 2004 first proposed, "以人为本"

## Section 5 — The complete 6-level demand hierarchy (Ethan was missing Level 2 + only had 4) / 完整 6 层诉求等级

**English:** When 中产阶级 grows, demands escalate up this hierarchy:

**中文:** 当 中产阶级 增长时, 诉求沿此等级**升级**:

| Level / 层 | English | 中文 | Mode | China's response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** | Basic technical (broken streetlights, water leaks) | 基本技术性 (路灯坏、水管漏) | Mode 1 | 12345 + 接诉即办 |
| **Level 2** ⚠️ | Service quality (better schools, faster bureaucracy, hospital quality) | 服务质量 (更好学校、更快政务、医院质量) ⚠️ Ethan kept forgetting this | Mode 1 | 一网通办 + 最多跑一次 |

| **Level 4** | Quality of life (clean air, food safety, environment) | 生活质量 (空气、食品安全、环境) | Mode 1 → Mode 2 zone | 环保 campaigns (partial; selective) |
| **Level 5** | Political voice (rights to organize, free press, criticize leaders) | 政治声音 (组织权、新闻自由、批评领导权) | Mode 2 | SUPPRESSED (网信办, 防火长城, 维权律师 crackdowns) |

### The structural trap / 结构陷阱

**English:** Faster Mode 1 responsiveness → raised expectations → MORE demands generated faster → demands escalate UP the hierarchy → eventually Level 5-6 hits the 党国体制 wall → suppression cost rises (维稳费 2019 = 1.4 万亿超军费). This is **政绩困局** in mechanism form.

**中文:** 模式 1 回应越快 → 期望越高 → 产生**更多**诉求更快 → 诉求沿等级**升级** → 最终 Level 5-6 撞上 党国体制 之墙 → 压制成本上升 (维稳费 2019 = 1.4 万亿超军费)。这就是 **政绩困局** 的机制形式。

## Section 9 — The "uncertain term" discipline rule / "不确定术语" 纪律规则

**English:** Ethan keeps inserting vague terms when uncertain ("断网禁", "1月31日", "公共服务 通常自愿性免费"). This pattern loses BOTH marks AND credibility.

**The rule:**
- If you can't recall the exact term → **OMIT it or describe the concept generically**
- If you can't recall the exact date → **OMIT it or use "近年" / "2010年代以来"**
- If you can't recall the exact data → **OMIT it or use qualitative ("大幅", "显著")**

**Wrong terms are worse than no terms.** Markers can see your uncertainty in vague wrong inserts; they CAN'T see uncertainty in clean omissions.

**中文:** Ethan 在不确定时一直插入模糊术语 ("断网禁"、"1月31日"、"公共服务通常自愿性免费")。此模式**既扣分又失公信力**。

**规则:**
- 记不起具体术语 → **省略或用通用描述**
- 记不起具体日期 → **省略或用 "近年" / "2010年代以来"**
- 记不起具体数据 → **省略或用定性 ("大幅"、"显著")**

**错术语比无术语更糟。** 阅卷者能从模糊错插中看到你的不确定; **看不到**干净省略中的不确定。

## Section 11 — Quick recall (one page) / 速记 (一页)

### The two axes / 两个轴线
- **党政关系** (vertical: Party vs State) ≠ **央地关系** (horizontal: Centre vs Local)
- Don't conflate. Q stems about 三权分工/总书记/常委 = Axis 1. Q stems about 中央/地方/分税制/LGFV = Axis 2.

### The 4 党政关系 phases / 4 阶段
- 一元化 (Mao, 党=国家) → 党政分开 (Deng, FAILED) → 党政机构并列 (江胡, ambiguous) → 党政分工 (Xi, explicit)
- Killer example: 朱镕基 (autonomy) vs 李克强 (sidelined)

### The 3 trap concepts / 3 个陷阱概念
- 阿克顿 (power corrupts) → 政绩困局/亨廷顿 (good 政绩 → 中产 → demands) → 塔西佗 (trust collapse)
- They're a CHAIN, not synonyms

### The 6-level demand hierarchy / 6 层诉求等级
- L1 basic technical → L2 service quality → L3 反腐 → L4 quality of life → L5 political voice → L6 structural reform
- Mode 1 (L1-4) China masters; Mode 2 (L5-6) China suppresses

### The 5-element checklist / 5 要素清单
1. 立场 upfront
2. 3+ 论点 with mechanism
3. 平衡 (genuine, not token)
4. 跨课题 (governance default: 政绩合法性 → 经济增长)
5. 数据 + 案例 (2+ data, 1+ case)

### Default 立场 / 默认立场
- 因素是非 / 根本 题 → **DISAGREE** (政绩 > 意识形态; trap)
- 程度题 → commit 高/较大/一定/较小 (never 中等)
- 比较题 (习 vs 江胡) → **PARTIAL** (execution yes, correction no)
- 条件题 ("必须...才能") → **DISAGREE** (structural lock + 苏东巨变)
- 最字题 → 解决难度 axis (结构性 > 技术性)

### Discipline rules / 纪律规则
- Self-rate **C only if ALL 5 elements present**
- **OMIT when uncertain** (don't guess terms / dates / data)
- ALWAYS use exact terms: 三权分工 (NOT 三权分立); 政绩 (NOT 政绩成绩); 党政分工 (NOT 党政分开)

### Scholar attributions (memorize) / 学者归属 (背)
- 使命型政党 → **郑永年**
- 晋升锦标赛 → **周黎安** (NOT 朱镕基)
- 分税制 / 大部制 → **朱镕基** (implementer)
- 碎片化威权主义 → **Lieberthal-Oksenberg (1988)**
- 政绩困局 / 亨廷顿陷阱 → **亨廷顿 (Huntington)**
- 风险社会 → **Beck (1986)**
