## Section 1 — How the CCP actually makes decisions / 中共到底怎么决策

### The decision-making hierarchy / 决策层级

**English:** Pyramid from broad to narrow:

```
中央委员会 (CCP Central Committee) — ~370 members (full + alternate)
       ↓ meets ~once a year (中央全会, plenum)
       ↓ delegates day-to-day to:
中央政治局 (Politburo) — 24 members (post-2022)
       ↓ meets monthly
       ↓ delegates urgent decisions to:
中央政治局常务委员会 (Politburo Standing Committee, "常委") — 7 members
       ↓ meets weekly, decides everything important
       ↓ supported by:
中央书记处 (CCP Secretariat) — operational arm, handles execution
       ↓
中央办公厅 (General Office) — administrative backbone
```

**中文:**
```
中央委员会 — 约 370 名 (正式 + 候补)
       ↓ 一年开一次 (中央全会)
中央政治局 — 24 人 (2022年后)
       ↓ 月度会议
中央政治局常务委员会 (常委) — 7 人
       ↓ 周度会议, 决定一切重要事
中央书记处 — 执行臂膀
中央办公厅 — 行政中枢
```

### How a major decision actually flows / 一个重大决策的实际流程

**English (using zero-COVID lockdown as example):**

1. **Spotting** — Public Health Commission + Wuhan officials report alarming case data to State Council
2. **Escalation** — State Council escalates to 政治局常委 because of scale
3. **Internal debate** — 习近平 calls emergency 常委 meeting. The 7 men debate options (lockdown vs targeted measures vs herd immunity). This is the 民主 phase — full expression of views internally. NO ONE OUTSIDE the 7 knows they're disagreeing.
4. **Decision** — 习 announces the verdict (lockdown Wuhan). The 集中 phase begins.
5. **Cascade execution:**
   - 中央书记处 drafts the implementation order
   - 中央办公厅 sends to State Council + military + relevant ministries
   - State Council mobilizes 国家卫健委, 国务院应急办, 公安部, 交通运输部
   - Provincial party committees implement at province level
   - City party committees implement at city level
   - All within hours — no public hesitation, no competing announcements
6. **No reversal** — even if officials disagree privately, no one publicly says "this is wrong." That would be violation of 四个服从 → discipline → 开除党籍.

**中文 (用 zero-COVID 武汉封城为例):**

1. **发现** — 国家卫健委 + 武汉官员 把警报数据报给国务院
2. **升级** — 国务院 因规模升级到 政治局常委
3. **内部辩论** — 习 召开 常委 紧急会议。7 个人辩论 (封城 vs 精准防控 vs 群体免疫)。这是 民主 阶段 — 内部充分表达。外人不知道他们在分歧。
4. **决策** — 习 宣布结论 (武汉封城)。集中 阶段开始。
5. **级联执行:** 中央书记处 起草令 → 中央办公厅 发给国务院 + 军队 + 相关部委 → 国务院调动 卫健委 + 应急办 + 公安部 + 交通运输部 → 省委 在省层面执行 → 市委 在市层面执行 → 数小时内完成, 无公开犹豫, 无竞争公告
6. **不可逆** — 即使官员私下不同意, 没人公开说 "这是错的"。那是违反 四个服从 → 处分 → 开除党籍

### Simple analogy / 简单比喻

**English:** Imagine 7 board members of a company at a closed-door meeting. They argue fiercely for 3 hours about a major decision. Once they vote and the chairman announces the verdict, every employee in the company implements it as if it's the company's united position. **Anyone publicly saying "I disagree with the board" gets fired immediately.** That's how 民主集中制 + 四个服从 makes a 1.4 billion person country move as one.

**中文:** 想象一家公司 7 个董事在闭门会议。激烈辩论 3 小时。一旦投票决定, 主席宣布结果, 全公司所有员工把它当作公司统一立场来执行。**任何公开说 "我不同意董事会" 的人立刻被开除。** 这就是 民主集中制 + 四个服从 让 14 亿人国家像一个人一样行动的方式。

## Section 2 — The Personnel System (the most underrated power center) / 人事系统

### 党管干部 — "The Party manages cadres" / "党管干部" 原则

**English:** This is one of the most important principles in Chinese politics — and one of the least understood outside China. **The Party owns all senior personnel decisions** across government, military, SOEs, universities, hospitals, media. Promotions, appointments, transfers — all routed through Party.

**中文:** 这是中国政治最重要的原则之一, 也是中国之外最少被理解的。**党拥有所有高级人事决定** — 政府、军队、国企、大学、医院、媒体。晋升、任命、调动 — 全部通过党。

### 中央组织部 (Organization Department) / 组织部

**English:** The single most powerful institution most foreigners haven't heard of. It controls the careers of:
- ~5,000 senior provincial + ministerial officials directly (省部级)
- ~50,000 厅局级 indirectly
- Through cascading subordinate departments: all ~80 million Party members' career paths

**Simple analogy / 简单比喻:** Imagine if Google's HR department also controlled who became president of every Fortune 500 company, every state governor, every university chancellor, every military general, every newspaper editor. That's 组织部.

**中文:** 大多数外国人没听过, 但是最强力的机构。它直接控制约 5000 名省部级官员的职业生涯, 间接控制约 50,000 名厅局级, 通过下级部门级联控制全部约 8000 万党员的职业路径。

### 干部考核 — How cadres are evaluated / 干部考核

**English:** Multi-dimensional scoring:
- **GDP growth** (most important historically — drives 晋升锦标赛)
- **Party loyalty** (政治表现) — increasingly weighted under Xi
- **Implementation of central directives** (执行力)
- **Stability maintenance** (维稳 — no major incidents on your watch)
- **Personal incorruptibility** (廉洁) — increasingly weighted post-2013

The shift under Xi: **less weight on GDP, more on party loyalty + ideological alignment**. Officials now need 政治表现 just as much as 经济表现.

**中文:** 多维度考核: GDP 增长 (历史上最重要, 驱动 晋升锦标赛) + 党的忠诚 (政治表现, 习时代权重增加) + 执行力 + 维稳 + 廉洁 (2013年后权重增加)。习时代转变: GDP 权重下降, 党忠诚 + 意识形态对齐 权重上升。

### Vivid example: why a smart, capable official can't just "act on their conscience"

**English:** Imagine you're a county Party Secretary who privately thinks zero-COVID is wrong by mid-2022. You have FOUR options:

1. **Publicly oppose** → expelled from Party within days → career destroyed → social network destroyed → possibly imprisoned. NOBODY does this.
2. **Quietly implement loosely** → if caught, severe discipline. Most don't risk this.
3. **Implement strictly + grumble privately** → standard cadre choice. Wait for the line to change.
4. **Try to escalate concerns through internal channels** → must go through your immediate superior, who decides whether to forward up. If superior thinks it's politically risky, your concern dies there.

Most rational officials pick **option 3** — strict implementation + private grumbling. This is why the system can't 自我纠错: the people who see problems on the ground have no safe way to surface them upward.

**中文:** 想象你是 2022 年中觉得 zero-COVID 错了的县委书记。四个选项:
1. 公开反对 → 几天内开除党籍 → 职业毁掉 → 社会关系毁掉 → 可能坐牢。没人这样做。
2. 悄悄松执行 → 被抓就严重处分。多数人不冒险。
3. 严格执行 + 私下抱怨 → 标准干部选择。等路线变。
4. 通过内部渠道升级关切 → 必须通过直属上级, 由他决定是否上报。如果上级觉得政治风险, 你的关切就死在那里。

多数理性官员选 **选项 3**。这就是为什么系统不能 自我纠错。

## Section 3 — Why internal dissent → career death / 为什么内部异见 = 职业死亡

### The discipline mechanism / 纪律机制

**English:** Three escalating levels:
- **党纪处分** — Party discipline warning (entry on permanent record)
- **开除党籍** — expulsion from Party (career-ending in any system position)
- **双开** — "double expulsion" — out of Party AND out of public office. Sometimes followed by criminal charges.

Why "career-ending"? In 党国体制, almost every senior position (government, SOE, university, military) requires Party membership. Lose Party membership = lose access to entire elite career path.

**中文:** 三层升级: 党纪处分 → 开除党籍 → 双开 (党籍 + 公职双开除, 有时跟随刑事指控)。

为什么 "职业终结"? 在 党国体制 里, 几乎所有高级职位 (政府、国企、大学、军队) 都需要党员身份。失去党员 = 失去进入精英职业道路的资格。

### Vivid examples / 案例

**English:**

- **赵紫阳 (1989):** General Secretary of CCP. Visited Tiananmen Square in May 1989 and said "we came too late" (sympathising with students). Stripped of all positions, placed under house arrest until death in 2005. Most senior official ever purged in this way.

- **任志强 (2020):** Real estate tycoon, princeling, former state-owned enterprise chairman. Wrote essay criticizing Xi's COVID handling (called him "a clown stripped naked who insisted on continuing being emperor"). Within months: expelled from Party, sentenced to 18 years in prison for corruption charges. The corruption charges were the pretext; the real reason was the criticism.

- **蔡霞 (2020):** Former professor at the Central Party School (the CCP's elite training academy). Criticized Xi's authoritarianism in a leaked audio recording. Expelled from Party. Forced into exile in the US.

- **Bao Tong / 鲍彤 (1989):** 赵紫阳's top aide. Imprisoned for 7 years after 1989. Kept under surveillance until his death in 2022. **No subsequent leader has ever apologised for Tiananmen.** The lesson is permanent.

**中文 (历史案例):**
- 赵紫阳 (1989): 中共总书记, 同情学生 → 撤职软禁至 2005 年去世
- 任志强 (2020): 国企董事长, 批评习 → 18 年徒刑 (借腐败罪)
- 蔡霞 (2020): 中央党校教授, 批评习 → 开除党籍, 流亡美国
- 鲍彤 (1989): 赵紫阳助手, 入狱 7 年

These examples are the ENFORCEMENT mechanism behind 四个服从. Every cadre knows them. 这些案例是 四个服从 背后的执行机制。每个干部都知道。

## Section 4 — How Party penetrates everything (党组 system) / 党如何渗透一切

**English:** Inside every State organ, every SOE, every university, every large private company — there's a **党组 (Party Group)**. This is the parallel Party structure inside the institution.

- A university has a 校长 (president, running academics) AND a 党委书记 (Party Secretary, running political alignment). The 党委书记 is the ACTUAL boss in most decisions.
- An SOE has a 总经理 (CEO) AND a 党组书记 (Party Group Secretary). Same dynamic.
- Even Tencent, Alibaba, ByteDance have internal Party cells.

This is how the Party operates inside institutions without formally controlling them on paper. The State organ has its formal head and its Party head; the Party head wins on anything political.

**中文:** 每个国家机关、每个国企、每个大学、每个大型私企里, 都有 党组。这是机构内部的并行党结构。

- 大学有 校长 (管学术) 和 党委书记 (管政治对齐)。党委书记 通常才是真正的老板。
- 国企有 总经理 (CEO) 和 党组书记。同样动力。
- 即便腾讯、阿里、字节跳动 内部都有党组。

这是党在不正式控制机构 (账面上) 的情况下, 在机构内部运营的方式。

## Section 5 — 条条 vs 块块 explained with concrete examples / 条条块块 用具体例子讲清

### The basic structure / 基本结构

**English:** Every Chinese government department reports to TWO bosses, creating two simultaneous chains of command:

- **条条 (vertical lines, "ministry chain"):** the FUNCTIONAL chain — environment ministry → provincial environment bureau → city environment bureau. Same function from top to bottom.
- **块块 (horizontal blocks, "geographic chain"):** the TERRITORIAL chain — province → city → county. Each level governs ALL functions within its territory.

**中文:**
- 条条: 功能纵向链条 — 环保部 → 省环保厅 → 市环保局
- 块块: 地理横向链条 — 省 → 市 → 县, 每级管辖区内所有功能

### Simple school analogy / 学校简单比喻

**English:** You're a student. Two bosses send you instructions:

- **块块 = your school principal** (runs YOUR school's daily life)
- **条条 = the city education department** (oversees specific subjects across ALL schools)

Your principal says: "Friday is sports day, no classes."
The city education department says: "Every school must have English class every Friday."

Who do you obey? In China, you don't pick one. You **negotiate, compromise, partially comply** — maybe a 30-minute English session squeezed in before sports day. This compromise IS the system in action.

**中文:** 你是学生, 两个老板给你指令:
- 块块 = 你的校长 (管你学校日常)
- 条条 = 教育局 (管所有学校的英语课)

校长说: 周五运动会, 不上课。
教育局说: 每周五必须有英语课。

你听谁? 在中国, 你不选一个。你**谈判、妥协、部分服从** — 也许在运动会前挤进 30 分钟英语。**这种妥协就是体制在运作。**

### Concrete real-world example / 真实例子

**English:** Tangshan steel mill 2017 (the canonical 上有政策下有对策 case):

- **条条 order:** Ministry of Environmental Protection says close 19 old polluting blast furnaces in Tangshan.
- **块块 incentive:** Tangshan city government needs GDP + employment for cadre promotions.
- **Compromise:** Local officials closed the 19 furnaces (technical compliance with 条条) AND built a bigger new steel plant (serving 块块 GDP goals). Pollution NOT reduced. Letter of central policy followed; spirit violated.

This is 上有政策, 下有对策 in mechanism form.

**中文:** 2017 唐山钢厂案:
- 条条 命令: 环保部 关闭 19 座旧高炉
- 块块 激励: 唐山市政府 需要 GDP + 就业为干部升迁
- 妥协: 关 19 座 (字面服从 条条) + 同时建一座更大新钢厂 (服务 块块 GDP)。污染未减。**上有政策, 下有对策 的机制形式。**

### Family analogy (another way to see it) / 家庭比喻

**English:** Two working-from-home parents:
- **块块 = Mom** (runs household, decides daily schedule)
- **条条 = Dad** (in charge of "all chore times throughout the week")

Mom says "no chores this Sunday, let's relax." Dad says "every Sunday 2pm is chore time."

The kids end up doing **half chores quickly** — partial compliance to both. That's 碎片化威权主义 in microcosm: orders from above arrive but get watered down by competing chains of authority at the receiving end.

**中文:** 父母都在家办公:
- 块块 = 妈妈 (管家务日程)
- 条条 = 爸爸 (管所有做家务时间)

妈说 "周日放松"。爸说 "周日 2 点做家务"。

孩子最后**快速做一半家务** — 对两边的部分服从。这就是 **碎片化威权主义** 的缩影: 上面的命令到了, 但在接收端被竞争的权威链条稀释。

## Section 6 — 碎片化威权主义 (Fragmented Authoritarianism) / 框架详解

### The framework / 框架本身

**English:** Lieberthal-Oksenberg coined this in 1988 (Policy Making in China: Leaders, Structures, and Processes). The thesis: **China LOOKS authoritarian on paper (one party, top-down) but in ACTUAL OPERATION it's fragmented across thousands of competing bureaucratic chains.**

**中文:** Lieberthal 和 Oksenberg 1988年提出 (《Policy Making in China》)。命题: **中国账面上看是威权 (一党, 自上而下), 但实际运作中是碎片化的, 横跨数千条竞争性官僚链条。**

### Four characteristics / 四个特征

| Characteristic | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| **讨价还价** | Bargaining — between bureaucratic agencies at all levels | 讨价还价 — 各级官僚机构之间 |
| **政策传导层层博弈** | Layer-by-layer policy contestation | 政策传导层层博弈 |
| **信息不对称** | Information asymmetry — those above don't fully know what's happening below | 信息不对称 — 上层不完全知道下层 |
| **执行偏差** | Implementation drift — orders get distorted as they cascade | 执行偏差 — 命令向下级联时被扭曲 |

### Concrete examples to make it vivid / 具体例子

**Example 1: COVID variations across provinces (2020-2022)**

**English:** "Zero-COVID" was central policy. But execution varied wildly:
- Shanghai initially resisted lockdown (March 2022), then was forced into it by central pressure (April 2022)
- Beijing implemented "soft lockdown" with more economic accommodations
- Hubei (after Wuhan trauma) was most strict
- Some rural counties barely enforced
- **Same central policy, fragmented local implementation.** That's 碎片化威权主义.

**中文:** "Zero-COVID" 是中央政策。但执行差异巨大:
- 上海起初抵抗 (2022年3月), 后被中央压力强迫 (2022年4月)
- 北京 "软封城" + 经济通融
- 湖北 (武汉创伤后) 最严格
- 一些农村县几乎不执行
- **同一中央政策, 碎片化地方执行。** 这就是 碎片化威权主义。

**Example 2: Environmental enforcement**

**English:** Air pollution regulations are central law. But enforcement varies because local governments depend on factory tax revenue. So:
- Some cities enforce strictly (Beijing, post-Olympics era)
- Other cities barely enforce (industrial cities in Hebei)
- "Inspection days" — locals tip off factories before central inspectors arrive
- Factories temporarily shut down for inspections, then restart

**中文:** 大气污染法规是中央法。但执行差异因为地方政府依赖工厂税收:
- 某些城市严格执行 (北京, 奥运后)
- 其他城市几乎不执行 (河北工业城市)
- "检查日" — 中央检查员到达前本地通风给工厂
- 工厂检查时暂停, 检查后重启

### Why 碎片化威权主义 matters / 为什么重要

**English:** It explains a paradox: China LOOKS like a monolithic dictatorship, but actual governance is messy, contested, partial. Central decisions don't smoothly translate into local action. **This makes China both more flexible (locals can adapt) AND less efficient (central policy drift)** than the "monolithic state" image suggests.

For essays: when a question asks "is the central government effective at X?", the high-mark answer acknowledges 碎片化威权主义 — central can decide, but execution is filtered through 条条/块块 negotiation. Effectiveness varies by issue area (military: high; environmental enforcement: medium; social control: variable).

**中文:** 它解释了一个悖论: 中国**看起来**是铁板一块的独裁, 但实际治理是混乱、有争议、部分性的。中央决定不会顺畅地翻译成地方行动。**这让中国既比 "铁板一块" 形象更灵活 (地方可以适应) 也更低效 (中央政策漂移)。**

考试应用: 当题目问 "中央政府在 X 上是否有效?", 高分答案承认 碎片化威权主义 — 中央能决定, 但执行被 条条/块块 谈判过滤。

## Section 7 — Why responsiveness ≠ democracy (the structural logic) / 为什么回应 ≠ 民主

### The key question Ethan asked / Ethan 问的关键问题

**English:** "If I listen to people, do I dilute my monopoly? Why does becoming responsive = becoming democratic?"

**The precise answer: it depends WHAT you respond to.**

There are TWO modes of responsiveness:

| Mode | What citizens get | What citizens DON'T get | Effect on monopoly |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technical responsiveness** (技术性回应) | Pothole fixed, water leak repaired, complaint about local official addressed | Power to choose officials, set policy direction, hold leaders accountable | NONE — monopoly intact |
| **Political responsiveness** (政治性回应) | Power to vote, free press, opposition party, courts that can rule against government | (these ARE democracy) | TOTAL — monopoly ends |

**中文:** "如果我听人民的诉求, 我是否会稀释垄断? 为什么变得回应 = 变得民主?"

精确答案: **取决于你回应什么。**

回应有两种模式:
- **技术性回应:** 修路、修水管、处理对地方官员的投诉。市民得到的: 具体问题解决。得不到的: 选择官员、设定政策方向、问责领导的权力。垄断影响: 无。
- **政治性回应:** 投票权、新闻自由、反对党、能裁定政府败诉的法院。这些就是民主本身。垄断影响: 全部失去。

### China's specific approach / 中国的具体做法

**English:** China is highly responsive on Mode 1 (技术性) and aggressively unresponsive on Mode 2 (政治性).

**Technical responsiveness mechanisms (Mode 1):**
- 12345 hotline (北京 接诉即办 — file complaint, get response within set days)
- 一网通办 (上海 — online government services, 95%+ "no need to visit office")
- 信访 system (centuries-old grievance channel, modernized with online platforms)
- 网络问政 (online forums where leaders periodically respond)
- 干部下访 (cadres descending to villages to hear concerns)

These all work. But they handle TECHNICAL problems (specific service failures, local corruption complaints). They do NOT change WHO holds power.

**Political non-responsiveness mechanisms (suppressing Mode 2):**
- 网络审查 (censorship)
- 防火长城 (Great Firewall)
- 社会信用 (social credit system)
- 2025年7月 国家互联网身份认证 (National Cyber ID)
- 信访 cap — people who 上访 (go to Beijing with grievances) too persistently get detained
- Suppression of organized civil society (NGO law restrictions)
- White Paper Protests 2022 → many participants tracked + detained

**中文:** 中国在模式 1 (技术性) 高度回应, 在模式 2 (政治性) 积极不回应。

**技术性回应:** 12345 + 接诉即办 + 一网通办 + 信访 + 网络问政 + 干部下访 — 都有效, 但处理具体服务失败 + 地方腐败投诉。不改变**谁**掌权。

**政治性不回应:** 网络审查 + 防火长城 + 社会信用 + 2025 国家互联网身份认证 + 信访封顶 + NGO 管制 + 白纸抗议参与者追踪拘留。

### Vivid example: the difference in action / 行动中的差异

**English:**

**Technical responsiveness example:** A resident in Beijing complains via 12345 that the streetlights on his street are broken. Within 7 days, lights are repaired. He's happy. The system worked. Monopoly: untouched.

**Political non-responsiveness example:** A group of citizens in Beijing want to form a political party advocating for free press. They face: registration impossible (NGO law), surveillance, detention, lawyers refused. They cannot form the party. Monopoly: defended.

**The party can be infinitely responsive on Mode 1 without giving up an inch of Mode 2.** This is why China can run a service-delivery state AND maintain authoritarian rule simultaneously. Singapore proves this scales (with caveats). Western democracies don't because they CHOSE Mode 2.

**中文:**
- 技术性: 居民投诉 12345 路灯坏 → 7 天内修好 → 满意。**垄断不动。**
- 政治性: 公民想组织新党 → 注册不可能, 监控, 拘留 → 无法组党。**垄断捍卫。**

党可以在模式 1 上无限回应, 而不让步模式 2 一寸。**这就是为什么中国可以同时运作 服务交付型 + 威权统治。**

## Section 8 — 公民社会 in China — what it actually is / 中国的公民社会到底是什么

### The framework: 强政府弱社会 / "Strong State, Weak Society"

**English:** China's civil society is structurally weak compared to Western democracies. The Party deliberately keeps it that way. Three reasons:
1. **使命型政党 logic:** mission needs unified mobilization, not pluralism
2. **历史经验:** independent civil society in 1989 (students + workers) almost toppled regime
3. **苏东巨变 lesson:** independent civil society in Eastern Europe (e.g., Polish Solidarity) was key to communist collapse

**中文:** 中国公民社会相对西方民主在结构上较弱。党刻意保持这样。三个原因:
1. 使命型政党 逻辑: 使命需要统一动员, 不是多元
2. 历史经验: 1989 年独立公民社会 (学生 + 工人) 差点推翻政权
3. 苏东巨变 教训: 东欧独立公民社会 (如波兰团结工会) 是共产党崩溃的关键

### The 双重管理体制 / "Dual Management System"

**English:** This is how China controls civil society organizations. To legally register an NGO, you must have:
1. **A "responsible business unit" sponsor** (业务主管单位) — a government agency that "supervises" your activities
2. **A "registration management unit"** (登记管理单位) — usually the Civil Affairs Department

The first requirement is the killer. **Who would sponsor you?** Most government agencies don't want the liability. If you can't find a sponsor, you can't register. Without registration, you can't legally fundraise, hire staff, hold events.

So China's "NGOs" are mostly:
- **Government-organized NGOs (GONGOs)** — Red Cross, All-China Women's Federation, Communist Youth League — essentially state arms
- **Foreign NGOs** — heavily restricted especially post-2017 (Foreign NGO Law)
- **Officially-tolerated grassroots** — environmental groups (Friends of Nature), some health/disability groups
- **Underground / quasi-legal** — religious groups (underground churches), 维权 lawyers, dissident networks — all face periodic crackdowns

**中文:** 双重管理体制 — 要合法注册 NGO, 必须有:
1. 业务主管单位 sponsor (政府机构 "监管" 你的活动)
2. 登记管理单位 (通常是民政部门)

第一个要求是杀手。多数政府机构不想承担责任。找不到 sponsor 就不能注册。

所以中国的 "NGO" 主要是:
- 政府组织的 NGO (GONGO): 红十字会、妇联、共青团 — 本质是国家臂膀
- 外国 NGO — 严重限制 (2017 外国 NGO 法)
- 官方容忍的草根: 自然之友 等环保组
- 地下/准合法: 地下教会、维权律师、异见网络 — 周期性打压

### Is 公民社会 effective at giving ideas to government? / 公民社会对政府提建议有效吗?

**English:** Mostly NO at the political level. Limited YES at the technical level.

- **Political input (e.g., "we want elections"):** Effectively zero channel. Suppressed.
- **Technical input (e.g., "this water quality is poor"):** Some effect through 12345, 信访, online petitions, expert consultations.
- **Crisis-triggered input:** When public outcry is large enough (三聚氰胺 milk 2008, 红十字会 scandal 2011, 白纸 protests 2022), the government may adjust — but it adjusts on its own terms.

The key insight: **the Party hears feedback when it helps Party stability. The Party suppresses feedback that threatens Party authority.** That's the filter.

**中文:** 政治层面: 几乎为零。技术层面: 部分有效。
- 政治输入 (如 "我们要选举"): 渠道为零。被压制。
- 技术输入 (如 "水质差"): 通过 12345、信访、网络请愿、专家咨询 有一定效果
- 危机触发: 公众愤怒大到一定程度 (2008 三鹿、2011 红十字会、2022 白纸), 政府可能调整 — 但按自己的条件调整

关键洞察: **党听帮助党稳定的反馈。党压制威胁党权威的反馈。** 这就是过滤器。

## Section 9 — Critical term distinctions / 关键术语区分 (CRITICAL FOR MARKS)

### 三权分立 vs 三权分工 / Separation vs Division

**English:** ⚠️ Confusing these costs marks immediately.

| 三权分立 (Western) | 三权分工 (Chinese) |
|---|---|
| Separation of powers | Division of labour |
| Executive, Legislative, Judiciary | 决策, 执行, 监察 |
| Three branches are CO-EQUAL — each can block the others | All three are UNDER PARTY — Party leads all |
| US: Congress vs President vs Supreme Court | China: 党 + 人大 (决策) / 国务院 (执行) / 国监委 + 纪委合署 (监察) |
| Designed to prevent tyranny | Designed to execute Party will uniformly |

**Never use 三权分立 to describe China.** Use 三权分工.

**中文:** ⚠️ 混淆这两个立刻扣分。
- 三权分立 (西方): 执行/立法/司法 同等, 互相制约。设计为防止暴政。
- 三权分工 (中国): 决策/执行/监察 都在党之下。设计为统一执行党的意志。

**绝不要用 三权分立 形容中国。** 用 三权分工。

### 一把手 vs 灯下黑 — different concepts / 不同概念

**English:**

**一把手 ("Number One"):** the TOP LEADER at any level (province, city, county, ministry). Usually the Party Secretary. Within his jurisdiction, he has decisive power. **The 一把手 problem:** at every level, the top person can't be effectively checked from within his own level because all subordinates depend on him for promotion.

**灯下黑 ("Dark under the lamp"):** literally "the area directly under the lamp is dark." Refers to the phenomenon that **the people whose job is to enforce anti-corruption are themselves corrupt**. The discipline inspectors (纪委 officials) are not immune to corruption, but they're the ones investigating others. Who watches the watchmen?

**Relationship:** Both describe limits on anti-corruption, but at different levels:
- 一把手 = structural problem (the top of each hierarchy is untouchable)
- 灯下黑 = operational problem (enforcers themselves can be corrupt)

A complete answer about anti-corruption ceiling uses BOTH.

**中文:**

**一把手** = 任何一级的最高领导 (省、市、县、部委)。通常是党委书记。在他的管辖范围内, 他有决定性权力。**一把手问题:** 每一级, 最高的人都不能从同级有效制约, 因为所有下属都依赖他升迁。

**灯下黑** = 字面 "灯正下方是黑的"。指 **执行反腐的人本身就腐败** 的现象。纪委官员不能免疫于腐败, 但他们却在调查别人。谁来监督监督者?

**关系:** 都描述反腐的局限, 但在不同层级:
- 一把手 = 结构性问题 (每个层级的顶都不可触碰)
- 灯下黑 = 运营性问题 (执行者本身可能腐败)

完整的反腐天花板答案两者都用。

### Vivid example for 灯下黑 / 案例

**English:** In 2014, the head of the Ministry of Public Security's 4th Bureau (responsible for anti-narcotics) was arrested for corruption — he had been taking bribes from drug traffickers. The man whose job was to fight crime was the criminal. That's 灯下黑.

**中文:** 2014年, 公安部禁毒局四局长被捕受贿 — 他从贩毒者收钱。打击犯罪的人就是罪犯。这就是 灯下黑。

### 法治 vs 法制 vs 党主法治 vs 党导法治 / Rule of Law term family

| Term | English | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **法治** | Rule OF law | Law as supreme standard, constrains even the ruler. "恶法非法" (bad law isn't law) |
| **法制** | Rule BY law | Law as instrument of ruler's power. Ruler is above law |
| **党主法治** | Party-supremacist rule of law (Western critical label) | The phrase Western critics use: "Party rules over rule of law" |
| **党导法治** | Party-led rule of law (Chinese self-label) | The phrase Chinese officials use: "Party guides rule of law" |

China **claims** 全面依法治国 (since 2014). In **practice** the system is closer to 法制 than 法治. China prefers the self-label 党导法治; critics use 党主法治. **The substance is the same: party leadership over law.**

**中文:** 中国**自称** 全面依法治国 (2014年起)。**实际** 体制更接近 法制 而非 法治。中国偏好自我标签 党导法治; 批评者用 党主法治。**实质相同: 党领导法律。**

### 党政分开 vs 党政机构并列 vs 党政分工 vs 一元化

| Phase | Term | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Mao (1949–78)** | **一元化** | Party = State, fused, totalist. State has no real autonomy |
| **Deng (1980–89)** | **党政分开** | SEPARATION — party handles politics, state handles administration autonomously. FAILED |
| **江/胡 (1989–2012)** | **党政机构并列** | PARALLEL institutions, overlapping personnel, party leads ambiguously |
| **Xi (2013+)** | **党政分工** | DIVISION OF LABOUR — one integrated system, party leads explicitly, state executes |

**Xi's defining slogan:** "**只有党政分工, 没有党政分开**" (only division of labour, no separation).

**中文:** 习的定义口号: "**只有党政分工, 没有党政分开**"。

## Section 11 — Quick Recall / 速记 — One page

### How decisions get made / 决策如何产生
- 政治局常委 (7 人) → 政治局 (24 人) → 中央委员会 (~370 人) → 各级党委 → 国家机器
- 民主集中制: 内部辩论 + 集中执行 + 公开零异见
- 四个服从: 党员服从组织 / 少数服从多数 / 下级服从上级 / 全党服从党中央

### Why dissent dies / 为什么异见死亡
- 开除党籍 = end of any elite career in 党国体制
- Examples: 赵紫阳 (1989), 任志强 (2020), 蔡霞 (2020)

### How Party penetrates institutions / 党如何渗透机构
- 党组 inside every State organ, SOE, university, large company
- 党管干部 + 组织部 controls personnel
- Real boss is usually 党委书记, not the formal head (校长, 总经理, etc.)

### 条条 vs 块块
- 条条 = vertical functional chain (ministry → provincial bureau → city bureau)
- 块块 = horizontal territorial chain (province → city → county)
- They cross-cut → 碎片化威权主义 → 上有政策下有对策

### 碎片化威权主义 (Lieberthal-Oksenberg 1988)
- China LOOKS monolithic, OPERATES fragmented
- 讨价还价 + 政策传导层层博弈 + 信息不对称 + 执行偏差
- Example: zero-COVID enforcement varied wildly across cities

### Why responsiveness ≠ democracy
- Mode 1 (technical): 12345, 接诉即办, 一网通办 — high responsiveness, monopoly untouched
- Mode 2 (political): elections, free press, opposition — = democracy itself, monopoly ends
- Party does Mode 1 aggressively, suppresses Mode 2 aggressively

### 公民社会 in China
- 强政府弱社会 (Strong state, weak society)
- 双重管理体制 (must have government sponsor)
- Result: GONGOs + tolerated grassroots + underground (periodically crushed)
- Mostly ineffective on political input; sometimes effective on technical input

### Term distinctions you MUST get right
- 三权分工 (Chinese) ≠ 三权分立 (Western)
- 一把手 (structural top untouchability) ≠ 灯下黑 (enforcers also corrupt)
- 法治 ≠ 法制; 党主法治 (critical label) ≠ 党导法治 (Chinese self-label)
- 党政分工 (Xi) ≠ 党政分开 (Deng's failed attempt)

### Scholars to attribute correctly
- 使命型政党 → 郑永年
- 晋升锦标赛 → 周黎安
- 1994 分税制 → 朱镕基 (implementer)
- 碎片化威权主义 → Lieberthal-Oksenberg 1988
