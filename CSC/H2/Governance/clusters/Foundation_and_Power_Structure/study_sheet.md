## Core Knowledge — Five Locked-Together Frameworks / 核心知识 — 五个互锁框架

### 1. 党国体制 (Party-State System)

**Definition / 定义:**
- **English:** Party (CCP) and State are fused into one organism. State is functionally an arm of the Party. Military, courts, civil service, media, SOEs all under Party command.
- **中文:** 党 (中共) 和国家融合为一个有机体。国家在功能上是党的臂膀。军队、法院、公务员、媒体、国企 全部由党指挥。

**Why China can't 自我纠错 under this architecture / 为什么这个架构下中国无法自我纠错 — six mechanisms:**

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No external check — Party controls every institution that could check it | 无外部制约 — 党控制所有可能制约它的制度 |
| 2 | 民主集中制 + 四个服从 suppresses internal dissent | 民主集中制 + 四个服从 压制内部异见 |
| 3 | Personnel system locks loyalty — promotion requires demonstrated agreement with current line | 人事系统锁定忠诚 — 晋升需要表现出对当前路线的服从 |
| 4 | 报喜不报忧 — officials lie upward to protect promotion | 报喜不报忧 — 官员向上撒谎以保住晋升 |
| 5 | Leader can't be publicly wrong — reversing = admitting error = political cost | 领导不能公开错 — 撤销 = 承认错误 = 政治代价 |
| 6 | No electoral feedback — voters can't kick out bad leaders | 没有选举反馈 — 选民无法投票推翻坏领导 |

**Vivid examples / 案例:**
- **大跃进 (1958–62) / Great Leap Forward:** 官员谎报粮食产量 → 中央按假数据征粮 → 真实歉收 → ~3000万人饿死 (officials lied about grain output → centre extracted grain based on false figures → famine → ~30 million died)
- **One-Child Policy (1980–2015):** 35 年的结构性错误, 没有机制纠正 (35 years of structural error, no correction mechanism)
- **Zero-COVID (2020–22):** 习个人押注政策 → 2022 奥密克戎下仍不撤 → 白纸运动 → 一夜逆转 → 大规模死亡 (Xi personally bet on policy → couldn't reverse under omicron → White Paper protests → overnight reversal → mass deaths)
- **Jack Ma 案 (2020):** 批评监管 = 批评党 → 蚂蚁集团 IPO 取消, $600 billion 蒸发 (criticize regulator = criticize Party → Ant Group IPO cancelled, $600B wiped out)

### 2. 使命型政党 vs 选举型政党 (郑永年)

**Definition / 定义:**

| | 使命型政党 (Mission-type) | 选举型政党 (Election-type) |
|---|---|---|
| **Legitimacy source / 合法性来源** | Historical mission / 历史使命 | Winning elections / 选举胜利 |
| **Member commitment / 党员认同** | Deep ideological / 深度意识形态 | Transactional / 交易性 |
| **Mission defined by / 使命由谁定** | The party itself / 党自己 | Voters every 4–5 years / 选民每 4-5 年 |
| **Examples / 例子** | CCP, Cuban CP, Vietnamese CP | US Democrats/Republicans, UK Labour/Conservative |

**Why this matters / 为什么重要:**
- **English:** 使命型政党 IDENTITY justifies 党国体制 STRUCTURE. Mission = too important for elections to disrupt → monopoly is necessary. Take away mission → monopoly looks like raw power-grab. Take away monopoly → mission can't be executed. **They're structurally locked.**
- **中文:** 使命型政党 身份为 党国体制 结构辩护。使命 = 太重要, 不能让选举打断 → 垄断是必要的。拿掉使命 → 垄断看起来是赤裸抢权。拿掉垄断 → 使命无法执行。**它们是结构性互锁的。**

**Simple analogy / 简单比喻:**
- 使命型政党 = a soccer team owner who built the team specifically to win the World Cup for his country. He doesn't let fans vote on the coach because he believes only he knows how to win the trophy. / 一个为了让国家赢得世界杯而专门组建球队的老板。他不让球迷投票选教练, 因为他相信只有他知道如何赢得奖杯。
- 选举型政党 = a soccer team owned by season-ticket-holding fans who vote on the coach each year. / 一个由年票球迷共同拥有、每年投票选教练的球队。

### 3. Weber 三型合法性 + China's adaptation / Weber 三型合法性 + 中国的改造

**Important clarification / 重要澄清:** China DOES use elements of all three Weber types — it just adapts them.

| Weber type | English | 中文 | China uses it? / 中国是否使用? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **传统型** | Traditional (custom, heredity) | 传统型 (习俗、世袭) | Partial — uses 5000-year civilization narrative + meritocratic appointment tradition, but no 天命观 explicit / 部分使用 — 用 5000 年文明叙事 + 选贤任能传统, 但不明用天命观 |
| **魅力型** | Charismatic (leader's personal magnetism) | 魅力型 (领袖个人魅力) | Selectively — Mao had it strongly, Xi cultivates cult of personality (习思想, 两个确立) / 选择性使用 — 毛 强烈拥有, 习 培育个人崇拜 |
| **法理型** | Legal-rational (law constrains ruler) | 法理型 (法律约束统治者) | **CANNOT use fully** — requires independent judiciary + constitutional limits + electoral procedure, all of which contradict 党的领导 / **不能完全使用** — 需要独立司法 + 宪法约束 + 选举程序, 都与党的领导矛盾 |

**China's own formulation / 中国的自创框架:**
- **意识形态合法性** (ideological legitimacy)
- **政绩合法性** (performance legitimacy) ← THE deeper base / 更深的基础
- **制度合法性** (procedural legitimacy)

**Why China defaults to 政绩 + 意识形态 / 为什么中国默认依赖 政绩 + 意识形态:**
- 法理型 would require law to constrain Party. But 党的领导 means Party leads law. Adopting 法理型 = dismantling 党国体制. So China's two viable options are 政绩 + 意识形态. / 法理型 需要法律约束党。但 党的领导 意味着党领导法律。采用 法理型 = 拆掉 党国体制。所以中国可行的两个选项是 政绩 + 意识形态。

### 4. 政绩 > 意识形态 hierarchy + 政绩困局 (亨廷顿陷阱)

**The hierarchy / 等级:**
- **English:** 政绩 is the deeper base. Ideology fills in when performance flags. Proof: when GDP slowed post-2015 (2019: 6.1%, 25-year low), regime pushed ideology HARDER (中国梦 2012, 两个确立 2021, 习思想 enshrined 2017). Pattern proves: when 政绩 wavers, ideology must compensate.
- **中文:** 政绩 是更深的基础。意识形态在 政绩 不足时填补。证明: 2015 后 GDP 放缓 (2019 年 6.1%, 25 年最低), 政权加强意识形态 (中国梦 2012、两个确立 2021、习思想 2017 写入党章)。模式证明: 政绩 摇摆时, 意识形态必须补偿。

**政绩困局 (亨廷顿陷阱) — the deeper trap / 更深的陷阱:**

| | 弱政绩 / Weak performance | 强政绩 / Strong performance |
|---|---|---|
| **Immediate effect / 直接效应** | Lose legitimacy (people unhappy) / 失去合法性 (人民不满) | Legitimacy strong (people happy) / 合法性强 (人民满意) |
| **Long-term effect / 长期效应** | Crisis / 危机 | **Also erosion** — middle class grows → new demands (voice, rights, accountability) → authoritarianism can't satisfy without dilution → forced choice: grant (regime change) or suppress (legitimacy loss differently) / **也是侵蚀** — 中产阶级 增长 → 新诉求 (表达、权利、问责) → 威权体制 无法满足 (除非稀释) → 被迫选择: 让步 (政权更迭) 或 压制 (从另一角度失去合法性) |

**Vivid historical examples / 历史案例:**
- **Taiwan 1949–2000:** 国民党 authoritarian → economic miracle 1960s–80s → middle class → 1987 解严 → 1996 first direct election → 2000 opposition wins. **Classic 亨廷顿 trajectory.** / 国民党 威权 → 1960-80 年代经济奇迹 → 中产阶级 → 1987 解严 → 1996 首次直选 → 2000 反对党执政。**典型 亨廷顿 路径。**
- **South Korea 1961–87:** 军政府 → economic miracle → 1987 民主化 → 1988 Olympics → liberal democracy / 军政府 → 经济奇迹 → 1987 民主化 → 1988 奥运 → 自由民主
- **Spain 1936–75:** Franco 独裁 → 经济现代化 → 1975 Franco 死 → 1978 民主. Same trajectory. / 同样路径
- **China 2001–24 in progress:** 中产 4% → 42.3%; 维稳费 2019 年 1.4万亿超军费. CCP suppresses rather than grants. **No authoritarian regime has yet survived this transition long-term.** / 中产 4% → 42.3%; 2019 维稳费 1.4万亿超军费。中共选择压制而非让步。**至今没有威权政体能长期通过这个转折。**

### 5. 塔西佗陷阱 (Tacitus Trap)

**Definition / 定义:**
- **English:** Once a government loses basic public trust, no statement or action will be believed regardless of content. It's a one-way door — irreversible legitimacy collapse.
- **中文:** 一旦政府失去基本公众信任, 无论说什么做什么都不会被相信。这是一个单向门 — 不可逆的合法性崩溃。

**Test / 测试:** government can tell truth + do genuine good, citizens still interpret cynically. / 政府说真话 + 做实事, 民众仍然 cynically 解读。

**Vivid examples / 案例:**
- **红十字会 + 郭美美 (2011):** 2008 地震后捐款 → 郭美美 炫富事件 → 信任崩溃 → 2020 武汉疫情时, 民众宁可直接捐给医院 也不捐红十字会. **15+ 年信任不恢复.** / Post-2008 quake donations → Guo Meimei flaunting wealth → trust collapsed → in 2020 Wuhan COVID, donors preferred giving directly to hospitals. **15+ years trust did not recover.**
- **李文亮 + COVID (2019–20):** 李 警告同事被警方训诫 → 后死于 COVID → 民众对所有官方 COVID 信息失去信任 (病例数、疫苗、封城、起源) / Li warned colleagues, was reprimanded by police, later died of COVID → public lost trust in all official COVID statements
- **三聚氰胺 奶粉 (2008):** 三鹿 加三聚氰胺 → 婴儿肾结石死亡 → 中国父母至今不信国产婴儿配方奶, 抢购外国奶粉 / Sanlu added melamine → babies died of kidney stones → Chinese parents still don't trust domestic formula, mass-buy foreign

## Historical Evolution — 党政关系 4 阶段 / 4-Phase Evolution

| Phase / 阶段 | Model / 模式 | What it meant / 含义 | What failed/changed / 失败/改变 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mao 一元化 (1949–78)** | Party = State, totalist / 党 = 国家, 全能主义 | All decisions to top Party; no institutional separation; State is empty shell / 所有决策上到党中央; 没有制度分离; 国家是空壳 | **Cultural Revolution disaster (1966–76) lasted 10 years; 大跃进 ~30M died.** 无纠错机制 → Deng concluded reform needed / 文革灾难 10 年; 大跃进 ~3000万死亡。需要改革 |
| **Deng 党政分开 (1980–89)** | Party and State SEPARATE / 党国分离 | Party handles politics; State handles administration; each autonomous / 党管政治; 国家管行政; 各自自主 | **Failed (4 reasons):** (1) 1989 六四 + 赵紫阳 清算政治否定; (2) 1991 苏东巨变警示; (3) 与 使命型政党 结构性冲突 (党失去人事控制 = 使命无法贯彻); (4) 地方干部抵制 / Failed: 1989 + 1991 + structural incompatibility + bureaucratic resistance |
| **江/胡 党政机构并列 (1989–2012)** | Parallel institutions, overlapping personnel / 平行制度, 人员重叠 | Both exist; same people often hold both; Party leads ambiguously / 两者都存在; 同一人常兼任; 党模糊领导 | **Xi diagnosed as drift:** 党的权威被削弱, 地方自主过大, 腐败猖獗 (周永康/薄熙来/徐才厚), 共识决策缓慢. / Xi diagnosed as drift: Party authority eroded, local autonomy too large, corruption rampant, consensus decisions slow |
| **Xi 党政分工 (2013+)** | One integrated system, division of labour / 一个整合系统, 分工 | Party leads everything explicitly ("党要领导一切" 19大); State functionally Party arm / 党明确领导一切; 国家在功能上是党的臂膀 | **Risk: 单点故障 (single-point-of-failure)** — if Xi's judgment fails, no backup. Brittle. / 风险: 单点故障 — 习的判断失败, 无备份。脆弱 |

**Key slogan / 关键口号:** "**只有党政分工, 没有党政分开**" (Only division of labour, no separation) — Xi's defining formula.

### 一元化 vs 党政分工 — what's the difference? (Critical clarification)

**Simple analogy / 简单比喻:**
- **一元化 (Mao) =** Small startup founder who is the CEO, accountant, salesperson, janitor, all in one. There's no actual "company" separate from him. / 一个小创业公司创始人, 同时是 CEO、会计、销售、清洁工。除了他没有真正的 "公司" 存在。
- **党政分开 (Deng) =** A holding company and an operating company with separate boards. Holding co (Party) only sets direction; operating co (State) runs day-to-day with full autonomy. CEOs of the two cos can disagree. / 控股公司 + 运营公司, 各有董事会。控股 (党) 只设方向; 运营 (国家) 完全自主运营。两 CEO 可以分歧。
- **党政机构并列 (江/胡) =** A company where the founder + the COO both show up at all meetings and both have a say. Founder usually wins but the COO has real authority too. / 创始人 + COO 都参加所有会议, 都有发言权。创始人通常赢, 但 COO 也有真正的权威。
- **党政分工 (Xi) =** CEO (Party) sets the menu, the strategy, hires managers; Managers (State) run day-to-day; but every decision must align with CEO direction and CEO has final say on everything. / CEO (党) 制定菜单、战略、雇人; 经理 (国家) 日常运营; 但每个决定必须与 CEO 方向一致, CEO 对一切有最终决定权。

**The key difference between 一元化 and 党政分工 / 一元化 与 党政分工 关键区别:**
- **一元化:** State is essentially nonexistent as institution. Party IS everything. / 国家作为制度基本不存在。党就是一切。
- **党政分工:** State exists as real institution with real ministries and personnel, but functionally serves Party. Party doesn't ABSORB the State; it COMMANDS the State. / 国家作为真实制度存在 (有真实部委和人员), 但功能上为党服务。党不**吸收**国家; 党**指挥**国家。

The institutional difference matters: under 党政分工 there's a real bureaucratic machinery with technical capacity. Under 一元化 there wasn't. That's why Xi's system can deliver high-speed rail, BRI, etc. that Mao's system couldn't. / 制度差异重要: 党政分工 下有真正的官僚机器和技术能力。一元化 下没有。所以习的体制能交付高铁、一带一路, 毛的体制不能。

## 6. 民主集中制 + 四个服从 — Operational Machinery

**Definition / 定义:**
- **民主 (Democratic):** full expression of views WITHIN the Party at top levels (Politburo debate) / 党内顶层充分表达 (政治局辩论)
- **集中 (Centralism):** once decision made, enforced uniformly with no public dissent / 一旦决定, 一致执行, 不容公开异见
- **四个服从:** 党员服从组织 / 少数服从多数 / 下级服从上级 / 全党服从党中央

**Simple analogy / 简单比喻:**
- Like a sports team in the locker room before the game. Players can argue tactics freely. But once the coach announces the play, everyone runs it. No one breaks formation on the field to do their own thing. / 像一支球队赛前在更衣室。球员可以自由争论战术。但教练一旦宣布打法, 所有人执行。没人在场上脱离阵型自己玩。

**Why it matters / 为什么重要:**
- **English:** This is what makes 党国体制 operationally possible. Without 民主集中制, internal Party debates would become public splits → instability (like late-Soviet Politburo factionalism that collapsed USSR). Without 四个服从, lower-level officials would resist higher-level decisions → policy implementation breaks (like Soviet republics defying Moscow 1989–91).
- **中文:** 这是让 党国体制 在运营上可能的东西。没有 民主集中制, 党内辩论会变成公开分裂 → 不稳定 (像晚期苏联政治局派系斗争, 让苏联崩溃)。没有 四个服从, 下级官员会抗拒上级决定 → 政策执行崩溃 (像 1989-91 苏联加盟共和国挑战莫斯科)。

**Vivid example / 案例:** 2020年1月23日 武汉 封城 — 政治局常委决定 → 全国所有部门数小时内执行, 没有任何官员公开质疑 → 与同期美国对比 (联邦慢、各州各自为政、佛州 vs 加州不同). / Jan 23, 2020 Wuhan lockdown — Politburo Standing Committee decided → all departments executed within hours, no official publicly questioned → contrast US (slow federal, states competed, Florida vs California differed).

## 7. 央地关系 — Central-Local Relations (Deepest Structural Friction)

### 1994 分税制 (Tax-Sharing Reform, 朱镕基)

**Mechanism / 机制:**
- Before 1994: 中央财政占 GDP 12% (1992), 弱中央 / Before 1994: central fiscal share ~12% of GDP, weak centre
- 1994 分税制 reform:
  - 增值税 (VAT) 75% 中央, 25% 地方 / VAT split 75% central, 25% local
  - 资源税 split (海洋石油 to centre, others to local)
  - 证券交易税 50/50
  - 关税 全部到中央 / All customs to central
- Result: 中央财政占比 跃升到 55%+ / Central jumped to 55%+

**The catch / 关键问题:** 事权 (spending obligations) were NOT centralized. Locals still bear: / 但 事权 没有上收, 地方仍要承担:
- 教育 (94.58% local) / Education
- 医疗 (98.81% local) / Healthcare
- 社保 (95.23% local) / Social security
- 城市基础设施 (99.84% local) / Urban infrastructure

**The famous formula / 著名公式:**
- **上级请客, 下级埋单** ("Superior treats, subordinate pays the bill")
- **央: 权大责小; 地: 权小责大** (Centre: big power small responsibility; Local: small power big responsibility)

### The cascade — what locals had to do / 级联效应 — 地方不得不做的

1. **土地财政 (Land finance):** 卖地为主要收入来源, 2010s 占地方收入 30-40% / Sell land use rights — became 30-40% of local revenue in 2010s
2. **LGFV 隐性债务:** 地方政府融资平台 (LGFV) 借贷, 估计 IMF $9 trillion (2024) / Hidden debt through Local Government Financing Vehicles, IMF estimates $9 trillion
3. **晋升锦标赛 distortion:** 干部按 GDP 升迁 → 过度投资基础设施、忽视环保和教育质量 / Cadre promotion by GDP → over-invest infrastructure, neglect environment and education

### 上有政策, 下有对策 — vivid example: Tangshan 2017

- 中央环保政策: 关闭 19 座旧高炉 / Central environmental policy: close 19 old furnaces
- 地方执行: 关了 19 座 + **同时建一座更大的新钢厂** 维持 GDP / Local action: closed 19 + **simultaneously built a bigger new steel plant** to maintain GDP
- 结果: 污染没减少, 字面服从中央, 实质违背中央 / Result: pollution not reduced; technical compliance, spiritual defiance

### 鬼城 (Ghost Cities) — vivid example of 晋升锦标赛 waste

- 鄂尔多斯, 天津于家堡, 营口 等城市建巨大住宅/商业项目, 无人入住 / Ordos, Tianjin Yujiapu, Yingkou built massive empty developments
- 为什么? 卖地 = 收入, 建设 = GDP, 官员已升迁后果才显现, 下任官员继承债务 / Why? Land sales = revenue, construction = GDP, official already promoted before consequences, next official inherits debt
- 估计 5000-6500 万套空置 (2024) / Estimated 50-65 million empty units

### 条条 vs 块块 + 碎片化威权主义 — simpler explanation / 更简单的解释

**条条 (Vertical lines) — the "ministry chain" / "部委链条":**
- Top-down functional control: 环保部 → 省环保厅 → 市环保局 / Top-down by function: Environmental Ministry → provincial environmental bureau → city environmental bureau
- 一个功能, 上下管到底 ("一竿子插到底") / One function, controlled top to bottom

**块块 (Horizontal blocks) — the "geographic chain" / "地理链条":**
- Territorial control: 省 → 市 → 县, each level governs ALL functions in its territory / Territorial: each level governs everything in its territory
- 一个地方, 管所有功能 / One place, governs all functions

**The crossing problem (key insight) / 交叉问题 (关键洞察):**
A city environmental bureau answers to BOTH:
- 块块: the city government (上级是市长) / its city government (boss: mayor)
- 条条: the provincial environmental bureau (上级是省厅) / the provincial environmental bureau (boss: provincial chief)

When the two send conflicting orders, the bureau has to navigate. / 当两边发不同指令时, 这个局只能权衡。

**Simple school analogy / 学校简单比喻:**
Imagine you're a student in a school. You have:
- **块块** = your school's principal (runs YOUR school) / 你学校的校长 (管你的学校)
- **条条** = the city's department of education (oversees ALL schools' English programs) / 教育局 (管所有学校的英语项目)

Your principal says "we're cancelling English class this Friday for sports day." Your education department says "all schools must have English class every Friday." Who do you obey?
- 校长说: 周五取消英语课办运动会
- 教育局说: 所有学校必须每周五有英语课
- 你听谁的?

In China, this conflict happens at every level. The official will quietly negotiate, dilute, partially comply — exactly 上有政策, 下有对策. / 在中国, 这种冲突发生在每一级。官员会悄悄谈判、稀释、部分服从 — 正是 上有政策, 下有对策。

**碎片化威权主义 (Fragmented Authoritarianism — Lieberthal-Oksenberg framework):**
- **English:** China is 威权 (authoritarian) IN NAME, fragmented IN OPERATION. Looks centralized on paper, but in practice every order gets bargained, diluted, partially implemented because of competing 条条 + 块块 chains. The system features: 讨价还价 (bargaining), 政策传导层层博弈 (layer-by-layer contestation), 信息不对称 (information asymmetry), 执行偏差 (implementation drift).
- **中文:** 中国名义上 威权, 实际运作 碎片化。账面上看起来集中, 但实际上每个命令都被 (条条 + 块块 链条竞争而) 讨价还价、稀释、部分执行。体制特征: 讨价还价、政策传导层层博弈、信息不对称、执行偏差。

**Simple family analogy / 家庭简单比喻:**
A family with both parents working from home. Mom (**块块**, runs the household) says "let's relax this Sunday, no chores." Dad (**条条**, in charge of all chore-times) says "every Sunday at 2pm, chores!" The kids end up doing half chores quickly. That's 碎片化威权主义 — orders from above arrive but get watered down by competing chains of authority on the ground. / 父母都在家办公的家庭。妈妈 (**块块**, 管家务) 说 "这周日放松, 不做家务"。爸爸 (**条条**, 管做家务时间) 说 "每周日下午 2 点做家务!" 孩子最后快速做一半家务。这就是 碎片化威权主义 — 上面的命令到了, 但被地面上竞争的权威链条稀释。

### 晋升锦标赛 (周黎安) — Promotion Tournament

**Definition / 定义:** Local cadres compete for promotion based on measurable indicators (chiefly GDP) under central HR control. Created equation **政绩 = 升迁**. / 地方干部按可测指标 (主要 GDP) 在中央人事控制下争升迁。创造了 **政绩 = 升迁** 等式。

**Why it's powerful / 为什么有力:** Same mechanism that drove China miracle (干部们拼命发展经济) also drives over-investment, environmental destruction, hidden debt, 报喜不报忧. / 同一个机制驱动了中国奇迹 (干部拼命发展) 也驱动了过度投资、环境破坏、隐性债务、报喜不报忧。

## 8. 党和国家机构改革 (Party-State Institutional Reform)

### 2018 reform — the milestone / 2018 改革 — 里程碑

- 8 正部级 + 7 副部级 合并 / mergers
- 新设党领导的委员会接管原本由国务院处理的领域:
  - **中央依法治国委员会** (rule of law)
  - **中央财经委员会** (economic policy)
  - **中央外事工作委员会** (foreign affairs)
  - **中央国家安全委员会** (national security, earlier)
- **国家监察委员会** 创立 + **监察法** 通过
- 中纪委 与 国监委 合署办公

**The pattern / 模式:** Policy authority migrated from 国务院 (State Council) to 中共中央 (CCP Central Committee). This is Xi's mechanism to make 党政分工 institutionally real. / 政策权威从国务院迁移到中共中央。这是习让党政分工制度化的机制。

### 三权分工 (Three-power Division of Labour) — NOT Western 三权分立

| Power / 权 | What / 内容 | Body / 机构 |
|---|---|---|
| **决策权** (Decision) | 制定政策 | Party committees + 人大 |
| **执行权** (Execution) | 落实政策 | 国务院 + 部门 |
| **监察权** (Supervision) | 监督官员 | 国监委 + 纪委合署 |

All three under unified Party leadership. NOT Western 三权分立 (exec/legislative/judicial separation). / 三者都在党的统一领导下。不是西方的三权分立。

### 五种政府类型 / Five Government Types

| Type / 类型 | English | 定义 |
|---|---|---|
| **法治政府** | Rule-of-law government | 权力依法设定/行使/受限 |
| **阳光政府** | Sunshine government | 政治透明 |
| **有限政府** | Limited government | 法律约束 (西式) |
| **责任政府** | Accountable government | 官员对结果负责 |
| **有为政府** | Active government | 市场失灵处主动作为 — 中国的独特定位 (区分于全能 + 西式有限) |

China officially aspires to all five but emphasizes 有为政府 — positions China between Mao 全能 and Western 有限. / 中国官方追求所有五种, 但强调 有为政府 — 中国定位介于 毛 全能 与 西式 有限 之间。
