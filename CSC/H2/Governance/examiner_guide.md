## PART 1 — Content Node Map (Coverage Checklist)

> Official syllabus nodes drawn from CSC 9629 2026 SEAB syllabus `[OFFICIAL]`. These are the UNTOUCHED/ACTIVE/FRAGILE/CLOSED tracking targets. No cluster is complete until every checked node here has been tested at least once.

### 1A — 总体特征 (General Characteristics of China's Governance System)

- [ ] **党国体制 (Party-state system)** — CCP's monopoly on political power; democratic centralism (民主集中制); party leads everything (党是领导一切). Spine: how the party-state structure enables AND constrains governance.
- [ ] **意识形态与政绩合法性 (Ideological + performance legitimacy)** — dual basis: Marxist-Leninist ideology + economic performance. 两个确立 (2021); 习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想. `[OFFICIAL]` 2024 specimen tested this directly as a 因素是非题.
- [ ] **中央-地方关系 (Central-local relations)** — 分税制 1994 (fiscal recentralisation); 财权上收/事权下放 (revenue up, spending obligations down); incentive structure for local cadres; 上有政策下有对策 (policy distortion).

### 1B — 挑战 (Governance Challenges)

- [ ] **贪污和腐败 (Corruption)** — systemic, multi-level; erodes economic efficiency + social equity + regime legitimacy; 运动式 vs 制度反腐 debate.
- [ ] **法治与司法改革 (Rule of law / judicial reform)** — 党主法治 vs 司法独立; 依法治国 as party ideology vs Western rule-of-law; 员额制; 司法责任制; 6th Five-Year Judicial Reform Outline (2024–2028).
- [ ] **回应人民诉求的能力 (Responsiveness to public demands)** — 信访制度; 网络问政; 12345 hotlines; consultative channels; limits under authoritarian structure.
- [ ] **少数民族问题 (Ethnic minority governance)** — 三股势力 (separatism, extremism, terrorism); 民族区域自治 制度; 维吾尔/新疆; 西藏; national identity vs minority rights; new 2026 Ethnic Unity Law.
- [ ] **执政能力与机构改革 (Governing capacity / institutional reform)** — 2018 党和国家机构改革; 放管服改革; 简政放权; 国家监察委 (National Supervisory Commission, 2018).
- [ ] **政治参与与异见 (Political participation and dissent)** — official channels (协商民主, 信访) vs non-institutional (online petition, social movements); 网络审查; 2025 national cyber ID; management of civil society / NGOs.

### 1C — 策略及成效 (Strategies and Effectiveness)

- [ ] **反腐策略 (Anti-corruption strategies)** — CCDI + NSC integrated enforcement; 不敢腐/不能腐/不想腐 三个阶段; 猎狐/天网 overseas asset recovery; 运动式 治标 vs 制度性 治本 tension.
- [ ] **司法改革策略 (Judicial reform strategies)** — 员额制 (judge quota reform); 司法责任制 (accountability for verdicts); circuit courts; removal of local interference. 6th Five-Year Judicial Reform Outline (2024–2028) `[RESEARCH]`.
- [ ] **公共服务与回应机制 (Public services + responsiveness mechanisms)** — 公共服务均等化; 基本公共服务标准化; 政府热线整合 (12345); e-government; 协商民主渠道.
- [ ] **少数民族管理策略 (Ethnic minority governance strategies)** — 兴边富民; 对口支援 (paired assistance); labour transfer programmes; ideological education campaigns (职业技能教育培训中心).
- [ ] **党和国家机构改革 (Party and state institutional reform)** — 2018 reforms merging CCDI + NSC; 监察法 (Supervision Law); 十五五规划 governance emphasis (2026–2030) `[RESEARCH]`.
- [ ] **政治参与管理 (Managing political participation)** — 制度化参与渠道; 协商民主; 网络治理法规 (CAC regulations); 12345 platform; cyber ID (国家互联网身份认证) July 2025 `[RESEARCH]`.

## PART 3 — Mark Scheme Logic (Governance-Specific)

> Drawn from CSC consolidated guide Parts 3, 4, 5, 6, filtered to governance patterns `[OFFICIAL]`/`[SCHOOL]`.

### 3.1 What earns marks in governance essays

**For 因素是非题 on 意识形态/绩效合法性 (the signature governance archetype):**
- Earns: clearly stating 立场 that DISAGREES with 意识形态 as the primary basis → naming 绩效合法性 as the REAL foundation (with data: 8亿脱贫, GDP 增长, 2024 GDP >130万亿) → running the 解决难度 comparison on the 合 segment (绩效 = 结构性基础; 意识形态 = 辅助手段).
- Earns: naming 两个确立 (2021) and 习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想 as the current ideological framework — NOT just "共产主义" (outdated framing).
- Loses: treating ideology and performance as equally important without committing to a verdict.

**For 最字题 on 腐败 as 最大挑战:**
- Earns: three-dimensional analysis — 经济损失 (资源错配, 审计署2023财政违规1.2万亿) + 政治合法性 (塔西佗陷阱) + 社会阶层固化 (仇官仇富, 周劼炫富事件).
- Earns: DISAGREE model → 先做腐败自身局限 + introduce 央地关系/法治缺失 as deeper structural factor → compare by 解决难度 (腐败是表象, 不受制约的权力是根源).
- Earns: distinguishing 运动式反腐 (治标) from 制度反腐 (治本) — this is the Tier-5 pivot.
- Loses: conflating 腐败 challenge with 反腐 strategy (they are different nodes — one is the problem, the other is the response).

**For 程度题 on 制度反腐:**
- Earns: specific institutional mechanisms named — 监察法 (2018), 国家监察委, 双规/留置 custody powers, 猎狐/天网 (overseas asset recovery) — and their mechanism explained (not just listed).
- Earns: genuine 平衡 — naming limits of 制度反腐: selective enforcement / 灯下黑 (those who enforce can be corrupt too), absence of independent judiciary, 既得利益者 resistance.
- Loses: treating 反腐 campaign = 习近平 power consolidation (this is a political science framing not a CSC framing; CSC tests governance outcomes, not CCP factional politics).

**For 是非题 on 央地关系:**
- Earns: naming the 财权/事权不匹配 as the analytical core — central government collects revenue, locals bear spending obligations (social services, infrastructure, land finance dependency).
- Earns: 土地财政 as the perverse incentive it created (local dependence on land sales → LGFV debt → systemic risk).
- Loses: Vague "conflict between central and local" without specifying the fiscal mechanism.

**For 少数民族 questions:**
- Earns: 三股势力 framework (分裂主义/极端主义/恐怖主义) as the stated rationale for governance policy — this is the CCP's own framing.
- Earns: distinguishing 官方立场 from 国际批评 without choosing one side (CSC rewards balanced analysis, not advocacy).
- Earns: cross-topic link to 外交 (international pressure, Uyghur Act, UN reports) — THIS is the 跨课题 gateway for 少数民族 questions.
- Loses: Using advocacy language ("genocide") in an analytical essay — framing should be analytical, not polemical.

### 3.2 Universal P2 governance traps (mark-loss summary)

| Error | Mark loss | Correct move |
|---|---|---|
| No 跨课题 | Cap at 18 | Link to 经济 (营商环境, 绩效合法性), 社会 (阶层固化, NGO), or 外交 (国际批评) |
| No genuine 平衡 | Cap at 18 | Genuine counter — not a token sentence |
| 述 > 论 (lists without mechanism) | Cap at 13–15 | Every data point needs a causal mechanism |
| Misreads 题型 | Wrong template throughout | Apply verb-rule check before writing |
| No 立场 in intro | Cap at Tier 2 | Declare stance in first paragraph |
| 意识形态 = 共产主义 | Marks for AO1 content lost | Use 中国特色社会主义 / 习近平新时代中国特色社会主义思想 |
| 运动式 = 有效反腐 | Factual error | 运动式 = 治标不治本; 制度反腐 = 治本 |

### 3.3 P1 governance-specific scoring

**1(a) governance:** "Explain the characteristics of [governance challenge/policy] from Source A." Target: 2 characteristics each with a mechanism. E.g., 反腐: ① 大量官员被查处(量) + 机制: 监察委集中权力; ② 军队尤其被波及(质) + 机制: 军队过去游离于民政管制.

**1(c) governance:** Most common trap — evaluating whether the government's POLICY is right instead of evaluating the SOURCE'S 适用度. The question is about the source's explanatory power, not your view on the policy. Always commit to 大/小 verdict, never 中等.

**1(d) governance:** "To what extent do Sources C, D, E explain [government strategy]?" The meta-question is about source explanatory power. Evaluate each source's ①针对性 ②有效性, then reach a degree verdict.

**B组 governance:** The recommendation must come from a 资料 gap, not from own knowledge. Anti-corruption recommendation: cite the source that shows the limit of current enforcement → your recommendation addresses THAT specific limit (e.g., source shows 运动式 works short-term → recommend institutionalised 独立监察 mechanism).

## PART 4 — Trap Model (Governance-Specific, Ranked by Mark-Loss Severity)

> Source: consolidated guide Part 7, governance cases from 2024 specimen + 2025 promos + research `[SCHOOL]`/`[INFERRED]`.

| Rank | Trap Name | What students misunderstand | Wrong answer it produces | How to fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **跨课题遗漏** | Governance essays are self-contained political analysis | Writes 3 governance paragraphs, never mentions economy/society/foreign → **capped at 18** | Always end the causal chain: 腐败 → 营商环境 → 经济效率 (经济跨课题); 腐败 → 仇官仇富 → 阶层固化 (社会跨课题) |
| 2 | **意识形态混淆** | Thinks ideology = communism and is still the CCP's legitimacy base | "共产主义不再适用, 因此意识形态不是基础" — misses that the CURRENT ideology IS 中国特色社会主义 + 习近平思想 | Frame as: 当前意识形态 ≠ 传统马列; 两个确立 (2021) 和习近平思想是新形态; BUT performance is still the deeper base |
| 3 | **运动式 = 有效** | Sees the huge prosecution numbers as proof of success | "2025年983,000人受到处分, 因此反腐成效显著" — ignores 灯下黑 / 运动式治标不治本 | Split: short-term deterrence (治标) YES; structural root causes (不受制约的权力) remain → 制度反腐 is still incomplete |
| 4 | **民族政策单面分析** | Treats either the CCP framing OR Western critique as the full picture | Either "三股势力威胁严重, 政策完全正当" OR "政策是种族灭绝" — both are single-perspective, both lose marks | Use: 官方立场(兴边富民, 反三股势力) + 国际批评(UN reports, 强迫同化) + 分析两者的真实冲突 + 跨课题外交影响 |
| 5 | **央地关系: 泛泛冲突** | States "central and local conflict" without naming the fiscal mechanism | "上有政策下有对策, 影响治理" with no mechanism | 必须说明: 财权上收/事权下放 + 土地财政 + 地方债 (LGFV) as the specific outcomes |
| 6 | **法治 vs 司法独立混淆** | Conflates 依法治国 with Western judicial independence | "中国推进依法治国, 因此已接近西方法治标准" | 党主法治 (party leads law) ≠ 法律主治 (rule of law). 区分: 中国的法治 = 用法律治国 (instrument); 西方法治 = 法律约束权力 (constraint). Both framings should be analysed, not conflated. |
| 7 | **回应诉求过度乐观** | Takes 网络问政 and 12345 hotlines as evidence of genuine responsiveness | "网络问政使政府能充分回应人民诉求" — ignores the structural limits (no independent judiciary, 维稳 priority over 维权) | 双刃剑: 技术性回应 (投诉→修路) YES; 政治性回应 (维权→打压) NO. This tension IS the analysis. |
| 8 | **1(c) 适用度陷阱** | Evaluates whether the POLICY is good instead of whether the SOURCE explains it well | "资料C说明反腐成效显著, 我认同/不认同" → wrong question answered → **cap at Tier 2** | Always ask: "Can this source explain X?" NOT "Is the policy right?" |
| 9 | **建议照搬资料** | Copies a suggestion already in the source as the P1 B组 recommendation | "根据资料E, 中国应继续推进制度反腐" — this is a paraphrase, not a creative recommendation → rejected | Recommendation must transcend the source: cite the source's LIMITATION, then propose what the source doesn't say |
| 10 | **政治改革 = 西方民主** | Treats any call for political reform as a call for liberal democracy | "中国要改善治理必须实行多党民主" | CSC framing: governance reform = 完善现有体制 (improving within CCP system), never regime change. Radical framing → out of syllabus scope. |

## PART 6 — Cross-Topic Links (跨课题 Map for Governance)

> This is the section that gates **19+** in Paper 2. Every governance essay must cross at least one of these. `[OFFICIAL]` bands explicitly reward 涉及相关范畴. Causal chains below are how to make the cross-topic feel natural, not bolt-on.

### 6.1 Governance → 经济 (most-used linkage)

| Governance node | Causal chain to 经济 | How to phrase in essay |
|---|---|---|
| 腐败 | 腐败 → 资源错配 → 营商环境恶化 → 外资流失 → 经济可持续受损 | "腐败不仅动摇政治合法性，更通过扭曲市场信号，破坏公平竞争环境，阻碍经济的可持续发展（跨课题：经济）" |
| 央地关系 | 财权上收/事权下放 → 土地财政 → 地方债 / LGFV → 系统性金融风险 → 经济稳定 | "分税制下的央地财政失衡不仅削弱地方治理能力，更孕育系统性金融风险，对经济可持续构成结构性威胁" |
| 绩效合法性 | 经济增长 ↔ 党的合法性 → 经济放缓 = 合法性危机 | "中共的绩效合法性建立在经济增长之上，经济放缓与青年失业率上升正考验这一支柱" |
| 党国体制 | 党管国企 → 市场化改革阻力 → 国企效率低 → 经济转型难 | "党的全面领导在政治上强化稳定，却在经济层面制造政企不分的结构性矛盾" |
| 法治 | 司法不独立 → 产权保护弱 → 私人投资信心低 → 经济创新受抑 | "法治不健全制约产权保护，令私企在面对国企竞争时处于制度性劣势" |

### 6.2 Governance → 社会 (second-most-used)

| Governance node | Causal chain to 社会 | How to phrase |
|---|---|---|
| 腐败 | 腐败 → 公共资源被侵占 → 教育/医疗质量不均 → 阶层固化 → 社会不稳定 | "腐败通过固化利益集团的资源垄断，将社会阶层差距转化为制度性屏障" |
| 民族政策 | 强制同化 → 民族文化认同危机 → 族裔紧张 → 社会稳定隐患 | "民族政策与少数民族文化认同的张力，构成社会稳定的长期隐患" |
| 政治参与 | 官方参与渠道 → NGO管理 → 公民社会发展受限 → 社会压力无出口 → 非制度化运动风险 | "当制度化参与渠道收窄，社会诉求可能以更难以预测的形式涌现" |
| 回应诉求 | 回应能力 ↔ 社会稳定 / 公众信任 | "政府回应诉求的能力直接决定公众对体制的信任度与政治认同感" |

### 6.3 Governance → 外交 (often overlooked — highest marks potential)
