## Priority 3 — the MCQ he got wrong, and why

**B2-18. Genome size, gene number and biological complexity.** He picked the wrong option and was
surprised only 6% got it right.

Two things he needs:

1. **"Solely" is what makes the introns statement false.** Introns contribute to low gene density, but
   they are not the sole cause. A stem carrying an absolute word is usually testing the absolute word.
2. **Gene number does not determine physiological complexity either.** He accepted that one as true.
   Neither genome size nor gene number tracks complexity.

His own landing, which is right: *"genome size or number of genes cannot determine physiological
complexity"*, and the size-tracks-complexity claim *"works for the lower range but not the upper
range"* (it holds across the prokaryote-to-eukaryote span and breaks down among higher eukaryotes).
