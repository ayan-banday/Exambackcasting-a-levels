## Priority 3 — FRAGILE (produced correctly and confidently once; one more clean pass closes it)

Glycogen is osmotically silent but glucose is not, so hydrolysing without exporting drops the water
potential and lyses the cell (C4, strong). Cells burst above ~50 °C by two independent routes,
fluidity and pump denaturation (C4, strong). β-keratin stability broken down per structural level
(C4, strong). Fluidity and lateral movement as the keyword whenever a membrane deforms. Transmembrane
proteins include the carriers, which is why losing them costs most. Simple diffusion does not plateau
because it needs no protein. Osmotic effect ranking glucose > glycogen > fat.
