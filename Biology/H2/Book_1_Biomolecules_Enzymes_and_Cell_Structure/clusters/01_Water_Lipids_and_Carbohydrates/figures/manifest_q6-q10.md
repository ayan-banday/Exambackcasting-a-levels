## Notes

- Every image was verified by reading the rendered source page (or, for q8b, the embedded image plus
  the surrounding document text) before cropping. Nothing here is a substitute or a look-alike.
- `q8b.png` is the raw JPEG embedded in the collated Word document. In the original Word layout a
  floating text box adds a "lipid droplet" arrow-label over one of the white vacuoles; that overlay is
  a separate Word object and is not part of the embedded image, so it is absent here. The micrograph
  itself is unaltered. Ordering was verified against `word/_rels/document.xml.rels` — image1.jpeg is
  the first image in the document and image2.png (the labelled "Lipid droplet" schematic, Fig. 2.2) is
  the second, which pins image1.jpeg to Fig. 2.1.
- The ACJC 2020 prelim exists only as this Word collation; there is no 2020 folder in the prelims
  archive, so the docx is the best available source for q8b.
- Micrographs (q7a, q8d) were rendered at 300 dpi before cropping to keep the fine detail readable.
