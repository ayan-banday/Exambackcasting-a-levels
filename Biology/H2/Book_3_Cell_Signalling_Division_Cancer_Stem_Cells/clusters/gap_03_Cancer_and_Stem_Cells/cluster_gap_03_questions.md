**(a)** Any three.

**Growth factors recognise and bind to the specific binding site of the tyrosine kinase receptor (RTK) on the target cell surface membrane, activating the RTK.**

**The activated RTK binds to the KRAS protein and activates it.**

**(b)** Any one.

**Ionising radiation, for example X-rays or UV rays.**

**A chemical carcinogen, for example tar in cigarette smoke.**

**(c)** Marking points 1 to 7 are capped at 4 marks, so points 8 and 9 are not optional extras.

**Single base pair substitution in the KRAS proto-oncogene.**

**Thymine replaces cytosine, so CTA replaces CCA in the 12th triplet of the DNA.**

**Instead of GGU, GAU is coded for in the mRNA codon, so this is a missense mutation.**

**This is a gain-of-function mutation leading to a hyperactive KRAS protein.**

The middle three are the marks.

**Glutamic acid at the 6th position is substituted by valine. Glutamic acid is hydrophilic whereas valine is hydrophobic.**

**This results in polymerisation of the molecules into long fibres inside red blood cells.**

**The fibres of abnormal haemoglobin deform red blood cells into a sickle shape.**
