Question 1

Example
Find \( \displaystyle\int 4\cos^{2}(3x)\,dx \). [4]
Solution with mark scheme
Step 1
Apply identity \( \cos^{2}\theta = \tfrac{1+\cos 2\theta}{2} \). M1
\( 4\cos^{2}(3x) = 4\cdot\tfrac{1+\cos 6x}{2} = 2 + 2\cos 6x \).
Step 2
Integrate. M1 A1
\( \int (2 + 2\cos 6x)\,dx = 2x + \tfrac{1}{3}\sin 6x + C \).
Step 3
\( +C \) required. A1
\[ = 2x + \tfrac{1}{3}\sin 6x + C \]

Find \( \displaystyle\int (3\sin^{2}x + 2\sec^{2}x)\,dx \). [4]
Answer with mark scheme
Step 1
Identity on \( \sin^{2} \). Direct integration on \( \sec^{2}x \) (do NOT rewrite as \( 1/\cos^{2}x \) — it's the derivative of \( \tan x \)). M1
\( 3\sin^{2}x = \tfrac{3}{2} - \tfrac{3}{2}\cos 2x \).
Step 2
Integrate. M1 A1
\( \int (\tfrac{3}{2} - \tfrac{3}{2}\cos 2x + 2\sec^{2}x)\,dx = \tfrac{3}{2}x - \tfrac{3}{4}\sin 2x + 2\tan x + C \).
Step 3
Answer. A1
\[ = \tfrac{3}{2}x - \tfrac{3}{4}\sin 2x + 2\tan x + C \]

Trig Powers: Identity-First Reflex.

Any \( \sin^{2},\; \cos^{2},\; \tan^{2} \) needs identity rewrite first. Exception: \( \sec^{2}x \) integrates directly to \( \tan x \).

Simplify. A1
\[ = \tfrac{1}{20}\tan^{-1}\!\tfrac{5x}{4} + C \]

Question 3

Example
Use the substitution \( x = 3\sin\theta \) to find \( \displaystyle\int \sqrt{9 - x^{2}}\,dx \). [6]
Solution with mark scheme
Step 1
Differentiate substitution (x is subject). B1
\( \dfrac{dx}{d\theta} = 3\cos\theta \Rightarrow dx = 3\cos\theta\,d\theta \).
Step 2
Simplify radical using \( \sin^{2}+\cos^{2}=1 \). M1
\( \sqrt{9 - 9\sin^{2}\theta} = 3\cos\theta \).
Step 3
Substitute.
\( \int 3\cos\theta \cdot 3\cos\theta\,d\theta = 9\int\cos^{2}\theta\,d\theta \).
Step 4
Apply identity — DON'T try power rule. M1
\( 9\cos^{2}\theta = \tfrac{9}{2}(1 + \cos 2\theta) \).
Step 5
Integrate. M1
\( \tfrac{9}{2}\theta + \tfrac{9}{4}\sin 2\theta + C \).
Step 6
Back-substitute. \( \theta = \sin^{-1}\!\tfrac{x}{3} \). \( \sin 2\theta = 2\sin\theta\cos\theta = \tfrac{2x\sqrt{9-x^{2}}}{9} \). A1
\[ \int\sqrt{9-x^{2}}\,dx = \tfrac{9}{2}\sin^{-1}\!\tfrac{x}{3} + \tfrac{x}{2}\sqrt{9-x^{2}} + C \]
Solution hidden — click Reveal
Practice

Question 4

Example
Find \( \displaystyle\int x^{2}\ln x\,dx \). [5]
Solution with mark scheme
Step 1
LIATE: Log beats Algebraic. B1
\( u = \ln x,\; dv = x^{2}\,dx \). \( du = \tfrac{1}{x}\,dx,\; v = \tfrac{x^{3}}{3} \).
Step 2
Apply formula. M1
\( \int x^{2}\ln x\,dx = \tfrac{x^{3}}{3}\ln x - \int \tfrac{x^{3}}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{x}\,dx \).
Step 3
SIMPLIFY the remaining integrand FIRST. M1
\( \tfrac{x^{3}}{3}\cdot\tfrac{1}{x} = \tfrac{x^{2}}{3} \).
Step 4
Integrate. A1
\[ = \tfrac{x^{3}}{3}\ln x - \tfrac{x^{3}}{9} + C \]
Solution hidden — click Reveal
Practice
Find \( \displaystyle\int x\cos 2x\,dx \). [4]
Answer with mark scheme
Step 1
LIATE. B1
\( u = x,\; dv = \cos 2x\,dx \). \( du = dx,\; v = \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 2x \).
Step 2
Apply. M1
\( \int x\cos 2x\,dx = \tfrac{x}{2}\sin 2x - \int \tfrac{1}{2}\sin 2x\,dx \).
Step 3
Integrate. M1 A1
\[ = \tfrac{x}{2}\sin 2x + \tfrac{1}{4}\cos 2x + C \]
Answer hidden — click Reveal
Archetype revealed
IBP Execution Discipline.

Three habits: write formula \( \int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du \) at top; LIATE picks \( u \); SIMPLIFY \( v\,du \) algebraically BEFORE integrating.

Question 5

Example
Find \( \displaystyle\int x^{2}\,e^{x}\,dx \). [5]
Solution with mark scheme
Step 1
First IBP. \( u = x^{2},\; dv = e^{x}\,dx \). M1
\( \int x^{2}e^{x}\,dx = x^{2}e^{x} - \int 2x\,e^{x}\,dx \).
Step 2
Second IBP on \( \int 2x\,e^{x}\,dx \). M1
\( = 2x\,e^{x} - 2e^{x} \).
Step 3
Substitute back. M1
\( \int x^{2}e^{x}\,dx = x^{2}e^{x} - 2x\,e^{x} + 2e^{x} + C \).
Step 4
Factor. A1 A1
\[ = e^{x}(x^{2} - 2x + 2) + C \]
Solution hidden — click Reveal
Practice
Find \( \displaystyle\int (\ln x)^{2}\,dx \). [5]
Answer with mark scheme
Step 1
First IBP. \( u = (\ln x)^{2},\; dv = dx \). \( du = \tfrac{2\ln x}{x}\,dx,\; v = x \). M1
\( \int (\ln x)^{2}\,dx = x(\ln x)^{2} - \int 2\ln x\,dx \) (after simplifying).
Step 2
Second IBP on \( \int 2\ln x\,dx \). M1
\( \int 2\ln x\,dx = 2x\ln x - 2x \).
Step 3
Substitute back. M1 A1 A1
\[ \int (\ln x)^{2}\,dx = x(\ln x)^{2} - 2x\ln x + 2x + C \]

Repeated IBP.

Integrand like \( x^{2}e^{x},\; (\ln x)^{2} \) — one IBP leaves an integral that needs IBP again. Reduce the polynomial power / peel the log by one each time.

Question 6

Example
Find \( \displaystyle\int \dfrac{2x + 1}{x^{2} + x - 2}\,dx \). [5]
Solution with mark scheme
Step 1
Factor denominator.
\( x^{2}+x-2 = (x+2)(x-1) \). Two distinct factors → partial fractions.
Step 2
Decompose. M1
\( \dfrac{2x+1}{(x+2)(x-1)} = \dfrac{A}{x+2} + \dfrac{B}{x-1} \).
Step 3
Solve. M1
\( 2x+1 = A(x-1) + B(x+2) \). At \( x = 1 \): \( 3 = 3B \Rightarrow B = 1 \). At \( x = -2 \): \( -3 = -3A \Rightarrow A = 1 \).
Step 4
Integrate. M1
\( \int (\tfrac{1}{x+2} + \tfrac{1}{x-1})\,dx = \ln|x+2| + \ln|x-1| + C \).
Step 5
Combine. A1
\[ = \ln|(x+2)(x-1)| + C \]
Solution hidden — click Reveal
Practice
Find \( \displaystyle\int \dfrac{5x - 7}{(x-1)(x-2)}\,dx \). [4]
Answer with mark scheme
Step 1
Decompose. M1
\( \dfrac{5x-7}{(x-1)(x-2)} = \dfrac{A}{x-1} + \dfrac{B}{x-2} \).
Step 2
Solve. M1
At \( x = 1 \): \( -2 = -A \Rightarrow A = 2 \). At \( x = 2 \): \( 3 = B \).
Step 3
Integrate. M1 A1
\[ = 2\ln|x-1| + 3\ln|x-2| + C \]
Answer hidden — click Reveal
Archetype revealed
Partial Fractions: When to Use.

Trigger: rational function with denominator factorable into TWO OR MORE distinct factors. Single-factor denominator → use Block 28 direct integration instead.

Question 7

Example
The region bounded by the curve \( y = e^{2x} \), the y-axis, and the line \( y = e^{2} \) is rotated through \( 360^{\circ} \) about the y-axis. Find the exact volume of the solid formed. [7]
Solution with mark scheme
Step 1
Rearrange curve for x in terms of y. B1
\( y = e^{2x} \Rightarrow x = \tfrac{1}{2}\ln y \Rightarrow x^{2} = \tfrac{1}{4}(\ln y)^{2} \).
Step 2
Limits in y. Curve meets y-axis at \( y = 1 \); top at \( y = e^{2} \). B1
Step 3
Set up. M1
\( V = \pi\int_{1}^{e^{2}} \tfrac{1}{4}(\ln y)^{2}\,dy = \tfrac{\pi}{4}\int_{1}^{e^{2}}(\ln y)^{2}\,dy \).
Step 4
\( \int (\ln y)^{2}\,dy \) via IBP twice (Q5). M1
\( = y(\ln y)^{2} - 2y\ln y + 2y + C \).
Step 5
Evaluate. M1
At \( y = e^{2} \): \( 4e^{2} - 4e^{2} + 2e^{2} = 2e^{2} \). At \( y = 1 \): \( 0 - 0 + 2 = 2 \). Difference: \( 2e^{2} - 2 \).
Step 6
Multiply by \( \tfrac{\pi}{4} \). A1
\[ V = \tfrac{\pi}{2}(e^{2} - 1) \]
Solution hidden — click Reveal
Practice
The region bounded by \( y = \ln x \), the x-axis, and the line \( x = e \) is rotated through \( 360^{\circ} \) about the x-axis. Find the exact volume. [6]
Answer with mark scheme
Step 1
About x-axis: \( V = \pi\int y^{2}\,dx \). Limits: \( y = 0 \Rightarrow x = 1 \); up to \( e \). B1

Step 5
Multiply by \( \pi \). A1
\[ V = \pi(e - 2) \]

Question 8

Example

Solution with mark scheme
Concept
Three standard patterns:
TriggerDE
Rate \( \propto N \)\( dN/dt = \pm kN \)
Rate \( \propto (K - N) \) (chasing K)\( dN/dt = k(K - N) \)
Rate \( \propto N(K - N) \)\( dN/dt = kN(K-N) \) (logistic)
Sign: quantity INCREASING → positive; DECREASING → negative.
Step 1
Pattern from wording. "Rate \( \propto (T - 25) \)" → chasing K = 25. M1
Step 2
Sign. Cooling → \( T \) decreasing → negative. M1
Step 3
Write. A1
\[ \frac{dT}{dt} = -k(T - 25),\; k > 0,\; T(0) = 200 \]

Question 9

Example
Use the substitution \( y = ux \) to find the general solution of \( x\dfrac{dy}{dx} = y + x \). [6]
Solution with mark scheme
Concept
Why differentiate the substitution? The original DE contains \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} \) — to rewrite in terms of \( u \), you need to convert \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} \) to \( \dfrac{du}{dx} \). Differentiating the substitution provides that link.
Step 1
Differentiate substitution (product rule). M1
\( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = u + x\dfrac{du}{dx} \).
Step 2
Substitute into DE (with \( y = ux \)). M1
\( x(u + x\dfrac{du}{dx}) = ux + x \).
Step 3
Simplify — \( ux \) cancels. M1
\( x^{2}\dfrac{du}{dx} = x \Rightarrow \dfrac{du}{dx} = \tfrac{1}{x} \).
Step 4
Separate and integrate. M1
\( u = \ln|x| + C \).
Step 5
Back-substitute. M1
\( \dfrac{y}{x} = \ln|x| + C \).
Step 6
Solve for \( y \). A1
\[ y = x\ln|x| + Cx \]
Solution hidden — click Reveal
Practice
Use the substitution \( v = y - x \) to find the general solution of \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} = (y - x)^{2} + 1 \). [5]
Answer with mark scheme
Step 1
Differentiate. M1
\( \dfrac{dv}{dx} = \dfrac{dy}{dx} - 1 \Rightarrow \dfrac{dy}{dx} = \dfrac{dv}{dx} + 1 \).
Step 2
Substitute. M1
\( \dfrac{dv}{dx} + 1 = v^{2} + 1 \).
Step 3
Simplify and separate. M1
\( \dfrac{dv}{v^{2}} = dx \).
Step 4
Integrate. M1
\( -\dfrac{1}{v} = x + C \Rightarrow v = -\dfrac{1}{x + C} \).
Step 5
Back-substitute. A1
\[ y = x - \dfrac{1}{x + C} \]
Answer hidden — click Reveal
Archetype revealed
DE by Given Substitution: Full Mechanics.

Six steps: differentiate substitution → solve for \( \dfrac{dy}{dx} \) → substitute → simplify → integrate separable → back-substitute.
